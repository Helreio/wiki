**Vial of Old Blood** —
A viscous, dark crimson liquid offered only to the high ranking within the church. Sanctified by a Luminary in the names of the [[The Heavenly Hosts|Heavenly Hosts]]. Contains a droplet of Crimsair’s blood.
**Effect:** When ingested it heals for 8d8 + 8 hit points and cures 1 *[[Hit Points#Grievous Wounds|Grievous Wound]]*.

---
**Hot Ice** - 90gp
Is a white, bitter-tasting crystal powder. After ingesting it a flash of warmth flares out from your chest. 
**Effect:** You are empowered to act faster. You gain one extra Action Point during your turn in combat and double your *Movement* speed.
**Duration:** 1 minute.

---
**Midnight Oil** - 43gp
This concoction is used during times of great distress numbing any pain and fatigue. 
**Effect:** If you are *[[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]]* or *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]* it heals for one hit point and suppresses all *[[Hit Points#Grievous Wounds|Grievous Wounds]]* effects.
**Duration:** 10 minutes. 

---
**Barkskin Potion** - 25gp
Shavings of redwood bark float inside the vial, suspended in hard liquor. Your skin exudes resin that solidifies on contact with air. 
**Effect:** The target gains a flat **EC** of 18 if the EC is higher, then it grants a bonus of **+2 EC** for the duration. 
**Duration:** 10 minutes.

---
**Basilisk Potion** - 130gp
This liquid contains blood from a green basilisk.
**Effect:** After you drink it, you can unleash a cloud of poison as a breath weapon. Exhaling the breath uses one Action Point. The breath deals **3 + 2d6 poison damage** in a 3-meter cone, and each creature in the area must make DC 14 *Fortitude Saving Throw* or take half damage. After you use the breath weapon, you can't do so again for the current round.
**Duration:** 10 minutes.

---
**Moonglow Elixir** - 180gp	
A dubious elixir made by the [[Church of the Seventh Light|Church]], dulling the mind momentarily. Only those with sufficient mental fortitude can truly utilize this medicine. 
**Effect:** Drinking the elixir grants the user invisibility, and you have a -5 penalty to *Willpower Saving Throws* for the duration.
**Duration:** 1 minute.

--- 
**Lead Tincture** - 135gp
A fairly disgusting, syrupy mixture that combines alchemy and medicine. Granting users a greater fortitude against attacks at the cost of speed. 
**Effect:** Consuming the elixir grants **Resistance: Physical 10** and halves your *Movement* speed (rounded up) for the duration.
**Duration:** 1 minute.

---
**Black Candle** - 34gp
A strange candle that burns with dark blue flame. Light it during a *Short Rest* to gain one *[[Rest Mechanic#Recovery Dice|Recovery Die]]*. The candle burns out afterward. 
**Effect:** +1 *[[Rest Mechanic#Recovery Dice|Recovery Die]]* for all creatures within 3 meters of the candle.
**Duration:** 5 minutes.
