==Requirements:== *Arcana Mastery III (+6)* + *Calligrapher's Supplies Mastery II (+4)* + Sigil Formula 

**Sigil formula** - Contains the list of reagents and magical sigils required to scribe the enchant.

**Seidhr vellum** - Can be applied to an item, by touching it with one hand and reading the sigils. It takes around 1 minute.  

**Cost** is 30% profit + 25% reagents/ingredients + 45% labor

A +1 (+2, +3) weapon/armor can hold only one (two, three) enchant/s. 
An enchant can be removed or transferred by a skilled *Master Maker*. 
The cost associated for such a task is ~10% of the enchant cost.
Only one enchant of the same name, regardless of rank, can be active on the same item.
