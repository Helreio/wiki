==Specialization Talents can only be taken once.==
\[p] - passive, \[a] - active  

## Tier I - 20 Exp 

- **\[a] Sanctified by Smite I** - Choose one Smite from the *[[Smite Spell List|Smite Spells list]].*
	Smite applies after you hit with a weapon attack. 
	Can be used once per Short Rest. (1/SR)

---
- **\[a] Channel Divinity I** - Choose one *Channel Divinity* from the *[[Channel Divinity|Channel Divinity list]]*.
	Can be used once per Short Rest. (1/SR)

---
- **\[a] Divine Sense** (1/SR) - The presence of strong evil registers on your senses like a noxious odor, and powerful good rings like heavenly music in your ears. 
	For one Action Point, you can open your awareness to detect such forces. Until the end of your next turn, you know the location of any celestial, fiend, or undead within 12 meters of you that is not behind total cover.

---
- **\[p] Halr’od’s Ire** - Against you, *Critical Strikes* deal 25% of the damage back to the attacker as radiant or fire damage. This damage cannot exceed more than half your maximum health (rounded up).

---
- **\[a, p] Weapons of the Devout** - As a Strixbane you modify your chosen weapon to be more efficient during combat. See below the effect for your specific weapon.

>***The Penitent Lash (Chain Whip)***
You affix a ball at the end of the chain that house a small vial. The first attack that hits breaks the vial applying the effect. 
E.g. Alchemical Fire, Vial of Acid etc.

>***The Hound’s Tooth (Sawcleaver)***
*Jagged Parry (Reaction)* - When you use the *[[Actions#Parry (Reaction)|Parry]]* reaction, you can make an attack in retaliation with *Advantage* and transform the weapon.

>***Condemnation (L. Crossbow)***
You modify the release mechanism of the crossbow to be able to fire a solid-iron spike. The weapon has enough force to punch through their defenses ablating **3 Armor (Ar)** instead of 1.  
>
>At close range within 3 meters, if you use an iron spike, the attack cannot be *Blocked*.

## Tier II - 45 Exp

- **\[a] Sanctified by Smite II** - Choose one Smite from the *[[Smite Spell List|Smite Spells list]].*
	Smite applies after you hit with a weapon attack. 
	Can be used twice per Short Rest. (2/SR)

---
- **\[a] Channel Divinity II** - Choose one additional *Channel Divinity* from the *[[Channel Divinity|Channel Divinity list]]*.
	*Channel Divinity* has two uses but a Short Rest only restores one use.

---
- **\[p] Divine Knowledge** - You can choose two *[[Incantation Spell List|Incantations]]* from Tier I - III. You can cast it a number of times equal to your *Charisma Modifier (minimum of one)* per *Short Rest*. 
	One Incantation can be changed during a *Long Rest* or Vigil (1/day), in which you pray for 10 minutes. 

---
- **\[p] Het’nor’s Benediction** - You gain a bonus to your *Intelligence (Arcana and Lore: Religion) Checks*. The bonus equals your *Charisma Modifier (minimum of one)*.
	Your spell attack modifier and save DC gain a bonus equal to your *Inteligence Modifier (minimum of one)* .
	**Math:**
	**Spell save DC** *= 8 + Charisma Modifier + Intelligence Modifier + Mastery Rank* 
	**Spell attack modifier** *= Charisma Modifier + Intelligence Modifier + Mastery Rank*

---
- **\[p] Writ of Sanctity** - You gain two additional known *[[Writs of Absolution]].*

---
## Tier III - 80 Exp

- **\[a] Svar’bog’s Reckoning** - When you *Critically Strike* with a weapon you gain one additional Action Point for this turn.

---
- **\[a, p] Weapons of the Righteous** - You gain one use of *Channel Divinity*. You can use one Action Point to imbue your chosen weapon with the Svar’bog’s Radiance, sanctifying the Strixbane's weapon for one minute. See below the effect for your specific weapon.

>**The Penitent Lash (Chain Whip)**
You unlatch the chain from the hilt of the weapon. In one hand you wield; 
***The Lash (Chain Whip)*** 
*(finesse, light reach(3m), exotic) (1d6 bludgeoning)*
The Lash retains all its previous attack options.
From the cross guard holy flames erupt in the shape of a long sword.  
***The Penitent (Longsword)*** 
*(finesse, light, exotic) (2d8 radiant, 2d8 fire)*

>**The Hound’s Tooth (Sawcleaver)**
The Light becomes viscous and coats your weapon empowering its damage and abilities.
***Saw Mode (2d8 slashing, 2d6 radiant)***
>**Rend** (1 Action Point): After you hit and damage a creature, for one Action Point you rend their soul until the end of your next turn. At the start of their turn they take **2d6 radiant damage**. This effect stacks, each application adding **2d6 to the damage**.
 **Flick (Transform)** (1 Action Point): You swing out the blade with force. Switch the weapon immediately into Cleaver Mode and strike up to 3 enemies in front of you (that are Large or smaller), knocking them back 2 meters and dealing your ***Ability Modifier* radiant damage**.
***Cleaver Mode (two-handed) (2d10 slashing, 2d6 radiant)***
**Guillotine** (1/Turn) (1 Action Point) Make a weapon Attack roll as you bring the extended blade down in a vertical arc. This attack cannot be *Blocked* or *Parried*. As the Light splatters in a 2-meter line behind the initial target it deals **2d6 radiant damage** to any enemy within the area. This effect does not require a successful hit on the initial target.  

>**Condemnation (L. Crossbow)**
Your weapon gains the *[[Weapons Table#Multiattack|Multiatack (3)]]* property. 
Each Attack Action now fires three bolts of light for **1d6 radiant + *Ability Modifier* damage** each, consuming no ammo. Make a separate Attack roll for each bolt. 
If you have taken a *[[Actions#Take Aim (1 action point)|Take Aim]]* action previously, it applies to all three bolts.
