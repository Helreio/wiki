==Specialization Talents can only be taken once.==
\[p] - passive, \[a] - active

## Tier I - 20 Exp

- **\[p] Tactical Master I** - Increase your Tactical Dice pool by one and choose one additional maneuver.

---
- **\[a] Battle Charge** - Replaces *[[Actions#Charge (3 action points)|Charge Action]]*. You can move up to 3 times your *Movement* speed (must move at least 3 meters in a straight line toward an enemy). Enemies have *Disadvantage* on *Opportunity Attacks* against you while you charge. At the end of the charge you can do two attacks instead of one.

---
- **\[p] Revenge** - When you use your *[[Actions#Shield Block (Reaction)|Shield Block]]* reaction you instantly counterattack with your weapon. 

---
- **\[a] Execute** - When you hit a bloodied creature (¼ health or less) with an attack, you can attempt to end its misery. By spending tactical dice up to your *Ability Modifier* with that weapon and adding them to the weapon damage. These dice are not doubled on crits.

---
## Tier II - 45 Exp

- **\[p] Tactical Master II** - Increase your Tactical Dice pool by one and choose one additional maneuver.

---
- **\[p] Second Wind** - When you successfully kill a worthy foe, you regain hit points equal to *3 + one Recovery Die* from your *Recovery Dice Pool*. 

---
- **\[a] Reckless Attack** - Before you make the first attack on your turn, you can decide to attack recklessly. This gives you *Advantage* on weapon attack rolls until the start of your next turn, also attack rolls against you have *Advantage* until the start of your next turn.

---
- **\[p] Momentum** - Upon rolling a *Critical Strike* for an attack you gain one Tactical Die. This can exceed the Tactical Dice maximum until the end of your next turn.

---
- **\[p] Opportunist** - *1d6 extra weapon damage* against injured foes.

---
## Tier III - 80 Exp

- **\[p] Tactical Master III** - Increase your Tactical Dice pool by one and choose one additional maneuver. 
	Additionally, when you enter combat and with no Tactical Dice you regain one.

---
- **\[a] Aimed Shot** - When using the *[[Actions#Take Aim (1 action point)|Take Aim]]* action you gain the full benefit or 17+ critical range with one Action Point (instead or three Action Points). 
	If you move (willing or unwilling) you lose the benefit of *[[Actions#Take Aim (1 action point)|Take Aim]]* action.

---
- **\[p] Last Stand** (1/LR) - When you are reduced to 1 hit point or below, your maximum hit points increase by *25 + Strength and Agility Modifier* for 1 minute. After this duration, you lose the additional hit points gained.
	If this effect expires and you are brought below 0 hit points you fall *[[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]]* and need to make *[[Saving Throws#Death Rolls|Death Rolls]]*.
