==Specialization Talents can only be taken once.==
\[p] - passive, \[a] - active

## Tier I - 20 Exp

- **\[p] Str’úm I** - Your gauntlet can be inscribed with an additional spell for a total of four spells from *[[Ta’vriek Arcanum Spell List|Ta’vriek Arcanum Spell list]]* or another spell school you have Lodes for.

---
- **\[p] Lode Augmentation I** - You implant one lode of krytth into your spine. 
	You gain *4+1d6 max lytel* and *2d6 Aetheric Erosion*.

---
- **\[p] Lytel Mastery I** - You can empower a spell that you are currently sustaining after you cast it by using your *Reaction*. 

---
- **\[p] Tactical Insight** - Your keen ability to assess tactical situations allows you to act quickly in battle. You add your *Intelligence Modifier* to your *Initiative*.

---
- **\[p] Spellblade** - After you cast a spell, the lingering magic around you empowers your next melee attack. The attack deals one extra dice of damage. This damage is the same as the spell damage from the cast, if the spell has no damage type the dice is the same as the weapon damage (e.g. bludgeoning, piercing, slashing). 
	This effect lasts until the end of your next turn.

---
## Tier II - 45 Exp

- **\[p] Str’úm II** - Your gauntlet can be inscribed with an additional spell for a total of five spells from *[[Ta’vriek Arcanum Spell List|Ta’vriek Arcanum Spell list]]* or another spell school you have Lodes for.

---
- **\[p]Lytel Mastery II** - While Sustaining a spell effect you have *+2 to EC*.

---
- **\[a] Elemental Transmutation** (1/Turn) - When you cast a spell you can spend one additional lytel to change the spell's damage type to *Acid, Cold, Fire* or *Lightning*. Spells that impose status effects change to the appropriate status effect.
	Frostbite for cold, Shocked for lightning, On Fire! for fire.
	You can use this feature once per turn. 

---
- **\[p] Power Surge** - When you cast a damaging spell you can add your *Intelligence Modifier* to the damage rolls.

---
- **\[p] Lode Augmentation II** - You implant one lode of krytth into your spine. 
	You gain *4+1d6 max lytel* and *2d6 Aetheric Erosion.*  

---
## Tier III - 80 Exp

- **\[p] Str’úm Master** - The lytel field generated by your gauntlet grants *Physical Resistance* equal to your *Intelligence Modifier.* 

---
- **\[p] Elemental Affinity** - Choose one type of elemental damage: *Acid, Cold, Fire* or *Lightning*. You ignore any *Resistance* for that type of damage. 

---
- **\[a] Battle Flow** (1/SR) - For one Action Point you enter a flow state for 1 minute. Your spells cost one Action Point less (to a minimum of 1) for the duration. Additionally, you gain *Temporary Hit Points* at the start of your turn equal to your *Intelligence Modifier*.
	You can use this feature once per *Short Rest*.
