==Specialization Talents can only be taken once.==
\[p] - passive, \[a] - active

## Tier I - 20 Exp

- **\[p] Edge I** - Increased your Edge pool by one. 

---
- **\[p] Twisting Edge I** - When you hit a *Vital Spot* you do two additional weapon dice instead.

---
- **\[a] Ghostly Strike** (2 Edge) - When you hit a target with an Attack, you can expend two Edge and impose *Disadvantage* on all attacks made against you (by the target of this attack) until the start of your next turn.

---
- **\[p] Ruthless** - You generate one Edge on *Critical Strikes*, this can exceed the Edge pool maximum for the current turn.

---
- **\[p] Crippling Strikes** - When you successfully *Critically Strike* a creature, their movement is reduced by 3 meters until the end of their next turn.

---
- **\[p] Killing Spree** - Delivering a finishing blow to a worthy foe restores all your Edge.  

---
## Tier II - 45 Exp

- **\[p] Edge II** - Increased your Edge pool by one. 
	*Action Surge* increased the Edge restored by one. 

---
- **\[p] Twisting Edge II** - When you hit a *Vital Spot* you do three additional weapon dice instead.

---
- **\[a] Uncanny Dodge** - When an attacker that you can see hits you with an Attack Roll, you can use your *Reaction* to dodge the attack. Roll an *Acrobatics Check* contested by the Attack roll, if you succeed you take no damage thus avoiding the attack.

---
- **\[p] Skulker** - If you miss with a ranged weapon attack you remain undetected by your target if prior to the attack you were hidden. 

---
- **\[a] Mutilate** (3 Edge) - When you successfully Attack a creature with a melee weapon, you can expend three Edge to mutilate your target. Half the physical damage done by the attack is taken again at the start of their turn as *[[Damage Types#^823df2|bleeding]]* damage.

---
- **\[p] Opportunist** - 1d6 extra weapon damage against injured foes.

---
- **\[p] Subterfuge** - When rolling for *Initiative* you can choose to do a Stealth/Hide Check instead.

---
## Tier III - 80 Exp
  
- **\[p] Edge III** - Increased your Edge pool by one. 
	Action Surge increased the Edge restored by one. 

---
- **\[a] Aimed Shot** - When using the *[[Actions#Take Aim (1 action point)|Take Aim]]* action you gain the full benefit or 17+ critical range with one Action Point (instead or three Action Points). 
	If you move (willing or unwilling) you lose the benefit of *[[Actions#Take Aim (1 action point)|Take Aim]]* action.

---
- \[**a] Veil of Shadows** (3 Edge) (1/SR) - During your turn for one Action Point and three Edge, you manifest a cloak of shadows around you. Any current magical effect on you is dispelled, and it protects you against magical attacks or effects until the start of your next turn. 

---
- **\[a] Vanish** (3 Edge) (1/SR) - During your turn for one Action Point and three Edge you vanish from sight and gain the *[[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]]* condition for 1 minute. 
	Any weapon attack or Action breaks the effect.
