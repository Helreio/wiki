==Specialization Talents can only be taken once.==
\[p] - passive, \[a] - active  

## Tier I - 20 Exp 

- **\[a] Channel Divinity I** - Choose one *Channel Divinity* from the *[[Channel Divinity|Channel Divinity list]]*.
	Can be used once per Short Rest. (1/SR)

---
- **\[p] Blessed Healer** - The healing spells you cast on others heal you as well. When you cast an *[[Incantation Spell List|Incantation]]* that restores hit points to a creature other than you, you regain hit points equal to *3 + Faith* used for the cast of the Incantation.

---
- **\[a] Effrie’s Eyes** (1/SR) - For one Action Point you channel the power of death. Until the end of your next turn, you know the location of any undead within 12 meters of you that isn't behind total cover and that isn't protected from divination magic. 

---
- **\[a] Warding Flare** - When a creature that you can see within 6 meters of yourself makes an attack roll, you can take a *Reaction* to impose *Disadvantage* on the attack roll, causing light to flare before it hits or misses. 

---
## Tier II - 45 Exp

- **\[p] Renewed Faith** - Increase your Faith pool by *2d6 + Charisma Modifier.*

---
- **\[p] Bless their souls I** - Your spell save DC and spell Attack modifier is increased by +1 Mastery Rank.

---
- **\[a] Channel Divinity II** - Choose one additional *Channel Divinity* from the *[[Channel Divinity|Channel Divinity list]]*.
	Channel Divinity has two uses but a *Short Rest* only restores one use.

---
- **\[p] Divine Knowledge I** - Increases your prepared *Incantations* by two. 
	You can now prepare three *[[Incantation Spell List|Incantations]]* from Tier III - IV. 

---
- **\[p] Solidarity** - When you take the *[[Actions#Help (1 action point, reaction)|Help]]* action or *[[Guidance]]* blessing to aid an ally’s attack, you can make one weapon attack. You can use this feature equal to your *Charisma Modifier* (minimum of one) per *Long Rest*. 

---
## Tier III - 80 Exp

- **\[p] Divine Knowledge II** - Increases your prepared Incantations by two. 
	You can prepare two *[[Incantation Spell List|Incantations]]* from Tier V - VI. 

---
- **\[p] Bless their souls II** - Your *spell save DC* and *spell Attack modifier* is increased by +1 Mastery Rank.

---
- **\[a] Rak’ul’s Scale of Judgment** - You can expend a use of *Channel Divinity* as a free action. Your next *[[Incantation Spell List|Incantation]]* deals maximum damage (or healing) on this turn, but your subsequent damaging or healing Incantation deals minimum damage (or healing).

---
- **\[a] Salvation upon thy flesh** (R) - As a *Reaction* you weave strands of light around a creature and forcefully move it towards you, negating any damage it would have taken (attacks or AoE) at that moment. The creature is moved within 1 meter of you. The range for this ability is 12 meters.
	
	You can use this feature equal to your *Charisma Modifier (minimum of one)* per *Long Rest*.
