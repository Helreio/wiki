## Tier I - 10 Exp
 
- **\[p] Build Tough** (1 week)
You train your body to sustain more punishment. You gain an additional *3 + 1d6 hit points* and one additional Recovery Die. 
==Can be taken three times.==

---
- **\[p] Ability Score Improvement I** (1 week)
You train the corresponding stat by activities during a week. You gain *+1 to one Stat.* (It increases health only at character creation)
==Can be taken two times.==

---
- **\[p] Acolyte I** (1 week)
You can choose one *[[Blessings Spell List|Blessing]].*
==Can be taken two times.==

---
- **\[p] Weapon, Spellcasting & Skill Improvement I** (4 days - 1 week)
A new skill, tool or weapon needs to be trained during a week in witch you do the relevant activity. To increase past Mastery Rank I, it takes 4 days. A skill, tool or weapon can only be increased up to Mastery Rank II. 
You can also increse your Spellcasting Mastery with this talent up to Mastery Rank II. 
==Can be taken five times.== ^5d9698

- **\[p] Build Bulky I** (1 week) 
You train your endurance close to your limits. Your *[[Rest Mechanic#Recovery Dice|Recovery Dice]]* are a *d8* instead of *d6*. 
You can use your *Recovery Dice* outside of a *Short Rest*. For two Action Points, you can expend one or more *Recovery Dice*, roll the dice, and regain a number of Hit Points.
**Math:** *1d8 per Recovery Dice + Strength Modifier (min. of 1) + Agility Modifier (min. of 1).*

Choose one weapon you have atleast Mastery Rank I. Your becomes the extension of your body, you can add your *Mastery Rank* to the damage dealt with that weapon.

Resets at the start of your turn.

- **\[p] Attunement Slots II** (1 day)

==Can only be taken once.==

---

During the very first round of combat, you have an additional Action Point. A total of four Action Points during the first round.

You train your endurance beyond your limits. Your *[[Rest Mechanic#Recovery Dice|Recovery Dice]]* are a *d12* instead of *d8*.

## Tier V - 120 Exp
- **\[p] Martial Exploits** (1 week)
Choose one of the following.

**Pierce.** For Ranged weapons. If you hit, your projectile passes through a secondary target directly behind the first. It automatically takes half of the damage.

**Feint.** Finesse weapons allow you to make a single weapon attack as part of the *[[Actions#Disengage (1 action points)|Disengage]]* or *[[Actions#Defensive Stance (2 action points)|Defensive Stance]]* action. 

**Daze.** When you hit a creature with a bludgeoning weapon, you can force them once per turn to make a *Fortitude Saving Throw*, or it cannot take *reactions* until the start of your next turn.
*Save DC is 10 + your Strength Modifier.*

**Expose Weakness.** For Light or Finesse weapons, when you hit with an attack you temporarily reduce the target’s *Evasion Class (EC) by 4* until another ally successfully hits them. (one creature at a time)

**Graze.** Heavy or Two-handed slashing weapons miss a creature they still deal damage equal to your *Ability Modifier* with that weapon.

**Sap.** For Versatile weapons, if you hit with an attack you can impose *Disadvantage* on their next attack roll until the start of your next turn. (one creature at a time)

**Push.** If you hit a creature with a bludgeoning weapon, you can push the creature up to *2 meters* straight away from yourself if it is Large or smaller. (1/Turn)
==Can be taken multiple times.==

---
- **\[p] Arcane Strike** (1 week)
After casting a Spell that consumes resources, you can make a melee weapon attack as park of the same Attack Action. (1/Turn)
==Can only be taken once.==
