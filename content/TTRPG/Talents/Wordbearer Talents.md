==Specialization Talents can only be taken once.==
\[p] - passive, \[a] - active

## Tier I - 20 Exp 

- **\[p] Divine Favour I** - Increase your Divine Favour pool by one. 

---
- **\[a] Ethyr’s Spear** (2 Divine Favour) - For one Action Point and two *Divine Favour*, you summon a spear made of white lightning. Make a range Attack roll, if you hit a target it deals **2d8 + Charisma Modifier radiant damage**. 
	The spear has *Finesse, Thrown (6/12m)* property and Mastery Rank is the highest you have with your best weapon. 

---
- **\[a] Divine Wrath** (3 Divine Favour) - For one Action Point and three *Divine Favour* you draw upon the Halr’od Light and strike creatures of your choice around you (1 meter radius) dealing **2d6 + Charisma Modifier radiant damage** on a failed *Fortitude Saving Throw*, half on a success. 

---
- **\[p] Retribution** - Your *Smite & [[Smite Spell List]]* can be empowered with additional *Divine Favour* up to your *Charisma Modifier*. 
	One additional Smite Dice per additional Divine Favour used.

---
- **\[p] Bulwark** - When you use *[[Actions#Shield Block (Reaction)|Shield Block]]* reaction it generates one *Divine Favour*. 

---
## Tier II - 45 Exp

- **\[p] Divine Favour II** - Increase your Divine Favour pool by one. 

---
- **\[a] Channel Divinity I** - Choose one *Channel Divinity* from the *[[Channel Divinity|Channel Divinity list]]*.
	Can be used once per Short Rest. (1/SR) 

---
- **\[p] Judgment** - Your normal Smite dice increase from a *d6* to a *d8*. 

---
- **\[p] Divine Knowledge** - You can choose one *[[Incantation Spell List|Incantation]]* from Tier I - II. 
	You can cast it for free once per Short Rest. (1/SR) 

---
- **\[p] Righteous Indignation** - Upon entering combat you start with three *Divine Favour* to aid your fight against the heathens.

---
- **\[a] Divine Sense** (1/SR) - The presence of strong evil registers on your senses like a noxious odor, and powerful good rings like heavenly music in your ears. 
	For one Action Point, you can open your awareness to detect such forces. Until the end of your next turn, you know the location of any celestial, fiend, or undead within 12 meters of you that is not behind total cover. 

---
- **\[p] Bulwark II** - Any use of Shield Block generates Divine Favour, even Shield Block without using a Reaction.

---
## Tier III - 80 Exp

- **\[a] Channel Divinity II** - Choose one additional Channel Divinity from the *[[Channel Divinity|Channel Divinity list]]*.
	Channel Divinity has two uses but a *Short Rest* only restores one use.

---
- **\[p] Commendation of the Light** - Killing a worthy foe refunds any *Divine Favour* spent that round.  

---
- **\[p] Execution Sentence** (1/SR) - For two Action Points, the High Heavens split apart and cast divine judgment on your foes. 
	A sunbeam marks a creature within 6 meters of you. 
	
	When you stop sustaining the ability the area around the creature becomes *Consecrated Ground* (2 meter radius) for 1 minute. 
	
	When the ability triggers (not sustained anymore) all creatures in the *Consecrated Ground* must make a *Reflex Saving Throw* or take **4d6 radiant damage** and gain the *[[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]]* condition until the start of your next turn. On a success they take only half damage. 
	
	For each additional Action Point spent to sustain the effect the damage is increased by **2d6** and the *Saving Throw DC by 2*. 
	
	This ability can be used once per *Short Rest*.

---
- **\[p] Oaths**
	Choose one of the oaths below: 
	- ***Oath of Protection***
		- +1 EC 
		Shield of Faith is increased to *2d8* (from *1d8*).
		When casting *Shield of Faith*, the Light lashes out reflecting half of the damage absorbed (rounded up) to the attacker.
		
	- ***Oath of the Herald***
		+ +3 to *Initiative*
		While on *Consecrated Ground* you are immune to the *[[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]]* condition.
		Your **[[Warden#^df4271|Lay on Hands]]** now cleanses a creature from *[[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]]*, *[[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]]* and *[[Conditions#Deafened|Deafened]]* conditions at a cost of 7 hit points spent from the pool.
		
	- ***Oath of the Martyr***
		+ +18 hit points
		You touch a willing creature and during 1 minute you mark it with a sigil creating a *Bond*. 
		When the creature takes damage it only takes half (rounded up) while the other half is transferred to you directly to your hit points if you are within 12 meters of each other. 
		If you are healed, the healing is split, half for you and half for your Bond (rounded up). 
		A creature can only have one *Bond* at a time.
		
	- ***Oath of Zealotry***
		+ +3 *Movement*
		When you are under 35% health (minimum threshold of 20hp) at the start of your turn, you gain one additional Action Point this turn.
