Oozes are gelatinous creatures that rarely have a fixed shape. They are mostly found in [[The Veiled Weft]], dwelling in caves and ruins feeding on refuse, carrion, or creatures unlucky enough to get in their way. 
Black puddings and gelatinous cubes are among the most recognizable oozes.
