Wyrms are large creatures from the [[Second Age]] and wield tremendous power. Only a few survived [[þæt Heofon Gewinn]] led by [[Siggruth; The Herald of War]] at the end of the [[Second Age]].
They are highly intelligent and have innate magic from the [[Making of the World; IO The Allfather|Allfather]]. From their remains powerful magic items were created. 
E.g. [[Gift of the Great Wyrm]]
