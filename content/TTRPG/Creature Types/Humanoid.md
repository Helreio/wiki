Humanoids are the main peoples of [[Mareth'Sol; The Physical World|The Physical World]], both civilized and savage, including humans and a variety of other species. They have language and culture, few if any innate magical abilities, and a bipedal form. The most common humanoid races are [[Humans|humans]], [[Leshy (half-breads)|leshy]] and [[Yfelkynn|yfelkynn]].
