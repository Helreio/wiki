Aberrations are utterly alien beings. Many of them have innate magical abilities drawn from outside the planes rather than the mystical forces of the world.
Notable Aberration: [[Ashrak, the Fallen Sun]]

They cover a wide range of weird things that just don’t fit any other category: chaos beasts, floating eyes, tentacled horrors, and so on.
