Constructs are made, not born. Some are programmed by their creators to follow a simple set of instructions, while others are imbued with sentience and capable of independent thought. 
Golems are the iconic constructs. 

They are programmed to follow a preset routine for their entire existence, with little to no variation. While most functioning constructs will follow their original creator’s orders to the letter, sometimes those orders can be rather open-ended or undefined.
