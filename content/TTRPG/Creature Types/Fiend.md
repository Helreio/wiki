Fiends are creatures of wickedness that are native to [[The Abyss]]. A few are the servants of deities, but many more labor under the leadership of [[The Hollowed Lords; Rulers of the Abyss|The Hollowed Lords]]. Evil priests and mages sometimes summon fiends to the material world to do their bidding. If an evil [[Celestial]] is a rarity, a good fiend is almost inconceivable.
