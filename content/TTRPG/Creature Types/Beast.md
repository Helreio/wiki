Beasts are nonhumanoid creatures that are a natural part of the[[Mareth'Sol; The Physical World|Physical World]] ecology. Some of them have magical powers, but most are unintelligent and lack any society or language. Beasts include all varieties.

Most natural beasts use tactics based on their instincts. Big carnivorous mammals are a serious threat even when facing a group of characters.
