Undead are once-living creatures brought to a horrifying state of undeath through the practice of necromantic magic or some unholy curse. Common undead include walking corpses, such as ghouls and zombies, as well as bodiless spirits, such as ghosts and wraiths.
Notable Wraith: [[Kahn-Rokh the Conqueror]]

***Deathless*** are a subtype of undead that are between life and death that retains its will, most are created by the [[Ereš-ki-gal|Eres-ki-gal Trimverate of Liches]]: [[Vardis Merrow]], [[Sevrik Dorn]] and [[Othmar Grell]]. 

[[Ereš; The First of his Kind]] was the first ***lich***. He served under [[Siggruth; The Herald of War]] in the [[þæt Heofon Gewinn]] against [[The Hollowed Lords; Rulers of the Abyss|The Hollowed Lords]] and their forces.
