*Necromancy*
**Lode:** Thanium I
**Lytel:** 1 point
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 1 meter
**Components:** V, S, M (A piece of string and a bit of wood)
**Duration:** 1 hour

#### Description
You bind to your will an invisible, mindless, shapeless spirit that performs simple tasks at your command until the spell ends. 
The servant springs into existence in an unoccupied space on the ground within range. 
It has EC 10, 1 hit point, and a Strength of 2, and it can’t attack. If it drops to 0 hit points, the spell ends.
Once on each of your turns for one *Action Point*, you can mentally command the servant to move up to 3 meters and interact with an object. The servant can perform simple tasks that a human servant could do, such as fetching things, cleaning, mending, folding clothes, lighting fires, serving food, and pouring wine. 
Once you give the command, the servant performs the task to the best of its ability until it completes the task, then waits for your next command.
If you command the servant to perform a task that would move it more than 12 meters away from you, the spell ends.
