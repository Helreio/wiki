*Spellbinding*
**Lode:** Hexlith I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action points
**Range:** 10 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
A verse of powerful magic is woven around the caster and willing allies. Up to three willing creatures within range are chosen. Each target gains *5 Temporary Hit Points* and can immediately use their *Reaction* to move up to half their speed without provoking *Opportunity Attacks.*

**Empower.** Upon casting the spell you can spend 3 additional lytel and increase the **Temporary Hit Points** by 5, and they have full Movement with their *Reaction.*
