*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point, Reaction
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You extend your hand toward a charged metal object or creature, exerting control to either attract it towards you or repel it away. If the object exceeds your weight, you are moved instead. The movement is gradual and non-damaging.

**Object Movement:**
- Objects weighing under ⅓ your weight move up to 12 meters.
- Objects weighing between ⅓ and ⅔ your weight move up to 6 meters.
- Objects heavier than ⅔ of your weight move up to 2 meters.

**Fixed Object Interaction:** If an object is fixed in place or kept in place by another reason, you can move yourself instead. The distance in meters equals your Intelligence Modifier. 

**Creature Interaction:** A creature affected by the *[[Conditions#Shocked|Shocked]]* condition is considered charged. A Medium or smaller creature must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* or be moved 2 meters toward or away from you. If the creature is Large or larger, you are moved instead.

**Empower:** By expending an additional lytel, you can increase the distance of movement by an amount equal to your *Intelligence Modifier.*
