*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** 10 meters
**Components:** S
**Duration:** 10 minutes

#### Description
You imbue a metal object with a static electric charge, allowing you to pick up small metal items, hold them together, repel or attract each other. 
You can also create small sparks by rubbing your fingers together while charged.
