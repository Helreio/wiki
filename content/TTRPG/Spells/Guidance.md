*Divination*
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 min

#### Description
You extend your hand and point at a target in range. Your faith grants a brief insight into the target's defenses. The first attack roll until the end of the spell, has a *+5 to damage* against the target, provided that this spell hasn't ended.
