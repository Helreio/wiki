*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 4
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Self (3-meter radius)
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 hour

#### Description
You present the symbol of your faith in the air and chant a litany bolstering the courage of your allies. The next time a creature affected by this spell must make a *Saving Throw* against a spell or effect that would cause the *[[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]]* condition, it succeeds automatically. If the creature is already *[[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]]* it repeats the *Saving Throw* for that effect with *Advantage*.

Once a creature has received this benefit, the spell ends for that creature.
