*Transmutation*
**Divine Favour:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** free action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
After you hit with a weapon attack, the weapon gleams with radiance as you strike. The attack deals an extra **1d6 radiant damage** to the target, which becomes visible if it's *[[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]]*, and the target sheds dim light in a 1-meter radius and can't become *[[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]]* for 1 minute.
