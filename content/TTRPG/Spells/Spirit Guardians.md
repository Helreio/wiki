*Summoning*
**Faith:** 8 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self (3-meter radius)
**Components:** V, S, M (a holy symbol)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You call forth cherubs to protect you. They fly around you to a distance of 3 meters for the duration. Their spectral form appears angelic.

When you cast this spell, you can designate any number of creatures you can see to be unaffected by it. An affected creature's *Movement* is halved in the area, and when the creature enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must make a *Willpower Saving Throw*. On a failed save, the creature takes **3d8 + *Charisma Modifier* radiant damage**. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage.
