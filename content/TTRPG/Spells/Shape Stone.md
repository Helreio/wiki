*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (soft clay)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You touch a stone object of Medium size or smaller or a section of stone no more than 1 meter in any dimension and form it into any shape you like. 
For example, you could shape a large rock into a weapon, statue, or coffer, or you could make a small passage through a wall that is 1 meter thick. 

You could also shape a stone door or its frame to seal the door shut. The object you create can have up to two hinges and a latch, but finer mechanical detail isn't possible.
