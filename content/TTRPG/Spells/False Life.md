*Necromancy*
**Lode:** Thanium I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of alcohol)
**Duration:** 8 hours

#### Description
You draw upon the lingering essence of death surrounding you and gain *3d4 + Spellcasting Modifier* Temporary Hit Points that last for 8 hours.
