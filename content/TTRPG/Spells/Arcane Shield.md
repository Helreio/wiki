*Abjuration*
**Lode:** Helliumite I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** Reaction, upon taking damage
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 round

#### Description
A barrier of magical force protects you, Until the start of your next turn you gain **Resistance: Physical 5** and *10 Temporary Hit Points* (until a Short Rest).

**Empower.** Spending additional lytel when casting the spell, up to your *Intelligence Modifier*, increases the *Temporary Hit Points by 5* per lytel spent.
