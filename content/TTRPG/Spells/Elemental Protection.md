*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 actions
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
For the duration, the willing creature you touch has **Resistance: 5** against one damage type of your choice: *acid, cold, fire* or *lightning.* 

**Empower.** When you cast this spell using additional faith points, you can increase the benefit by 5 per every two faith points used. (to a maximum of 15 resistance for 6 faith points).
