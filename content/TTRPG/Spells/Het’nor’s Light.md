#### Description
You touch one object that is no larger than 2 meters in any dimension and specify any number of creatures you can see within 2 meters. Until the spell ends, the object sheds bright silvery light in a 4-meter radius and dim light for an additional 4 meter. This light is only visible to the creatures you specified during the initial casting of the spell; all other creatures perceive the area affected by the ghost lantern as they regularly would.

**Empower.** Additional faith points increase the duration by 30 minutes per point used.
