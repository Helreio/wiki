*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** Reaction
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You attempt to interfere with a creature in the process of casting a magical effect or spell. 

Make a contested *Spellcasting Check* against their *Spellcasting Check*. If successful the spell effect fizzles out, if not the spell is cast. 
In case of an ability, the creature must make a*Willpower Saving Throw* against your Spell Save DC.
- On a natural 20 the spell backfires and affects only the caster. 
- On a natural 1 you empower the spell effect. Double the damage of the spell/effect or *Disadvantage* on the *Saving Throw* if there is no damage component.
