*Devastation*
**Divine Favour:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** free action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
After you hit with a weapon attack, your weapon surges with pale light. Your attack deals an extra **2d6 cold damage**, and within a 3-meter cone behind the initial target must make a successful *Fortitude Saving Throw* or take **2d6 cold damage** as the rime from your weapon splashes beyond.
