*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** 3 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of dried carrot)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
You choose a creature within range and improve their sight. The creature can see in Dim Light as if it were Bright Light, and within Darkness as if it were Dim Light for a range of 10 meters.

**Empower.** You can cast it on multiple creatures by spending 2 additional lytel, up to your *Intelligence Modifier*, per creature.
