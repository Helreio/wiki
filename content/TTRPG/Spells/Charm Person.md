*Spellbinding*
**Lode:** Hexlith I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 30 minutes

#### Description
One Humanoid you can see within range makes a *Willpower Saving Throw.* It does so with *Advantage* if you or your allies are fighting it. On a failed save, the target has the *[[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]]* condition until the spell ends or until you or your allies damage it. The *[[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]]* creature is Friendly to you. When the spell ends, the target knows it was *[[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]]* by you.

**Empower.** You can target additional humanoids when you cast this spell for 2 additional lytel, up to your *Intelligence Modifier* per humanoid.
