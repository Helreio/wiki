*Abjuration*
**Lode:** Helliumite I
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You touch a willing creature and for the next elemental damage it takes it has **Resistance: Elemental 3** then the effect is consumed.
Can only affect one creature at a time.
