*Devastation*
**Faith:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 actions
**Range:** Self (1-meter radius
**Components:** V, M (a holy symbol)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You utter a divine word, and burning radiance erupts from you. Each creature of your choice within 1 meter that you can see, must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* or take **2d6 radiant damage**, half on a success.

**Empower.** The spell's damage increases by **1d6** for each additional faith point used (up to a maximum equal to your *Charisma Modifier*).
