*Necromancy*
**Lode:** Thanium I
**Lytel:** 6 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 8 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You send a bolt of ghastly black miasma towards a creature that you can see within range. The creature needs to make a *Fortitude Saving Throw*, or take **8d8 necrotic damage** on a failed save or half as much damage on a success. A Plant creature automatically fails the save.

Alternatively, target a nonmagical plant that isn't a creature, such as a tree or shrub. It doesn't make a save; it simply withers and dies.

**Empower.** For each 2 additional spent, up to your *Intelligence Modifier*, the damage increases by 1d8. And the creature's Hit Point maximum is reduced by half of the damage taken until a Long Rest.
