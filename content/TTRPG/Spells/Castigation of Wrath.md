*Spellbinding*
**Divine Favour:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** free action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
After you hit with a weapon attack, your weapon pierces the mind dealing an extra **1d6 psychic damage.** Additionally, if the target is a creature, it must make a *Willpower Saving Throw* or be *[[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]]* of you for 1 minute. At the end of its turn the creature can make a *Willpower Saving Throw* against your spell save DC to end the effect.
