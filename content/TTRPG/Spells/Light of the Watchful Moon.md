*Divination*
**Faith:** 6
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self (10 meters)
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
Regardless of the time of day or location of your battle, you command the watchful gaze of the moon to illuminate threats to your allies. Shafts of bright moonlight, each 1 meter wide, shine down from the sky (or from the ceiling if you are indoors), illuminating all threats within range, whether they're enemies seen or unseen, traps or hidden hazards. An enemy creature that makes a successful *Willpower Saving Throw* resists the effect and is not picked out by the moon's soft glow. Attacks against creatures that fail have *Advantage*.

The glow makes *[[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]]* creatures visible. Targets that move out of the spell's range are no longer illuminated. New threats are revealed as they enter the range or as you move.
