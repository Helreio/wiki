*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 8 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self (12-meter radius)
**Components:** V, S, M (a small prism)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minute

#### Description
A golden radiance spreads out from you, providing bright light up to 6 meters away, and dim light for an additional 6 meters. Any creatures or objects in the area of bright light that are *[[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]]* become visible while they are within 6 meters of you. Likewise, any magical disguises or illusions within 12 meters of you become feeble and transparent, their illusory nature obvious. These magical effects return to full potency if they move more than 12 meters away.

When a creature that has taken a different form—through spells such as *Polymorph* or shapechange, or from inborn abilities, enters the light, it must succeed on a *Willpower Saving Throw* or be forced to return to its original form. Likewise, a creature attempting to change shape within the light must succeed on a *Willpower Saving Throw* in order to do so.
