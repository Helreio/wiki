*Transmutation*
**Divine Favour:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** free action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
After you hit with a weapon attack, your weapon flares with white flame and the attack deals an extra **1d6 radiant damage** to the target. Immediately after the attack, your form is shrouded by holy flames, and you are able to travel up to 3 meters without provoking *Opportunity Attacks*.
