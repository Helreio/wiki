*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Antorium I & Sailumite I 
**Lytel:** 1 point
**Casting Time:** Reaction, upon taking fire damage
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (Str’úm)
**Duration:** 1 round

#### Description
You consume and inhale fire, making it less harmful to you. You gain **Resistance: Fire 5** until the start of your next turn. 
Until the end of your next turn, you can use the *Belch Smoke* action for one Action Point. 
The spell ends if you fall *[[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]]*, inhale, or exhale (this includes speaking).

**Belch Smoke (1 Action Point).** You exhale what remains of the smoke, ending the spell and creating a smoke cloud in a 1-meter area within 4 meters of you. All creatures within the smoke cloud are *heavily obscured* and *[[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]]*. The smoke lasts for 1 minute or until dispersed by a strong wind.

**Empower.** When casting the spell, you can spend additional lytel up to your *Intelligence Modifier* increases the **Resistance: Fire by 5** per lytel spent, and the smoke area increases in size to a 2x2 (3x3, 4x4 etc) meters square.
