*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** S
**Duration:** 5 minutes

#### Description
Drawing upon the moisture in the air you condense it in your palm, creating one piece of ice. 
You can also dissipate the heat of an object making it cold to the touch.
