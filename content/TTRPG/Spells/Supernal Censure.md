*Devastation*
**Divine Favour:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** free action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
After you hit with a weapon attack, your weapon shatters the ambient magic surrounding the creature, and the attack deals an extra **2d8 bludgeoning damage.** If the creature is sustaining on a spell must make a successful *Willpower Saving Throw* or that spell is broken.
