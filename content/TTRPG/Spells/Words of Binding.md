*Necromancy*
**Faith:** 10 points
**Casting Time:** 1 minute
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (blood of the creature & a ruby worth 500 gp)
**Duration:** 5 days

#### Description
You attempt to fix the severed connection between the body and the willing soul of a deceased creature that died in the last 10 min. You draw ancient sigils on the deceased creature to draw the soul back to the body. That creature returns to life with 1 hit point. This spell can't return to life a creature that has died of old age, nor can it restore any missing body parts. The soul does not actually inhabit the body, it merely puppeteers it, and it cannot feel pain or other sensations. The body will continue to take damage and decay at the rate of *-2 Strength Score* at dawn, and will eventually expire after 5 days if not resurrected by other means.
