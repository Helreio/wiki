*Illusion*
**Lode:** Veillum I
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** 2 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 minute

#### Description
You create a magical effect within range. You can have up to three of its effects active at a time.

- **Sensory Effect.** You create an instantaneous, harmless sensory effect, such as a shower of sparks, a puff of wind, faint musical notes, or an odd odor.

- **Minor Sensation.** You chill, warm, or flavor up to 10 cubic centimeters of nonliving material for 10 minutes.

- **Altered Eyes.** You alter the appearance of your eyes for 1 minute.

- **Booming Voice.** Your voice booms up to three times as loud as normal for 1 minute.

- **Fire Play.** You instantaneously light or snuff out a candle, a torch, or a small campfire.

- **Magic Mark.** You make a color, a small mark, or a symbol appear on an object or a surface for 10 minutes.

- **Tremors.** You cause harmless tremors in the ground for 1 minute.

- **Minor Mirage.** You create an illusory image that can fit in your hand. It lasts until the end of your next turn. 

- **Mage Hand.** A spectral, floating hand appears at a point you choose within range. The hand lasts for 1 min. The hand vanishes if it is ever more than 6 meters away from you or if you cast this effect again.
	For one Action Point you can move the hand up to 6 meters and can use the hand to manipulate objects and it can carry 3 kg. (it cannot attack)
