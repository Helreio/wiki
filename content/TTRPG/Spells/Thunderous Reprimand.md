*Devastation*
**Divine Favour:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** free action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
After you hit with a weapon attack, your weapon rings with thunder that is audible within 300 feet of you, and the attack deals an extra **1d8 bludgeoning damage** to the target. Additionally, if the target is a creature, it must succeed on a *Fortitude Saving Throw* or be pushed 2 meters away or knocked *[[Conditions#Prone|Prone]]*.
