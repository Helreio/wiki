*Divination*
**Lode:** Arumvel I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 3 action points
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
For the duration, you sense the presence of magical effects within 6 meters of yourself. If you sense such effects, you can distinguish the spell school of the magic.

The spell is blocked by 30 cm of stone, dirt, or wood; 2.5 cm of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
