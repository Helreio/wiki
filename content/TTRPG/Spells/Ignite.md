*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** 8 meters
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
When a creature or an object (eg. torch) is *[[Conditions#On Fire!|On Fire!]]* you can incite the flames by snapping your fingers dealing **2d6 fire damage** and extinguishing the flames. 

**Empower.** You can spend additional lytel up to your *Intelligence Modifier* to spread fire to additional targets (equal to the lytel spent) within 3 meters from the original target. They need to make a *Reflex Saving Throw* or take **2d6 fire damage** on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
