*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 6 meters, (2 meter cone)
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You create an icicle in your hand. As part of the spells you can make a melee or range attack against a target dealing **3d4 cold and 3d4 piercing damage.** On impact, it shatters in a 2-meter cone behind the target. Any creature in the area of effect should make a *Reflex Saving Throw* or take **3d4 cold and 3d4 piercing** damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a success.

**Empower.**  You can spend additional lytel up to your Intelligence Modifier to increase the initial cold and piercing damage by **2d4** each 2 additional lytel spent.
