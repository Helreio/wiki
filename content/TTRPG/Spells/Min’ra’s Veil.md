*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 8 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self (6-meter radius)
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
Life-preserving energy radiates from you in an aura with a 12-meter radius. Until the spell ends, the aura moves with you, centered on you. Each non-hostile creature in the aura (including you) has **Resistance: Necrotic 10**, and its hit point maximum can't be reduced. In addition, a non-hostile, living creature regains 1*d6 + Charisma Modifier* hit points when it starts its turn in the aura with 0 hit points.
