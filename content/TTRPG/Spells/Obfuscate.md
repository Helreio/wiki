*Abjuration*
**Lode:** Helliumite I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of gold dust)
**Duration:** 8 hours

#### Description
For the duration, you hide a target, object or place (no larger than 2 meters) that you touch from Divination magic (*[[Scrying]], [[Divine Sense]]*).
