*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
One creature or loose object of your choice that you can see within range rises vertically up to 3 meters and remains suspended there for the duration. The spell can levitate an object that weighs up to 10 times your *Intelligence Modifier* kilograms. An unwilling creature that succeeds on a *Fortitude Saving Throw* is unaffected.

The target can move only by pushing or pulling against a fixed object or surface within reach. You can change the target's altitude by up to *3 meters* on your turn. If you are the target, you can move up or down as part of your *Move Action*. Otherwise, you can use one Action Point to move the target, which must remain within the spell's range.

When the spell ends, the target floats gently to the ground.

**Empower.** You can spend additional lytel to increase the weight you can lift, +10 kg per one lytel spent.
