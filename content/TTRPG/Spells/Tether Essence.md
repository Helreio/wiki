*Necromancy*
**Lode:** Thanium I
**Lytel:** 5 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (a spool of silver wire worth at least 50 gp, which the spell consumes)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
Two creatures you can see within range must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw*, with *Disadvantage* if they are within 6 meters of each other. Either creature can willingly fail the save. If either save succeeds, the spell has no effect. If both saves fail, the creatures are magically linked for the duration, regardless of the distance between them. 
When damage is dealt to one of them, the same damage (cannot be mitigated) is dealt to the other one. 
When *Hit Points* are restored to one of them, the same number of *Hit Points* is restored to the other one. If either of the tethered creatures is reduced to 0 hit points, the spell ends on both. 
When the spell ends on one creature, it ends on both.
