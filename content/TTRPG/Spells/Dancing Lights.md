*Illusion*
**Lode:** Veillum I
**Lytel:** 1 point
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sooth)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You create up to four torch-size lights within range, making them appear as torches, lanterns, or glowing orbs that hover for the duration. Alternatively, you combine the four lights into one glowing Medium form of any color. Whichever form you choose, each light sheds Dim Light in a 2-meter radius.

As part of the *Sustain Action*, you can move the lights up to 12 meters to a space within range. A light must be within 4 meters of another light created by this spell, and a light vanishes if it exceeds the spell's range.
