*Summoning*
**Faith:** 12 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self (1-meter)
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
A sword made of holy fire blazes to life by your side. The size and shape of the blade conforms to your will, but it is never larger than a two-handed weapon sized for a Medium creature.
 
Upon casting the spell or taking the *Sustain Action* you can command the blade to move & attack once during your turn. 
As a *Reaction*, if the blade is within 1 meter of you, you can interpose it between yourself and the attack parrying the strike. Half of the parried damage (rounded up) is taken by the attacker as **radiant damage** if the attacker is within 1 meter or you.

>**Whirling Flame Sword**
>*(sword)*
>*(2d6 fire + 2d6 radiant damage + Charisma Modifier)*
**Movement (Levitate):** 8 meters 
**To hit:** Your Spell attack modifier
>
>**Rihtwīs Līġ.** Double damage against *undead* and *fiends*.
**Bærn þæt flæsc.** On a *Critical Strike*, the target catches fire; the target takes **2d6 fire + 2d6 radiant damage** at the start of each of its turns until the spell ends, or the blade is unsummoned. 
**Parry:** Weapon dice + spell attack modifier. (Works on range attacks)
>
>The burning blade sheds bright light in a 2-meter radius and dim light for an additional 2 meters.

Any creature that does not have [[Piety]] 3 or more with the *Light of the Seventh* and attempts to hold the weapon, immediately takes damage from the sword, and needs to make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* or drop the weapon. 
If the sword is not dropped, at the start of their turn the wielder takes automatically the weapon damage. 
If you move the blade on your turn and its wielded by a creature, it needs to make a contested *Athletics Check* vs *your Spellcasting Check* to hold on to the weapon.
