*Transmutation*
**Faith:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 actions
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (a non magical weapon)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute 

#### Description
You call upon Halr’od's Light to imbues a weapon with a fragment of power. The weapon deals an additional **1d8 radiant damage** for 1 minute. 

**Empower.** When you cast this spell for each 2 additional faith points used, you can imbue one more weapon (up to a maximum equal to your *Charisma Modifier* number of weapons).
