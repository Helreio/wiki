*Illusion*
**Lode:** Veillum I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Self (3 meter cone)
**Components:** S, M (a shard of glass)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colorful light. Each creature in a 3-meter Cone originating from you must succeed on a *Fortitude Saving Throw* or take **2d4 fire damage** and have the *[[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]]* condition until the end of your next turn. On a successful save, they take **2d4 fire damage.**
