*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self (3-meter cone)
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You breathe forth a cone of acidic droplets. Every creature within range needs to make a *Reflex Saving Throw* or take **3d8 acid damage** on a failed save, on a success they take half as much damage.
