*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You invoke the power of the *Myrakai*, The Elemental Lords and can sustain it for up to one minute.
Choose one from the investitures below:

- **Flame Lord Artan.** You inhale and create fire within yourself gaining **Resistance: Cold 5** and you cannot gain the *[[Conditions#Frostbite|Frostbite]]* condition.

- **Tide Lord Nén.** A sheen of water covers your body, you change its state into a gel-like substance granting you **Resistance: Fire 5**, and you cannot gain the *[[Conditions#On Fire!|On Fire!]]* condition.  

- **Stone Lord Már.** Your skin becomes hard as stone, granting **Resistance: Physical 5** and *Advantage* on checks and saves to be forcefully moved. 
 
- **Sky Lord Hwé.** Wind swirls around your body (1-meter radius), range weapon attacks have *Disadvantage* against you. 
	It hedges out vapor, gas, and fog that can be dispersed by strong wind.
