*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self (3-meter cone)
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
A blast of cold air erupts from your hand. Each creature in a 3-meter cone must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw.* A creature takes **3d4 cold damage** on a failed save and gains the *[[Conditions#Frostbite|Frostbite]]* condition (until the end of your next turn) or half as much damage on a successful one.
A creature killed by this effect becomes a frozen statue until it thaws.

**Empower.** You can spend additional lytel up to your *Intelligence Modifier* to increase the *Spell Save DC by +1* for each additional lytel spent.
