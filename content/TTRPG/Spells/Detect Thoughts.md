*Divination*
**Lode:** Arumvel I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustain, up to 1 minute

#### Description
- **Sense Thoughts.** You sense the presence of thoughts within 6 meters of yourself that belong to creatures that know languages. You don't read the thoughts, but you know that a thinking creature is present.

- **Read Thoughts.** Target one creature you can see within 6 meters of yourself or one creature within 6 meters of yourself that you detected with the *Sense Thoughts* option. You learn what is most on the target's mind right now. If the target doesn't know any languages and isn't telepathic, you learn nothing.

For one Action Point on your next turn, you can try to probe deeper into the target's mind. If you probe deeper, the target makes a *Willpower Saving Throw.* On a failed save, you discern the target's reasoning, emotions, and something that looms large in its mind (such as a worry, love, or hate). On a successful save, the spell ends. Either way, the target knows that you are probing into its mind, and until you shift your attention away from the target's mind, the target can make for one Action Point on its turn an *Intelligence Check* against your *Spell Save DC*, ending the spell on a success.

The spell is blocked by 30 cm of stone, dirt, or wood; 2.5 cm of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
