*Necromancy*
**Lode:** Thanium I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
While channeling the chill of the grave you touch a creature within range. The creature must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw*. On a failed save, the target takes **2d10 necrotic damage**, and it can't regain Hit Points until the end of your next turn. On a success it takes only the damage.
