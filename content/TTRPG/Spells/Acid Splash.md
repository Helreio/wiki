*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** 10 meters
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You hurl a bubble of acid. Choose one creature within range, or choose two creatures within range that are within 1 meter of each other. A target must succeed on a *Reflex Saving Throw* or take **2d8 acid damage.**
