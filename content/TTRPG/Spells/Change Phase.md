*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of mercury)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You touch an object (solid, liquid, or gas) of a few centimeters big and change its state for 1 minute. After the effect ends the object returns to normal.

As part of the *Sustain Action* you can melt, freeze, vaporize, condense, sublimate or deposit the matter on each of your subsequent turns.

A living creature must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* with *Advantage* against your spell save DC to resist the effect.
