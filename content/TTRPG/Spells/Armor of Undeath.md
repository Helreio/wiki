*Necromancy*
**Lode:** Thanium I
**Lytel:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M
**Duration:** 2 hours

#### Description
You touch a skeleton or corpse, and a protective magical force of undeath surrounds you until the spell ends. 
The skeleton or corpse need not be complete, but enough must remain for an *[[Animate Dead]]* spell to work.

The remains spread around you, your base **EC** becomes *13 + Agility Modifier*. The spell ends if you equip any armor or if you dismiss the spell with one action point. 
The caster also gains *10 + Spellcasting Modifier Temporary Hit Points* that last until the spell ends.
