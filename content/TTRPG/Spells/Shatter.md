*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You unleash a wave of compressed lytel. Each creature in a 3-meter Cube originating from you makes a *Fortitude Saving Throw.* On a failed save, a creature takes **3d8 bludgeoning damage** and is pushed 3 meters away from you. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

In addition, unsecured objects that are entirely within the spell area are pushed 3 meters away from you.
A nonmagical object that isn't being worn or carried also takes the damage if it's in the spell's area.

**Empower.** You can spend additional lytel up to your *Intelligence Modifier* to increase the damage by **2d8** each every 2 additional lytel spent.
