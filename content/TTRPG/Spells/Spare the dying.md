*Necromancy*
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You call upon divine grace, extending the Light to a creature within range that has 0 Hit Points and isn't dead. The creature remains *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]* for 1d12 minutes but no longer falters *[[Conditions#On Death’s Door|On Death's Door]]*. After the time passes the creature regains 1 hit point.
