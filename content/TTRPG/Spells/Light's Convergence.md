*Transmutation*
**Faith:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 10 meters (self)
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
A radiant beam of light surges from your hand, the beam is 1 meter wide and 10 meters long. You can choose for each creature if they are healed or harmed.
On your turn you can move the beam as part of the *Sustain Action*.
The damage and healing only applies on the initial spell cast and when taking the *Sustain Action*.

- **Harm.** The creature must succeed on a *Reflex Saving Throw* or take **2d4 radiant damage + *Charisma Modifier***, halved on a success.

- **Heal.** The creature regains hit points equal to *2d4 + Charisma Modifier*.

**Empower.** The spell’s damage and healing increase by **1d4** for every two additional faith points used (up to a maximum equal to your *Charisma Modifier*).
