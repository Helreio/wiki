*Abjuration*
**Lode:** Helliumite I
**Lytel:** 2 points + the inscribed spell cost
**Casting Time:** 30 minutes
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (Seidhr parchment and ink)
**Duration:** 24 hours

#### Description
You inscribe a sigil that later unleashes a spell effect. You inscribe it either on a surface or an object. The sigil can cover an area no larger than 2 meters in diameter. When you inscribe the sigil, you set its trigger. The sigil is nearly imperceptible and requires a successful *Investigation or Arcana Check* against your Spell Save DC to notice.

Choose a spell that you know to be transcribed.
