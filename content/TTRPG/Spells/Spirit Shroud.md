*Necromancy*
**Lode:** Thanium I
**Lytel:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You call forth spirits of the dead, which flit around you for the spell's duration. The spirits are intangible and invulnerable.

Until the spell ends, any attack you make deals **2d8 extra damage** when you hit a creature within 2 meters of you. This damage is **radiant, necrotic,** or **cold** (your choice when you cast the spell).

In addition, any creature that starts its turn within 2 meters of you has its *Movement* reduced by 2 meters.
