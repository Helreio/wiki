*Transmutation*
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, M (Vial of holy water)
**Duration:** 5 minutes

#### Description
You touch one vial of holy water. Until the spell ends, it sheds bright light in a 3 meters radius and dim light for an additional 3 meters. The light can be colored as you like. Completely covering the object with something opaque blocks the light. The spell ends if you dismiss it with one Action Point.

If you target an object held or worn by a hostile creature, that creature must succeed on a *Reflex Saving Throw* to avoid the spell.

If cast upon a *[[Luminous Globe]]* the duration is extended by two hours.
