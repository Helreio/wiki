*Necromancy*
**Lode:** Thanium I
**Lytel:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You sacrifice some of your health to mend another creature's injuries. You take **3d8 necrotic damage**, which can't be reduced in any way, and one creature of your choice that you can see within range regains a number of hit points equal to twice the necrotic damage you take.
