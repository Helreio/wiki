*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 24 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (three thorns)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
The ground in a 4-meter radius sphere centered on a point within range sprouts hard spikes and thorns. The area becomes *Difficult Terrain* for the duration. When a creature moves into or within the area, it takes **2d4 piercing damage** for every 1 meter it travels.

The transformation of the ground is camouflaged to look natural. Any creature that can't see the area when the spell is cast must succeed on an *Intuition Check* against your spell save DC to recognize the terrain as hazardous before entering it.

**Empower.** You can spend additional lytel, up to your *Intelligence Modifier*, to increase the radius by 1 meter per additional lytel spent.
