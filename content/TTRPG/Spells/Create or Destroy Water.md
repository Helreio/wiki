*Transmutation*
**Faith:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of water if creating water or a few grains of sand if destroying it)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You either create or destroy water.

- **Create Water.** You create up to 5 liters of clean water within range in an open container. Alternatively, the water falls as rain in a 3-meter cube within range, extinguishing exposed flames in the area.

- **Destroy Water.** You destroy up to 5 liters of water in an open container within range. Alternatively, you destroy fog in a 6-meter cube within range.

When you cast this spell using additional faith points, you create or destroy 5 additional liters of water, or the size of the cube increases by 1 meter, for each additional faith point.
