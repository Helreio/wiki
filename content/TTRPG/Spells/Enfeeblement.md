*Spellbinding*
**Lode:** Hexlith I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action points
**Range:** 10 meters
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
You place a curse on a creature that you can see within range, the creature must make a *Willpower Saving Throw* or take **2d6 psychic damage** and become cursed. If it succeeds it only takes the damage. 
Until the curse ends, you deal an extra **1d6 psychic damage** to the target whenever you hit it with an attack roll. Also, choose one ability when you cast the spell. The target has *Disadvantage* on ability checks made with the chosen ability.

If the target drops to 0 Hit Points before this spell ends, you can use one Action Point on a later turn to curse a new creature.
