*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 6 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points 
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 hour

#### Description
Rak’ul’s will extends over one willing creature protecting its mind. The creature becomes immune to *psychic damage* and mind‑altering effects for the spell's duration.
