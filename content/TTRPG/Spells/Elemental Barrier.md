*Abjuration*
**Lode:** Helliumite I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** Reaction, upon taking elemental damage
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 round

#### Description
The spell shields the caster from the incoming energy, lessening its effect. You absorb **2d6 + Intelligence Modifier** of the triggering damage type.

Your next weapon attack or spell is imbued with the damage absorbed until the end of your next turn. 

**Empower.** For each additional lytel spent, up to you *Intelligence Modifier*, the damage absorption is increased by *1d6*.
