*Abjuration*
**Lode:** Helliumite I
**Lytel:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points, Reaction
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
Choose one of the effects below.

**Purge.** Choose one creature, object, or magical effect within range. Make an *Arcana Check* vs DC 10 + Lytel spent on the spell (or *Willpower Modifier* of the creature) to dispel any ongoing magical effect on.

**Spellbreak.** As a *Reaction* you attempt to interrupt a creature in the process of casting a spell effect. Make a contested *Spellcasting Check* against their *Spellcasting Check*. 
In case of an ability, the creature must make a*Willpower Saving Throw* against your Spell Save DC.
- On a natural 20 the spell backfires and affects only the caster. 
- On a natural 1 you empower the spell effect. Double the damage of the spell/effect or *Disadvantage* on the *Saving Throw* if there is no damage component.

**Empower.** You can spend an additional lytel, up to your *Intelligence Modifier*, to increase the Spell Save DC by +1 or gain a bonus of +1 to your Arcana Check/Spellcasting Check for each additional lytel spent.
