*Devastation*
**Divine Favour:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** free action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
After you hit with a weapon attack, your weapon flares with white-hot intensity, and the attack deals an extra **2d6 fire damage** to the target and causes the target to gain the *[[Conditions#On Fire!|On Fire!]]* condition for 1 minute.
