*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I & Sailumite I 
**Lytel:** 5 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (Str’úm)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
A beam of compressed fire blasts forward in an 18-meter-long, 1 meter wide Line from your hand in a direction you choose. Each creature in the Line must make a *Reflex Saving Throw*, taking **10d6 fire damage** on a failed save or half as much damage on a success.

**Empower.** You can increase the spell save DC by 1 for each additional lytel spent (up to your *Intelligence Modifier*).
