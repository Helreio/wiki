*Illusion*
**Divine Favour:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** free action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
After you hit with a weapon attack, your weapon pierces both body and mind, and the attack deals an extra **1d6 psychic damage** to the target. The target must make a *Willpower Saving Throw*. On a failed save, it has *Disadvantage* on attack rolls and ability checks, and can't take *reactions* until the end of its next turn.
