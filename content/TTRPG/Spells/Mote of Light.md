*Transmutation*
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You launch a mote of light at one creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes **1d4 radiant damage**, and until the start of your next turn, it emits *Dim Light* in a 2-meter radius.
