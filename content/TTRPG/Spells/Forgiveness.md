*Spellbinding*
**Faith:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You touch a troubled creature's soul and grant it forgiveness for its crimes. If the creature is *[[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]]* or *[[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]]*, it can use its *reaction* to repeat the *Saving Throw* for the effect, ending it on a success. If it succeeds, it gains *2d6 temporary* hit points.
