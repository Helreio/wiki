*Spellbinding*
**Lode:** Hexlith I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action points
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
A willing creature you touch is imbued with bravery. Until the spell ends, the creature is immune to the *[[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]]* condition and gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your spellcasting ability modifier at the start of each of its turns.
