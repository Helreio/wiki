*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a pair of gold rings worth at least 50 gp each, which you and the target must wear for the duration)
**Duration:** 1 hour

#### Description
This spell wards a willing creature you touch and creates a mystic connection between you and the target until the spell ends. While the target is within 12 meters of you, it gains a **+2 bonus to EC** and ***Saving Throws***. Also, each time it takes damage, you share the damage and each takes half of the amount (rounded up).

The spell ends if you drop to 0 hit points or if you and the target become separated by more than 12 meters. It also ends if the spell is cast again on either of the connected creatures. You can also dismiss the spell as one Action Point.
