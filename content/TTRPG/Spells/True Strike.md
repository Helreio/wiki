*Divination*
**Lode:** Arumvel I
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a weapon you have Mastery with)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You make one attack with the weapon used in the spell's casting. The attack uses your *Spell Attack Modifier* for the attack and damage rolls instead of *Strength* or *Agility*.
