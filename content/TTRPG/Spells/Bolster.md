*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (fur or a feather)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
You touch a creature and choose *Strength, Agility, Intelligence* or *Charisma*. For the duration, the target has *Advantage* on ability checks using the chosen ability.

**Empower.** By expending 2 additional lytel, up to your *Intelligence Modifier*, you can cast it on more creature.
