*Necromancy*
**Divine Favour:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** free action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
After you hit with a weapon attack, your weapon radiates with terrible energy, and you deal an extra **2d6 necrotic damage.**

If the target of that attack was a creature, it must make a successful *Fortitude Saving Throw* or take **2d6 necrotic damage** at the start of each of its turns for 1 minute, its flesh rotting around the wound. 
An affected creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. The rotting effect also ends if the affected creature receives healing.
