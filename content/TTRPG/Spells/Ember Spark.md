*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points, Reaction
**Range:** 6 meters, (2 meter radius sphere) 
**Components:** V, S, M (a pebble or tiny object)
**Duration:** 3 rounds

#### Description
You infuse flames into a tiny object transforming it into an ember. After three rounds it detonates. You can throw it as part of the spell casting. 
Activating it earlier by using a *Reaction* is possible but not earlier than the start of the next round. All creatures within 2 meters of the ember must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* or take **2d6 fire and 2d6 bludgeoning damage** on a failed save, or half as much on a success. 
Any creature who failed the *Saving Throw* is *[[Conditions#Deafened|Defended]]* for 1 minute. 

**Empower.** You can spend additional lytel up to your *Intelligence Modifier* to increase the area of effect by 1 meter per lytel spent.
