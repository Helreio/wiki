*Spellbinding*
**Faith:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** 18 meters
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You choose a creature you can see within range. A bead of black energy issues forth from your hand. Until the spell ends, each time damage is dealt to the target it must make a *Willpower Saving Throw.* On a failed save, it falls *[[Conditions#Prone|Prone]]* as its body is filled with torturous agony taking an additional **2d4 psychic damage**.
On the start of their turn they make a *Willpower Saving Throw* to break free of the effect.
