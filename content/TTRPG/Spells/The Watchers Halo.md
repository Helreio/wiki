*Transmutation*
**Faith:** 8 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self (8 meters)
**Components:** V, S 
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
A nimbus of golden light surrounds your head for the duration. The halo sheds *bright light* in a 4-meter radius and *dim light* for an additional 4 meters.

This spell grants you a pool of **8d8** for the duration. After you cast the spell, for one Action Point during the spell's duration or when you use the Sustain Action, you can expend dice from this pool to restore a number of hit points to one creature within 8 meters that you can see. 

Additionally, you have *Advantage* on *Charisma Checks* made against good aligned creatures within 6 meters.

**Empower.** When you cast this spell using additional faith points (up to your *Charisma Modifier*), the spell's pool of healing increases by *1d8* for each additional faith point used.
