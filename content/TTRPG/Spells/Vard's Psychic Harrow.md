*Spellbinding*
**Lode:** Hexlith I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 10 meters
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
One creature of your choice that you can see within range hears a discordant melody in its mind. The target makes a *Willpower Saving Throw*. On a failed save, it takes 3**d6 psychic damage** and must immediately use its *Reaction*, if available, to move as far away from you as it can, using the safest route. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage.

**Empower.** For each additional lytel spent, up to your *Intelligence Modifier* you can increase the damage by **1d6**.
