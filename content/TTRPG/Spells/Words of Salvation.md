*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 6 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 round

#### Description
You utter a protective holy word toward your allies. Choose three creatures that you can see within range. Until the end of your next turn, the allies you have chosen gain the benefit of **double the Armor (Ar).** 
If a target moves farther than 12 meters from you, the effect ends for that creature.
