*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (stone carved in the shape of a hand)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You choose a 1-meter unoccupied space on the ground that you can see within range. A hand emerges from compacted soil and stone and reaches for one creature you can see within 1 meter of it. The target must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw*. On a failed save, it takes **2d6 bludgeoning damage** and is *[[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]]* for the spell's duration.

As part of the *Sustain Action*, you can cause the hand to crush the restrained target, which must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw*. The target takes **2d6 bludgeoning damage** on a failed save, or half as much damage on a success.

For one Action Point, you can cause the hand to reach for a different creature or move to a different unoccupied space within range. The hand releases a restrained target if you do either. 
If you want to move the restrained creature, the creature must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* against your spell save DC to resist the force movement. On a fail you move the creature a number of meters equal to your *Spellcasting Modifier*.

The restrained creature can use one Action Point to make an *Athletics Check* against your spell save DC. On a success, the target escapes and is no longer restrained.
