*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I & Sailumite I 
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 8 meters
**Components:** S, M (Str’úm)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You gather lytel in the palm of your *Str’úm* and release a bead of compressed blue flames, it juts out towards a target you choose within range. Make a ranged Spell Attack, if the spell hits the target takes **3d8 bludgeoning + 4d6 fire damage** and needs to make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* or fall *[[Conditions#Prone|Prone]]*. On a successful save they take the damage only.
Regardless if they succeed or not they gain the *[[Conditions#On Fire!|On Fire!]]* condition.
