*Necromancy*
**Lode:** Thanium I
**Lytel:** 1 point
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You touch a willing creature and forcefully channel their latent life force to rapidly knit their wounds. The creature must immediately spend 1 or more of their unspent *[[Rest Mechanic#Recovery Dice|Recovery Dice]]*, up to a maximum number equal to your *Spellcasting Modifier*. 
For each die rolled they add your Spellcasting Modifier to the healing recieved.
**Math:** *Recovery Die + your Spellcasting Modifier*
