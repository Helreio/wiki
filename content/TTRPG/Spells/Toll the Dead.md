*Necromancy*
**Faith:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You point at one creature you can see within range, and the sound of a dolorous bell fills the air around it for a moment. The target must succeed on a *Willpower Saving Throw* or take **2d8 necrotic damage**. Half on a success.
If the target is missing any of its hit points, it instead takes **2d12 necrotic damage**.
