*Devastation*
**Faith:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 24 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 round

#### Description
A flash of light streaks toward a creature of your choice within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes **3d6 radiant damage**, and the next attack roll made against this target before the end of your next turn has *Advantage*, thanks to the mystical dim light glittering on the target until then.

**Empower.** When you cast this Invocation using additional faith points, the damage increases by **1d6** for each additional faith point used. (up to a maximum equal to your Charisma modifier).
