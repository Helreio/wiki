*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self (3-meter cone), Self (6-meter line)
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
As you hold your hand in front of you, flames shoot forth. 
- **Cone.** Each creature in a 3-meter cone must make a *Reflex Saving Throw*. A creature takes **3d6 fire damage** on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
- **Line.** Each creature in a 6-meter line must make a *Reflex Saving Throw*, or they take **3d6 fire damage** on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

The fire ignites any flammable objects in the area.

**Empower.** You can spend additional lytel up to your *Intelligence Modifier* to increase the damage by **1d6** per additional lytel spent.
