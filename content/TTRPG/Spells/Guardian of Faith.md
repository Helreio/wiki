*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You conjure a minor celestial manifestation to protect a creature you can see within range. A faintly glowing image resembling a human head and shoulders hovers within 1 meter of the target for the duration. The manifestation moves to interpose itself between the target and any incoming attacks, granting the target a **+2 bonus to EC**.
Also, the first time the target gets a failure on a *Reflex Saving Throw* while the spell is active, it can use its *Reaction* to reroll the save. The spell then ends.
