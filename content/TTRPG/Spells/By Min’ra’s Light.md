*Divination*
**Faith:** 6
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** Self (6-meter radius)
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minute

#### Description
The area within 6 meters of you becomes bathed in magical moonlight. In addition to providing dim light, it highlights objects and locations that are hidden or that hold a useful clue. Until the spell ends, all *Intelligence (Intuition)* and *Intelligence (Investigation) Checks* made in the area are made with *Advantage*.

*{Min’ra’s Light guides your path}*
