*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Antorium I & Sailumite I 
**Lytel:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point, Reaction
**Range:** Self
**Components:** S, M (Str’úm)
**Duration:** 1 minute

#### Description
The air around you shimmers with small flames. Any creature that hits you with a melee attack while within 1 meter of you takes **fire damage equal to your *Intelligence Modifier***.

While this spell is active, you can use a *Reaction* upon taking damage from a melee attack and spend one Lytel to wreathe the attacker in flames. They must make a *Reflex Saving Throw* and take **3d6 fire damage,** half on a successful save.

You shed bright light for 4 meters and dim light for an additional 4 meters.
