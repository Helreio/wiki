*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You instill deep cold into the body of a creature, damaging it and impairing joints and muscles. The target must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* or take **2d4 cold damage** and gain the *[[Conditions#Frostbite|Frostbite]]* condition (until the start of your next turn) on a failed save or take half as much damage on a success. 
Taking fire damage will end this condition.
