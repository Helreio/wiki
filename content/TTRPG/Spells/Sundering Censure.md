*Transmutation*
**Divine Favour:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** free action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
After you hit with a weapon attack, your weapon shimmers with burning light dealing an extra 2**d6 radiant damage.** The target must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* or suffer a penalty to **Armor (Ar)** equal to your *Charisma Modifier* until the start of your next turn.
