*School*
**Faith:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (a sprinkling of holy water)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You bless a number of creatures within range equal to your *Charisma Modifier*. Whenever a target makes an *Attack Roll* or a *Saving Throw* before the spell ends, the target can roll a *d4* and add the number rolled to the attack roll or *Saving Throw*.
