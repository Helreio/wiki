*Divination*
**Lode:** Arumvel I
**Lytel:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (a bit of bat fur)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 hour

#### Description
You create an *[[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]]* eye within range that hovers for the duration. You mentally receive visual information from the eye, which can see in every direction. It also has *[[Darkvision]]* with a range of 6 meters feet.

As part of the Sustain Action, you can move the eye up to 6 meters in any direction. A solid barrier blocks the eye's movement, but the eye can pass through an opening as small as 5 centimeters in diameter.
If the eye takes damage or moves further than 200 meters away from you, the spell immediately ends.
