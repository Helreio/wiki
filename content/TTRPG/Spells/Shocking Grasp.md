*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You channel lightning through your body to deliver a jolt to a creature within your reach. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes **1d10 lightning damage,** and gains the *[[Conditions#Shocked|Shocked]]* condition until the start of its next turn. 
You have *Advantage* on the spell attack roll if the target is wearing metal armor.

**Empower.** By expending 3 additional lytel you inflict also the *[[Conditions#Stunned|Stunned]]* condition on the target until the start of your next turn.
