*Illusion*
**Lode:** Veillum I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 16 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You create a twisting pattern of colors in a 6-meter Cube within range. The pattern appears for a moment and vanishes. Each creature in the area who can see the pattern must succeed on a *Willpower Saving Throw* or have the *[[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]]* condition for the duration. While charmed, the creature has the *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]* condition and a Speed of 0.

The spell ends for an affected creature if it takes any damage or if someone else uses one *Action Point* to shake the creature out of its stupor.
