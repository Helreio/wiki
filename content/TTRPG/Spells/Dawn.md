*Devastation*
**Faith:** 10 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (a suncatcher pendant worth at least 100 gp)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
The Light of Dawn shines down on a location you specify within range. Until the spell ends, a 4-meter radius, 8-meter high cylinder of bright light glimmers there. This light is sunlight.

When the cylinder appears, each creature in it must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw*, taking **5d10 radiant damage** on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. A creature must also make this *Saving Throw* whenever it ends its turn in the cylinder.

If you're within 12 meters of the cylinder, you can move it up to 12 meters with the *Sustain Action* on your turn.
