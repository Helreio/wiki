*Divination*
**Casting Time:** Reaction
**Range:** 6 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You call upon divine protection to aid a creature in need. When a creature within 6 meters of you is about to make a *Saving Throw*, you can use your Reaction to grant them providence. The target can roll a *d4* and add the number rolled to the *Saving Throw*.
