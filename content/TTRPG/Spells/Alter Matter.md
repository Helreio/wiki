*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of mercury)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You touch an object (solid or liquid) of 10 centimeters big and change its composition for 1 minute (E.g. iron to gold, or even a lode type to a different one for the purpose of gaining temporary access to a different school of magic). After the effect ends the object returns to normal.

**Empower.** When you spend additional lytel, up to your *Intelligence Modifier*, you can extend the duration to 10 min, 1 hour, 8 hours. Each extension costs 2 lytel.
