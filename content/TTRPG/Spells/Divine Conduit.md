*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 10 minutes
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (some hair, fingernail clippings, or other piece of the creature's body)
**Duration:** 24 hours

#### Description
You place your hand upon a willing creature and create a magical Bond between you and your target. For the next 24 hours, any incantation with a range of touch that you cast upon the target can be cast from up to 6 meters away. 
A creature can have only one *Bond* at a time.
