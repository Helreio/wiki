*Divination*
**Lode:** Arumvel I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 3 action points
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 hour

#### Description
For the duration, you see creatures and objects that have the *[[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]]* condition as if they were visible, and you have *Advantage* on *Investigation* & *Intuition Checks* to discern illusions.
