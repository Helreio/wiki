*Abjuration*
**Faith:** 4 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
You touch a creature and can end either one disease or one condition afflicting it. The condition can be *[[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]]*, *[[Conditions#Deafened|Deafened]]*, *[[Conditions#Stunned|Stunned]]*, or *[[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]]*.

For an additional 2 Faith points you can also end: 
- One effect that petrified the target.
- One curse, including the target's attunement to a cursed magic item.
- Any reduction to one of the target's ability scores.
- One effect reducing the target's hit point maximum.
