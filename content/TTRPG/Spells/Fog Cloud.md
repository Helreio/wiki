*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 2 action points
**Range:** 12 meters
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of water)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 30 minutes

#### Description
You create a 4-meter radius sphere of fog centered on a point within range. The sphere spreads around corners, and its area is heavily obscured. It lasts for the duration or until a wind of moderate or greater speed disperses it. 

**Empower.** You can increase the radius by 2 meters per additional lytel spent, up to your *Intelligence Modifier*.
