*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Self
**Components:** S
**Duration:** 10 minutes

#### Description
A small bead of flame appears on your finger or palm. It remains there for the duration. The flame sheds dim light for 1 meter. The spell ends if you dismiss it as a free action.
