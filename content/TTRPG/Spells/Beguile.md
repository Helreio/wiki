*Spellbinding*
**Lode:** Hexlith I
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** 2 meters
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

#### Description
You magically emanate a sense of friendship toward one creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a *Willpower Saving Throw* or have the *[[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]]* condition for the duration. The target succeeds automatically if you're fighting it, or if you have cast this spell on it within the past 24 hours.

The spell ends early if the target takes damage or if you make an attack roll, deal damage, or force anyone else to make a *Saving Throw*. When the spell ends, the target knows it was *[[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]]* by you.
