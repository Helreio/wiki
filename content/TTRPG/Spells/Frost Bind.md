*Devastation*
**Lode:** Antorium I
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** Reaction
**Range:** 10 meters
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

#### Description
As a reaction you can target a creature within range that has the *[[Conditions#Frostbite|Frostbite]]* condition in an attempt to restrain it. The creature needs to make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* or gain the *[[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]]* condition, until the start of your next turn, and take **3d4 cold damage** on a failed save or take half as much damage on a success. 
Taking fire damage will end this effect.
  
**Empower.** You can spend additional lytel up to your *Intelligence Modifier* to target one additional creature, within range, for every 2 additional lytel spent.
