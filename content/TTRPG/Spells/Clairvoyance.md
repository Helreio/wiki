*Divination*
**Faith:** 8 points
**Casting Time:** 10 minutes
**Range:** 7 kilometers
**Components:** V, S, M (a focus worth at least 100 gp, either a goat horn for hearing or a glass eye for seeing)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
You create an *[[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]]* orb within range in a location familiar to you (a place you have visited or seen before) or in an obvious location that is unfamiliar to you (such as behind a door, around a corner, or in a grove of trees). The orb remains in place for the duration, and it can't be attacked or otherwise interacted with.

When you cast the spell, you choose seeing or hearing. You can use the chosen sense through the sensor as if you were in its space. For 2 action points, you can switch between seeing and hearing.

A creature that can see the sensor (such as a creature benefiting from *See invisibility* spell, *Truesight* or another way of sensing it) sees a luminous, intangible orb about the size of your fist.
