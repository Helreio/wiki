*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Antorium I & Sailumite I 
**Lytel:** 2 points
**Casting Time:** 1 action point
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (Str’úm)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
A sphere of wind swirls around you in a 1-meter radius and moves with you, remaining centered on you. The wind lasts for the spell's duration.

The spell has following effects:

- It *[[Conditions#Deafened|Deafens]]* you and other creatures in its area.
- It extinguishes unprotected flames in its area that are torch-sized or smaller.
- It hedges out vapor, gas, and fog that can be dispersed by strong wind in the area of effect.
- The attack rolls of ranged weapon attacks have *Disadvantage* if the attacks pass in or out of the wind.
