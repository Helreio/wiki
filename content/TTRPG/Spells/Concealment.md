*Transmutation*
**Lode:** Sailumite I
**Lytel:** 3 points
**Casting Time:** 3 action points
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (ashes from burnt parchment)
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 10 minutes

#### Description
You radiate a concealing aura in a 6-meter radius for the duration. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose has a **+6 bonus to Stealth/Hide Checks** and leave no tracks.

**Empower.** You can spend additional lytel, up to your *Intelligence Modifier*, to increase the duration by 10 min per additional lytel spent.
