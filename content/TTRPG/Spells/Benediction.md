*Abjuration*
**Casting Time:** 1 action point 
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S 
**Duration:** Up to 1 minute

#### Description
You touch one willing creature. The target gains a **+2 bonus to EC** for the next attack made against it, then the spell ends. 
This effect can only be active on one target at a time.
