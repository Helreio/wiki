***Hit Points:** 15 + \[Strength Modifier (min. of 1) + Agility Modifier (min. of 1)] * d6’s*

==Restrictions:== 
Only Noble or Bastard background.
Only Leshy or Human lineage.

### Proficiencies: 
- **Armor:** none
- **Weapons:** Choose one simple weapon to gain Mastery I (+2).
- **Tools:** Mastery II (+4) with Calligraphy Tools
- **Saving Throws:** You gain Mastery I (+2) with Willpower Saving Throws.
- **Skills:** Choose three Mastery I (+2) from Arcana, Stealth/Hide, Investigation, Intuition, Insight, Medicine, Lore: Religion and Lore: Occultism.
- **Language:** [[Old Azakian]]

**Starting Gold:** 6d6 x 10gp (average 210 gp)

### Class Features:

**Lytel (Resource)**
Choose two implants from the *[[Lytel Lode Implants|Lytel Lodes list]]* granting you access to their specific schools of magic. You can cast spells on your turn using Lytel.
- Lytel equals to your **2x *Intelligence Modifier* + 2d6.**
- Choose a number of spells equal to ***5 + your Intelligence Modifier (min. of 1)*** from the chosen spell schools. 
- You regain your *lytel* at the end of a Long Rest. 
- You also gain ***2d6 Aetheric Erosion*** upon receiving the implants.
- Any additional lodes increase your **Aetheric Erosion (AE)** by **2d4** and **Lytel** by **1 + 1d6** per implant. 

**Cimmerian Conduit:** When taking a *Short Rest* you tap into the conduits and draw power. You can expend *[[Rest Mechanic#Recovery Dice|Recovery Dice]]* to restore *1 + 1d4 Lytel per recovery dice spent.*

**Spellcasting Ability:** *Intelligence* is your spellcasting ability for your thaumaturge spells. In addition, you use your *Intelligence Modifier* when setting the saving throw DC for a spell you cast and when making an attack roll with one. 
	**Spell save DC** *= 8 + Intelligence modifier + Mastery I (+2)*
	**Spell Attack modifier** *= Intelligence modifier + Mastery I (+2)*

**Spellcasting Focus**
You can use an Arcane focus as a spellcasting focus for your thaumaturge spells ignoring material components requirements without a gold cost mentioned. 

**Aetheric Erosion (AE)** 
When you cast a spell that requires spending more Lytel (spell points) than your current maximum capacity allows, you risk eroding your very soul. 
You take 1d4 *Aetheric Erosion* for every excess *Lytel* spent that you don't have to cast the spell.

## Ta’vriek Arcanum

In the third year at the academy you are awarded with the **Str’úm** glove. This serves as your *Arcane Focus* and requires a specific connection to your nervous system. This specialization focuses on medium range fire damage.

You must have the Glove on your hand to cast any spell from the *[[Ta’vriek Arcanum Spell List|Ta’vriek Arcanum Spell list]].* Choose three spells from the list, they do not count against your spells known. 
Instead of the Ta'vriek spells you can also choose spells from the *[[Devastation Spell List|Devastation]]* or *[[Transmutation Spell List|Transmutation]]* list.

>[!warning] Str’úm 
>*(finesse, light)* 
>*(glove) (Arcane Focus)*
>*(1d8 bludgeoning)*
>
>**Passive Effect**
>When you attack with the Glove, you can use your *Intelligence Modifier* instead of *Strength* or *Agility* for the attack and damage rolls. 
>While you are wearing no armor and not wielding a shield, your ***EC equals 10 + your Dexterity Modifier + your Intelligence Modifier.*** This represents a constant, low-level lytel field generated by your implants that hums and vibrates, causing constant minor headaches or nosebleeds. 
>The Glove is considered as an *Antorium Lode (Devastation)* & *Sailumite Lode (Transmutation)*. 
>You gain an additional ***1 + 1d6 lytel*** and a *+1 to Spell Attack Bonus* and *Spell Save DC*.
