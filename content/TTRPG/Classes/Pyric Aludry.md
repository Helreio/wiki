## Consumables
**Holy Paper.**  An alchemical creation that wreaths weapons in holy fire when rubbed along their surface. Rubbing this coarse paper along your weapon (or three pieces of ammunition) requires one Action Point, adding **1d6 radiant damage** to your attacks with that weapon for one minute.
This item can't be used with weapons with pre-existing elemental (enchanted) effects.

**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, a pinch of Quicklime, one vial Holy Oil, three drops of Vitriol
**Tools:** Calligrapher's Supplies
**Crafting Time:** 30 minutes

**Minor (DC 10):** 1d4 radiant damage
**Moderate (DC 13):** 1d6 radiant damage
**Masterwork (DC 17):** 2d6 radiant damage

**Blood Vial.** A crimson vial containing undeath. Ingesting the potion heals **2d8 hit points**. After 1 minute you lose the health gained.

**Reagents:** one empty Vial, two Blood Dregs, alcohol 10ml, three drops of Nightshade extract 
**Tools:** Alchemist's supplies
**Crafting Time:** 1 hour

**Minor (DC 10):** 1d6 hit points
**Moderate (DC 13):** 2d8 hit points
**Masterwork (DC 17):** 2d10 hit points

**Pack of Smokes.** Roughly the size of an egg. A piece of parchment is folded and packed with a volatile mixture of quicklime, dried grave-mold spores, and charcoal dust, all stabilized by a paste of bonemarrow ash.

For one Action Point, When slammed against a surface it triggers a rapid reaction, instantly unleashing a dense plume of ash-grey smoke in a 3x3 meter area.

**Reagents:** three Seidhr vellums, charcoal dust, five pinches of Bonemarrow ash, two pinches of Grave-Mold spores, two pinches of Quicklime
**Tools:** Alchemist's supplies
**Crafting Time:** 45 minutes

**Minor (DC 10):** 1x1 meter area
**Moderate (DC 13):** 3x3 meter area
**Masterwork (DC 17):** 5x5 meter area

**Vial of Pyre.** This volatile mixture is contained within a glass vial.
Upon impact the glass shatters, self-igniting itself burning with an intense, blue flame. It adheres to stone, wood, or flesh, refusing to be snuffed out by water or simple smothering. The mixture burns for 1 minute, dealing **1d6 fire and 1d6 radiant damage** on first contact and then at the start of the creature's turn. To extinguish the flames takes one full turn.

**Reagents:** one empty Vial, alcohol 10ml, Ox tallow 20g, 10 drops of Vitriol, a pinch of Quicklime, one Vial of Holy Oil
**Tools:** Alchemist's supplies
**Crafting Time:** 1 hour

**Minor (DC 10):** 1d4 fire and 1d4 radiant damage
**Moderate (DC 13):** 1d6 fire and 1d6 radiant damage
**Masterwork (DC 17):** 2d6 fire and 2d6 radiant damage

**Grave-moss Ointment.** This ointment temporarily masks the heartbeat and warmth, making it harder for undead to detect you with their *Sense Life* Trait for 10 minutes. 

**Reagents:** Ox tallow 30g, one Vial of Dead Water, five pinches of Grave-moss spores, two pinches of Bonemarrow ash, one Blood Dreg
**Tools:** Alchemist's supplies
**Crafting Time:** 20 minutes

**Minor (DC 10):** 5 minute duration
**Moderate (DC 13):** 30 minutes duration
**Masterwork (DC 15):** 1 hour duration

**Vile Blood.** This viscous goo-like mixture is death itself. If a creature ingests the concoction it takes **1d12 necrotic damage** and becomes *[[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]]* for up to a day unless it is targeted by a [Words of Restoration Incantation] or similar magic.

**Reagents:** Ox tallow 10g, one Vial of Dead Water, two Blood Dregs, one pinch of Bonemarrow ash
**Tools:** Alchemist's supplies
**Crafting Time:** 1 hour

**Minor (DC 13):** A creature must make a DC 10 *Fortitude Saving Throw*. On a failed save, it takes **1d6 necrotic damage** and is *[[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]]* for 6 hours. On a success, the creature takes half damage.
**Moderate (DC 15):** A creature must make a DC 12 *Fortitude Saving Throw*. On a failed save, it takes **1d12 necrotic damage** and is *[[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]]* for 12 hours. On a success, the creature takes half damage.
**Masterwork (DC 18):** A creature must make a DC 15 *Fortitude Saving Throw*. On a failed save, it takes **2d12 necrotic damage** and is *[[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]]* for 24 hours. On a success, the creature takes half damage.

## Reagents

- **Alcohol 90%proof**
- **Iron dust**
- **Seal Wax**
- **Silver shavings**
- **Ox Tallow**
- **Seidhr vellum:** You coat one parchment with tallow mixed with three drops of Vitriol.
- **Corpse Dust:** The desiccated flesh needs to be at least seven years old since the creature died.
- **Gallstones:** They need to be ground into a powder and mixed with alcohol. 
- **Vitriol:** Used to embed the ink into the parchment. 
- **Nightshade Extract:** Used as a solvent. 
- **Blood Dregs:** Coagulated blood taken from an undead or koshmera. 
- **Quicklime:** Heating crushed limestone to high temperature creates quicklime. Used to create heat and purification, highly reactive. 
- **Grave-Mold Spores:** Kept in airtight into a vial with alcohol. 
- **Bonemarrow** Ash: Ground from the bones of creatures. It acts as a binding agent,
- **Holy Water:** A member of the church may create holy water by performing a special ritual. The ritual takes 1 hour to perform, uses 25 gp worth of powdered silver and the *[[Incantation Spell List|Ceremony Incantation]]*.
- **Holy Oil:** A vial of oil used for anointing. It is made with the same process as Holy Water.
- **Living Water:** A vial of water containing morning dew taken from the courtyard (*Consecrated Ground*) of a temple belonging to the Light of the Seven.
- **Dead Water:** A vial of foul water filled from a spring within the Veiled Weft.
