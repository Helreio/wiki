### Banish the Wicked
For one Action Point, you present your holy symbol and speak a prayer censuring creatures hostile towards you. Each creature that can see or hear you within 6 meters of you must make a *Willpower Saving Throw*. If the creature fails its saving throw, it is *Turned* for 1 minute or until it takes any damage.

A *Turned* creature must spend its turns trying to move as far away from you as it can, and it can't willingly move to a space within 6 meters of you. It also can't take reactions. For its Action Points, it can use only Run or try to escape from an effect that prevents it from moving. If there's nowhere to move, the creature may take a *[[Actions#Defensive Stance (2 action points)|Defensive Stance]]*.

### Kalydra’s Will

For one Action Point you draw from the divine light of Kalydra. Wings of light emerge above your back empowering your attacks for one minute. Your weapon attacks deal an additional **2d8 radiant damage**. While channeling the light you remain standing even at 0 hit points for the duration of the channel. 
You still die if your *[[Hit Points#Grievous Wound|Grievous Wounds]]* reach 6 stacks.

For any instance of damage taken at 0 hit points you make a *Willpower Saving Throw* with the DC 10, if you fail you take one Grievous Wound. The saving throw DC increases **by 1** for each subsequent save one after the first. 

### Purifying Light

As a man of faith, you can call upon the power of your deity to emit a radiant aura that purges negative effects and heals allies. When you activate this ability for one Action Point, a bright light emanates from you in a 6 meter radius, washing over your allies and cleansing them of poisons and diseases, while also healing them for *1d8 + Charisma Modifier* and an additional *1d8* per ailment cured. 

### Min’ra’s Sanctuary

For one Action Point, you present your holy symbol to Min’ra, and moonlight emanates from you in a 6 meter radius. The light is centered on you, and is filled with dim light. The light moves with you, and it lasts for 1 minute or until you are *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]*. Whenever a creature (including you) ends its turn in the light, you can grant it *temporary hit points* equal to *1d6 + your Charisma Modifier.*

### Radiant Bulwark

For one Action Point, you channel divine energy into a creature that you can see within 6 meters of you (including yourself). The first time that creature is hit by an attack it gains **Resistance: Radiant & Necrotic 10** for 1 minute, and the attacker takes **radiant damage** equal to ***2d10 + your Charisma Modifier*.**

### Sworn Enmity

For one Action Point, you can utter a vow of enmity against a creature you can see within 6 meters of you, using your *Channel Divinity*. You gain *Advantage* on attack rolls against the creature for 1 minute or until it drops to 0 hit points or falls *[[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]]*.

### Light of Dawn

For one Action Point, you present your holy symbol, and any magical darkness within 6 meters of you is dispelled. Additionally, each hostile creature within 6 meters of you must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw*. A creature takes **radiant damage** equal to ***2d6 + your Charisma Modifier*** on a failed saving throw, and half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that has total cover from you is not affected. 
The light fades after 1 min.
