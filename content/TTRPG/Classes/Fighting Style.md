### Heightened Awareness:

You have *Blindsight* with a range of 2 meters. Within that range, you can effectively see anything that isn't behind total cover, even if you're blinded or in darkness. Moreover, you can sense an invisible creature within that range, unless the creature successfully hides from you.

### Shield Master (R):

When a creature you can see is attacked and is within 3 meters, you can use your *Reaction* to intervene between the attacker and the creature intercepting the attack and redirecting the damage to yourself. You move within 1 meter of the creature protected. 
==You must be wielding a shield.==

### Master of Heavy Arms:

When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an attack you make with a melee weapon that you are wielding with two hands, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll, even if the new roll is a 1 or a 2. The weapon must have *two-handed* or *versatile* property for you to gain this benefit.

### Merciless Marauder (R):

Whenever you hit a creature with an attack while you are holding a different weapon in each hand, you can use your *Reaction* to make another weapon attack.

### Sixth Sense: 

You gain a **+2 bonus** to *Initiative*. You can't be surprised while you are conscious. Other creatures don't gain *Advantage* on attack rolls against you as a result of using Stealth/Hide Action.

### Master of Polearms:

While you are wielding a halberd, pike, quarterstaff, or spear, other creatures provoke an *Opportunity Attack* from you when they enter the reach you have with that weapon.

### Sentinel (R):

When a creature within 1 meter of you makes an attack against a target other than you, you can use your Reaction to make a melee weapon attack against the attacking creature. Creatures within 1 meter of you provoke Opportunity Attacks from you even if they take the Disengage Action before leaving your reach.

### Standard Bearer (R):

When an ally within 1 meter of you makes an attack against a creature that you can see, you can grant them *Advantage* on their attack roll as a *Reaction*. You must be carrying a banner, flag, or standard in your hand to use this *Reaction*. You can equip your pike, halberd or a spear with a banner and your *[[Actions#Help (1 action point, reaction)|Help]]* action grants them **1d8** instead of **1d6**.

### Sharpshooter: 

Attacking at long range doesn't impose *Disadvantage* on your ranged weapon attack rolls. You deal an additional **+2** to **damage** made with a ranged weapon.

### Close Quarters Shooter:

When making a ranged weapon attack while you are within 1 meter of a hostile creature, you do not have *Disadvantage* on the attack roll. You may make *Opportunity Attacks* (within 1 meter of you) with ranged weapons as if they were melee weapons. 

*[[Actions#Charge (3 action points)|Charge]]* action allows you to make an Attack with a ranged weapon if the opponent is within 1 meter of you after you charge. 

### Unyielding Combatant:

Once per turn when you roll damage for a melee weapon attack, you can reroll the weapon’s damage dice and use either total.

### Strix Slayer: 

When a creature within 1 meter of you casts a spell with verbal or somatic components, you can use your *Reaction* to make a melee weapon attack against that creature. You also have *Advantage* on *Saving Throws* against spells cast by creatures within 1 meter of you.

### Durable:

Whenever you take the *[[Actions#Defensive Stance (2 action points)|Defensive Stance]]* action in combat, you can choose to spend one (or more) *[[Rest Mechanic#Recovery Dice|Recovery Dice]]* to heal yourself. 

### Unarmored Defence:

Your **Evasion Class** is equal to *10 + Agility Modifier + Strength Modifier* while not wearing any armor and not wielding a shield. 

### Mounted Combatant:

You are a dangerous foe to face while mounted. While you are mounted and aren't incapacitated, you gain the following benefits:
- You have *Advantage* on melee attack rolls against any unmounted creature that is smaller than your mount.   
- You can force an attack targeted at your mount to target you instead.
- If your mount is subjected to an effect that allows it to make *Reflex Saving Throw* to take half damage, it instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it fails.

### Brawler:

You've developed the skills necessary to hold your own in close-quarters brawling. You gain the following benefits:
- Your *Unarmed Strikes* now deal *1d6 + Strength or Agility Modifier* and are considered weapon attacks. 
- You have *Advantage* on attack rolls against a creature *Grappled* by you.
- When you hit a creature with an *Unarmed Strike* as part of the Attack action on your turn, you can use both the Damage and the Grapple, Restrain or Shove option. You can use this benefit only once per turn.
- You don't have to spend extra movement to move a creature Grappled by you if the creature is your size or smaller.

### Defensive Duelist:

When you're holding a Melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain **+2 bonus** to **damage** rolls with that weapon. And using the *[[Actions#Parry (Reaction)|Parry]]* reaction you can add your weapon Mastery Rank to the total amount parried. 
**Math:** *weapon damage dice + Ability Modifier + weapon Mastery Rank*
