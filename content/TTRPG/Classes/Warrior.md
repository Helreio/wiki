***Hit Points:** 15 + \[Strength Modifier (min. of 1) + Agility Modifier (min. of 1)] * d6’s*

### Proficiencies:
- **Armor:** light armor, medium armor, shields  
- **Weapons:** Choose four weapons to gain Mastery I (+2), either simple or martial. 
- **Saving Throws:** Choose one between Fortitude Saving Throws or Reflex Saving Throws to gain Mastery II (+4).
- **Skills:** Choose three skills to gain Mastery I (+2) from Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Lore, Medicine, Intuition, Intimidation, Investigation, Sleight of Hand, Stealth/Hide and Survival. 

**Starting gold:** 7d6 x 10gp (average 250gp)

### Class Features:

- **Action Surge 1/SR**
As a Free Action you can push yourself beyond your normal limits for a brief moment. On your turn, you gain two additional Action Points that can be used to attack, move, or an object interaction.

- **Fighting Style**
You adopt two particular styles of fighting as your specialty. Choose from the following *[[Fighting Style|Fighting Style List]].*

## Master at Arms
**Armor:** heavy armor
**Weapon Training:** Any weapon you wield gains a *+1 to hit* and *damage*.

### Tactical Dice (Resource) 
You have a pool of *four Tactical Dice* and you can use them on *Battle Maneuvers*, all maneuvers cost one tactical die if not specified otherwise. 
- You regain all of your expended tactical dice when you finish a Short Rest. These dice are **d8’s.** 
- *Action Surge* grants *two Tactical Dice*, and if it exceeds the limit, you gain them temporarily until the end of your turn.
- Your save *DC is 10 + Ability Modifier used by the weapon*. 

You can choose three Battle Maneuvers from the following: 
- **Shield Bash:** Using an Action Point you attack with your shield a creature within range, roll a D20 + Strength Modifier + Shield EC to hit. The attack deals *one tactical die + Shield EC + Strength Modifier* damage. After you hit the target they need to make a *Fortitude Saving Throw*, or they are *[[Conditions#Stunned|Stunned]]* until the start of your next turn.
- **Hamstring:** When you hit with an attack you can attempt to restrict the enemy's movement. You deal an extra *Tactical Die damage* and halve their movement (including Run & Charge) until the start of your next turn.
- **Trip Attack:** When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can expend one tactical die to attempt to knock the target down. You add the tactical die to the attack's damage roll, and if the target is Large or smaller, it must make a *Fortitude Saving Throw*. On a failed save, you knock the target *[[Conditions#Prone|Prone]].*
- **Riposte:** When you use the *[[Actions#Parry (Reaction)|Parry]]* reaction on a melee attack against you, within the same *reaction* you can spend one tactical die and make a melee weapon attack against the aggressor. If you hit, you add the tactical die to the attack's damage roll.
- **Rallying Cry:** You let out a loud battle cry for one Action Point, granting temporary hit points equal to *one Tactical Die + Charisma Modifier* to yourself and nearby allies who can hear you.
- **War Cry:** For one Action Point you let out a fierce battle cry, intimidating nearby enemies within 6 meters, if they fail a *Willpower Saving Throw* they become frightened for 1 minute They will repeat the save at the end of their turn. 
- **Challenging Shout:** For one Action Point you unleash a commanding shout, forcing one enemy within 6 meters to focus their attacks on you. They must succeed on a *Willpower Saving Throw*, or be drawn to you for 1 minute. 
For the duration, the enemy has *Disadvantage* on attack rolls against creatures other than you, and must make a *Willpower Saving Throw* each time it attempts to move more than 6 meters away from you; if it succeeds on this saving throw, it doesn’t restrict the target’s movement for that turn. The Condition ends if you end your turn more than 6 meters away from the target.
- **Raise Shield:** When you take the *[[Actions#Defensive Stance (2 action points)|Defensive Stance]]* action, you can expend a Tactical Die and gain one use of *[[Actions#Shield Block (Reaction)|Shield Block]]* reaction as a free action without using your *Reaction* until the end of your next turn. 
- **Shield Barrier:** Raising your shield for one Action Point, you create a defensive barrier, providing half cover (***+2 to EC*** and ***Reflex Saves***) and *one Tactical Die + Ability Modifier* temporary hit points to yourself until the start of your next turn. 
- **Commanding Strike:** When you make an attack on your turn, you can forgo one of your Action Points and direct one of your companions to strike. When you do so, choose a friendly creature who can see or hear you and expend one tactical die. That creature can immediately make one weapon attack, adding the Tactical Die to the attack's damage roll.
- **Reposition:** You can expend one tactical die and one Action Point to maneuver one of your comrades into a more advantageous position. That creature can move as a Free Action on this turn a number of meters equal to one Tactical Die without provoking *Opportunity Attacks*.
- **Precision Attack:** When you make a weapon attack roll, you can expend one Tactical Die and add it to your damage roll. This attack cannot be *Blocke*d or *Parried*.
- **Sweeping Strike (1/turn):** You attempt a sweeping attack with your melee weapon by expending one Action Point and a Tactical Die. You can target up to *three creatures* within 1 meter of you, they must make a *Reflex Saving Throw* or take *one Tactical Die + weapon damage dice + your Ability Modifier* damage. On a success they take half damage, if they fail they take the full amount.
- **Volley (1/turn):** You expend one Action Point and one tactical die to fire rapidly a few pieces of ammunition in an area of *3x3 meters* within the range of the weapon. The creatures within the targeted area must make a *Reflex Saving Throw* or take *one Tactical Die + weapon damage dice + your Ability Modifier* damage. 
On a success they take no damage and on a fail they take half damage. 
Ammo consumed is equal to how many targets are in the 3x3 area.

## Assassin

**Tools:** Choose one from Thieves' tools, Disguise kit, Forgery Kit and Poisoner’s Kit. You gain Mastery I (+2) with the chosen kit.
**Camouflage:** *Advantage* on Stealth/Hide Checks while in dim light or shadows. 

### Edge (Resource)

You have 2 Edge and can be used as a Free Action. You gain them back at the end of your turn. 
When you use *Action Surge* you also gain one additional Edge, this can exceed the limit until the end of your turn.  

Can be used on the following:

- **1 Edge:** During your turn you can use Stealth/Hide Action or Stand Action as a Free Action by spending one Edge.
- **1 Edge:** After attacking with a weapon you can reposition yourself up to half your *Movement* (rounded up).
- **1 Edge:** When you have *Advantage* on attack rolls or a conscious ally within 1 meter of your target, you can spend one Edge to find the weak spot within their defenses, ignoring any *Armor (Ar)* for that attack.
- **1 Edge:** After hitting with a weapon Attack, you can spend one Edge to find a *Vital Spot*, dealing one additional weapon damage die.
