==Requirement:== Calligrapher's Supplies + [[Pyric Aludry#Reagents|Reagents]]
**Spell save DC** is the makers spell save DC. 
**Math:** *8 + Charisma Modifier + Mastery Rank.*

A creature can have a number of *Writs* affixed to their soul equal to their *Charisma Modifier*.
  
  
## Writs

- **Rána’s Warm Embrace**
**Casting Time:** Free Action
**Range:** Self
**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, Seal Wax, one vial Living Water
**Duration:** Instantaneous  

The creature deliberately sacrifices ¼ of their current health. In exchange, they immediately suppress all *[[Hit Points#Grievous Wounds|Grievous Wounds]]* and status effects/conditions (except *[[Conditions#Prone|Prone]]*) for 1 minute. 

Any new *Grievous Wounds* or status effects/conditions are not affected by this Writ.

---
- **Het’nor’s Decree**
**Casting Time:** 1 Action Point, Reaction
**Range:** Self (6 meters)
**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, Seal Wax, a pinch of Iron powder, a pinch of Salt, a pinch of Silver shavings
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You attempt to dispel or suppress a localized spell, magical barrier, or magical trap. 
Make a *Spellcasting Check* against the spell save DC. 

In case of a successful suppression the duration is equal to your *Arcana Check * minutes* and it requires a *[[Actions#^845704|Sustain]]* action.   

---
- **Rak’ul’s All-Seeing Eye**
**Casting Time:** 1 Action Point
**Range:** Self
**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, Seal Wax, a drop of Nightshade Extract, a vial of Holy Water, a pinch of Corpse dust 
**Duration:** 10 minutes

The caster gains supernatural sight. They can see through mundane illusions, detect active magic as a glowing aura, and spot invisible spirits or creatures if they are within 6 meters. 
The seal burns away after 10 minutes.

---
- **Savr’ya’s Binding Chains**
**Casting Time:** Reaction
**Range:** Self (2 meters)
**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, Seal Wax, a pinch of Iron powder, a vial of Holy Water 
**Duration:** Instantaneous

When a creature gets within 3 meters, you can spend a *Reaction*. The creature is completely rooted by ethereal chains to the ground (0 *Movement*) until the end of its next turn. Monstrosities (koshmeras), fiends and undead take **2d6 radiant damage** when bound by the *Writ*.

---
- **Isil’s Mark of Purity**
**Casting Time:** Reaction
**Range:** Self
**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, Seal Wax, a vial of Living Water, a vial of Holy Oil, three drops of Nightshade Extract
**Duration:** Instantaneous

If you fail a *Saving Throw*, you can choose to succeed instead. 
The effect activates automatically if the failure state of the effect would have mind-altering effects, make you unconscious or otherwise take *[[Stats & Skills#Aetheric Erosion (AE)|Aetheric Erosion]]*. 

A creature can only have one *Isil’s Mark of Purity* affixed to their soul.

---
- **Oort’s Cloud**
**Casting Time:** Reaction
**Range:** Self
**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, Seal Wax, a pinch of Iron powder, a pinch of Salt, three drops of Vitriol, a pinch of Quicklime
**Duration:** 5 rounds

As a *Reaction* the Writ burns into a small 1 meter cloud around the caster. Ranged attacks (arrows, spells) passing through the smoke have a **-5 penalty to hit**. 
Any creature (including yourself) attempting to cast a spell inside the smoke must pass a *DC 15 Fortitude Saving Throw* or choke, losing their Action Point/s spent casting the spell. 

The cloud follows the caster and after 5 rounds the ash-gray smoke dissipates.

---
- **Rak’ul’s Sanctions** 
**Casting Time:** 1 Action Point
**Range:** Self (1 meter)
**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, Seal Wax, one vial of Holy Water, one vial of Holy Oil, one drop of Nightshade Extract
**Duration:** Instantaneous

For one Action Point you brand a target within 1 meter of you with this sanctified parchment. The creature must make a *Willpower Saving Throw*. On a failed save the creature cannot use any shape-shifting or stealth until the end of their next turn. 

Allies of the caster gain a **+5 to Hit** when attacking the branded target, as the mark glows faintly through clothing and armor. 

---
- **Blood for Svar’bog**
**Casting Time:** Reaction
**Range:** Self
**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, Seal Wax, a pinch of Bonemarrow ash, one vial of Holy Oil
**Duration:** Instantaneous

As a *Reaction* right after the caster takes damage from an enemy. The caster’s next melee attack deals bonus radiant or necrotic damage equal to the exact amount of damage they just received. 

---
- **Radiance of the Dawn**
**Casting Time:** 1 Action Point
**Range:** Self (1 meter)
**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, Seal Wax, five drops of Vitriol, five drops of Nightshade Extract, a pinch of Silver shavings, one vial of Holy Water
**Duration:** Instantaneous

All enemies in front of the caster (1 meter) are instantly *[[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]]* until the end of their next turn. 

Undead, fiends and creatures of darkness take **2d6 radiant damage** and are knocked *[[Conditions#Prone|Prone]]* by the Light.

---
- **The Old Crone’s Bargain**
**Casting Time:** Free Action
**Range:** Self
**Reagents:** one Seidhr vellum, Seal Wax, a drop of blood from the creature, a pinch of Bonemarrow ash
**Duration:** Instantaneous

When this Writ is used, the caster accepts Effrie’s Bargain. 
The caster cannot be disarmed, incapacitated, knocked unconscious, or forced to flee until the end of his next turn. Their weapon attacks automatically deal maximum damage for the next three Attack Actions that hit. Once the attacks are concluded or one minute passes, the caster is immediately *[[Conditions#Fatigued|Fatigued]]* (until a *Short Rest*) and suffers a number of *[[Hit Points#Grievous Wounds|Grievous Wounds]]* equal to the number of empowered attacks used.
