#### Description
This ornate longbow is a replica of the first Azak Emperor’s weapon. 
The wielder of this weapon heals for 1 hitpoint for every 10 damage dealt by this bow.

**Last Breath.** When you are reduced to *10 Hit Points* or lower you instantly gain **30 Temporary Hit Points** for 1 minute. 
This ability trigger once per *Short Rest*.
