*\[attune] (ring)* 

#### Description
A finely crafted ring with a small, crimson gemstone set into its band. To attune to the Ring of Storage, the wearer must place a drop of their own blood onto the gemstone. This connection is permanent unless the wearer willingly parts with the ring or dies.

- **Ring of Storage I** 
**Dimensional Vault.** The ring contains a personal extradimensional space, capable of storing up to 100 kg of non-living material, though no single object can exceed 2 meters in any dimension. 
Retrieving or Stowing an item requires an Action Point.

- **Ring of Storage II** 
**Instant Swap.** Once per long rest, as a free *Reaction*, the wearer can instantly stow (or retrieve) an item you are holding or wearing into the ring, even if you are *[[Conditions#Grappled|Grappled]]*, *[[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]]*, or otherwise *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]*.
