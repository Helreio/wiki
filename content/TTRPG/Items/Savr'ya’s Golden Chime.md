Rare
*\[attune] (bell) (holy symbol)*

#### Description
A tiny silver bell inlaid with a golden sigil of Savr'ya.
For one Action Point you can ring the bell. All creatures within 3 meters of you cannot make *Opportunity Attacks* until the start of your next turn.
This ability can be used twice per Short Rest.
