*\[attune] (belt) (unique)*
*+16 Hit Points*
*Resistance: Physical 5*

#### Description
Forged from interlocking plates of black iron, this heavy belt serves as the unbreakable anchor of an undying conqueror.

**Indestructible.** For one Action Point you draw upon the fragments of undeath lingering around you. You gain *Temporary Hit Points* equal to half the damage (rounded up) taken since the end of your last turn. 
At the beginning of your next turn any *Temporary Hit Points* remaining are converted into healing. 
At the end of your turn, roll a d6. On a 5 or 6, the ability recharges.

*{"Pain is fleeting. My will is eternal." - [[Kahn-Rokh the Conqueror]]}*

==Curse==
You cannot regain Hit Points by any normal means or Divine Powers (E.g. Healing Potions, Lay on Hand, Incantations etc).
