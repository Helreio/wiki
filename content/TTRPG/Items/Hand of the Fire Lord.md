*\[attune] (greathammer) (heavy, two-handed, siege)*
*(2d8 bludgeoning)*
*+2d6 fire damage*

#### Description
This greathammer was forged in the magma pits under the Scorched Peaks. Therefore, it gained Fire Lord’s Artan blessing during the smiting process.
It glows with an inner fire, leaving behind trails of smoldering ash and embers with each devastating blow. 

**Conflagration.** On a *Critical Strike*, the hammer unleashes a fiery shockwave that engulfs nearby enemies (within 1 meter of the target) in flames, causing them to burn for additional damage over time, for **2d6 fire damage**, at the start of their turn. 
They have *[[Conditions#On Fire!|On Fire!]]* condition for one minute or until extinguished by using three *Action Points*.
