*\[attune] (maul) (heavy, two-handed, reach (1m), siege) (unique)*
*(2d8 bludgeoning)*
*+2 to Strength Score*
*+2d6 necrotic damage*

#### Description
Forged from the screaming souls of the very sorcerers who foolishly sought to enslave him, this monolithic mass of unyielding black iron serves as the symbol of [[Kahn-Rokh the Conqueror]]'s will. 
With every earth-shattering descent, agonizing whispers cry out from the weapon.

**Shatter.** For one Action Point you strike with all your might in a 3-meter line. All creatures must make a DC 16 R*eflex Saving Throw* or take **4d8 bludgeoning & 4d6 necrotic damage.** On a success they only take half. 
If only one creature is within the area of effect the damage is doubled.
This ability can only be used once per turn. ^fb277a

==Curse of the Abyss== 
This item can only be wielded by an [[Undead]] or [[Fiend]], if otherwise the necrotic damage dealt is also taken by the wielder.

*{“A fortress fell before this weapon. Do you truly believe your flesh will endure?” - Kahn-Rokh the Conqueror}*
