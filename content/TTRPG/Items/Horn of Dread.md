*\[attune] (musical instrument)*

#### Description
The severed horn of a Terrogheist, repurposed as a battle horn. Its terrifying aura still lingers within the item.

**Shriek.** For one Action Point you can blow the horn. All creatures within 12 meters must make a DC 15 *Willpower Saving Throw* or become *[[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]]* until the end of your next turn.
