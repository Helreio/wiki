Mythic
*\[attune] (greathammer) (heavy, two-handed, siege) (unique) (Quest I)*
*(2d10 bludgeoning)*
*+2d6 radiant damage*
*+2d6 fire damage*

#### Description
Deep, jagged veins run across the obsidian-dark iron, pulsing white-hot from within. The haft is wrapped in blackened leather, still warm to the touch, as if the weapon itself is a dying star desperately clinging to its remaining heat. 
When swung, the surrounding air shimmers and ripples with distortion and unsettling screams of dying stars. 

**Unmaking.** Your melee attacks ignores resistance to *physical, fire & radiant damage.* 

---
**Awakened Power** (Quest II)
- **Ashrak’s Wrath.**
For one Action Point, you can ignite the weapon, causing flames to wreathe its surface for 1 minute emanating bright light for 4 meters and dim light for an additional 4 meters. 
**While ignited:** The weapon deals an extra **2d6 fire & 2d6 radiant damage** (8d6 total).
When you hit a creature, it must succeed on a DC 19 *Fortitude Saving Throw* or be knocked *[[Conditions#Prone|Prone]]* from the sheer force of the strike.
This ability can be used once per *Short Rest.*

- **Molten Sundering.**
For three Action Points you can slam the weapon into the ground, unleashing a 4-meter radius wave of molten flames.
Each creature in the area must make a DC 19 *Reflex Saving Throw*, taking **4d6 fire, 4d6 radiant damage and 4d10 bludgeoning damage** on a failed save, or half on a success. If you reduce a creature to 0 hit points, it erupts into flame, leaving only nonflammable equipment behind.
The ground in the area glows red-hot for 1 minute, becoming *Difficult Terrain* and igniting flammable objects. For each meter a creature moves through this area it takes **1d6 fire & 1d6 radiant damage.** 
You are not harmed by moving through this area effect.
At the end of your turn, roll a d6. On a 6, the ability recharges.

==Curse of the Fallen Sun==
If you go more than 24 hours without killing a creature (worthy foe) with this weapon, you suffer one *[[Hit Points#Grievous Wounds|Grievous Wound]]* that cannot be removed until you spill fresh blood in combat against a worthy foe.
You stand against all creations of the [[Making of the World; IO The Allfather|Allfather]]. Your alignment changes to *Chaotic Neutral*.
