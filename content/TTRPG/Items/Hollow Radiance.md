#### Description
This tarnished black armor is imbued with dark sorceries from the Abyss. Each wearer who dies donning the armor, their heart is claimed by the item increasing the maximum number of **Heartbeat** stacks. 

**Hollow.** Each combat round (Start of the Round), gain a *Heartbeat* stack (max 3).
Each stack grants the wearer **+1 necrotic damage** to spells and weapon attacks.

**Radiance.** At max stacks nearby creatures within 2 meters take the number of *Heartbeat* stacks **(3) + 1d12 necrotic damage** when they enter the emanation or end their turn within the effect. 
The damage can be taken only once per turn.

==Curse== 
For each minute spent on *Consecrated Ground* you take **1d6 necrotic damage**. 
This armor can only be removed by *[[Words of Restoration]]* incantation and the item is destroyed by the spell. 

*{A similar gift was bestowed by [[The Hollowed Lords; Rulers of the Abyss|The Hollowed Lords]] upon [[Kahn-Rokh the Conqueror|Kahn-Roth]] at the beginning of his reign}*
