Rare
*\[attune] (musical instrument) (unique)*

#### Description
The chipped blue paint at the edges shows its age.  
- **Song of Serenity.** Once per day as part of a *Short Rest* you can sing a soothing song of relief removing one Grievous Wound.
- **Song of Hope.** When a creature fails a *Willpower Saving Throw*, within 12 meters, versus a charm or fear effect. You can use your *Reaction* to play a tune and attempt to dispel the effect. You make a *Performance Check* vs the effect’s DC, if successful the target is no longer *[[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]]* or *[[Conditions#Frightened|Frightened]]*.
