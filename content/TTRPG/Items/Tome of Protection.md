*\[attune by a Warden] (book) (arcane focus)*

#### Description
The holy text imbues the wearer shield with divine light. 
While attuned, your *[[Actions#Shield Block (Reaction)|Shield Block Reaction]]* is replaced by **Holy Shield Reaction**.

**Holy Shield.** The block value of your shield increases by your *Charisma Modifier*, and any attack blocked with this *reaction* lashes out with the Light dealing **2d6 radiant damage** to the attacker.

*{The Light protects!}*
