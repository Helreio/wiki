*(1d8 piercing)*
*+2 to Hit*

#### Description
A cursed jagged [[Wyrm|wyrm]] fang that has felled countless creatures. The fang was retrieved from the remains of the ancient wyrm [[Va’reth]] after his death in the Second Age. 

Upon hitting the target you mark it with **Mercy** until the end of your next turn. (This mark stacks)

**Crimson Rite.** When [[Death]] consumes *Mercy* marks from a worthy foe, this weapon heals the wielder 1 hit point per mark consumed, and 5 hit points if the target is a Celestial.

*{A swift Death}*
