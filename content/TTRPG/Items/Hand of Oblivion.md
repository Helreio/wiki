#### Description
This band of black iron hums with green threads of lytel.

**Death's Grasp.** For one Action Point you can summon a spectral dark green hand 8 meters in front of you and every creature caught between you and the hand, must make a DC 16 *Fortitude Saving Throw* or get pulled towards you in melee and taking **2d8 bludgeoning & 2d6 necrotic damage**. On a success they take half the damage and they are not moved.
This ability can be used once per *Short Rest.*
