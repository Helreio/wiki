#### Description
A dire wolf's tooth on a string with Leshen symbols carved into it.

**Pack Tactics.** The wearer has *Advantage* on the first Attack roll against a creature if at least one of its allies is within 1 meter of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated. 
This effect applies once per turn.
