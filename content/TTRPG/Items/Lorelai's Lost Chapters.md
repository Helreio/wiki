### 0: Prologue
You can cast 'Fire Bolt', 'Ray of Frost', and 'Mage Hand'.  This property can be used a total of three times, and the page regains all expended charges daily at dawn.

>[!danger] Fire Bolt
>**School:** Devastation 
>**Casting Time:** 2 action points
>**Range:** 18 meters
>**Components:** V, S
>**Duration:** Instantaneous
>You hurl a mote of fire at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes **1d10 fire damage.** A flammable object hit by this spell ignites if it isn't being worn or carried.

>[!info] Ray of Frost
>**School:** Devastation 
>**Casting Time:** 2 action points
>**Range:** 12 meters
>**Components:** V, S
>**Duration:** Instantaneous
>A frigid beam of blue-white light streaks toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes **1d8 Cold damage**, and its Speed is reduced by 2 meters until the start of your next turn.

>[!example] Mage Hand
>**School:** Illusion 
>**Casting Time:** 2 action points
>**Range:** 6 meters
>**Components:** V, S
>**Duration:** 1 minute
>A spectral, floating hand appears at a point you choose within range. The hand lasts for the duration. The hand vanishes if it is ever more than 6 meters away from you or if you cast this spell again.
>When you cast the spell, you can use the hand to manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, or pour the contents out of a vial.
>
>For one *Action Point* on your later turns, you can control the hand thus again. As part of that action, you can move the hand up to 6 meters.
The hand can’t attack, activate magic items, or carry more than 5 kilograms.

### 1: Snooze
You can cast 'Snooze' without expending any lytel. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

>[!example] Snooze
>**School:** Spellbinding 
>**Casting Time:** 2 action points
>**Range:** 12 meters (1 meter sphere)
>**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sand)
>**Duration:** 1 minute
>Each creature of your choice in a 1-meter-radius Sphere centered on a point within range must succeed on a *Willpower Saving Throw* or have the *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]* condition until the end of its next turn, at which point it must repeat the save. If the target fails the second save, the target has the *[[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]]* condition for the duration. The spell ends on a target if it takes damage or someone within 1 meter of it takes an action to shake it out of the spell’s effect.
>
>Creatures that don't sleep or that have Immunity to the *[[Conditions#Charmed|Charmed]]* condition automatically succeed on saves against this spell.

---
### 2a: Flaming Fingers
You can cast 'Flaming Fingers' without expending any lytel. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

>[!danger] Flaming Fingers
>**School:** Devastation 
>**Casting Time:** 2 action points
>**Range:** Self, 3-meter cone
>**Components:** V, S
>**Duration:** Instantaneous
>A thin sheet of flames shoots forth from you. Each creature in a 3-meter Cone makes a *Reflex Saving Throw*, taking **3d6 fire damage** on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
>Flammable objects in the Cone that aren’t being worn or carried start burning.

---
### 2b: Messy Missile
You can cast 'Messy Missile' without expending any lytel. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

>[!info] Messy Missile
>**School:** Devastation 
>**Casting Time:** 2 action points
>**Range:** 18 meters
>**Components:** V, S
>**Duration:** Instantaneous
>You create three glowing darts of magical force. Each dart strikes a creature of your choice that you can see within range. A dart deals **1d4 + 1 arcane damage** to its target. The darts all strike simultaneously, and you can direct them to hit one creature or several.
