Artifact
*\[attune] (spellbook) (arcane focus) (unique)*

#### Description
This leather bound tome is filled with unintelligible runes from languages long forgotten, creating an enchantment that sucks the soul from fallen creatures in order to empower the wielder. If you touch this item you are forcefully attuned to it and it can be used as a spellbook.

**Soulstealer.** While attuned to this tome, each worthy creature that you slay adds a soul to the tome. Creatures you slay cannot be resurrected by any means, unless a *[[Words of Restoration]]* invocation or *[[Remove Curse]]* spell is cast upon the tome, releasing the souls trapped within and unattuning you from the item. 

**Broken Vessel.** While attuned to this item you forsake life and death. You gain **Vulnerability: Radiant & Necrotic 10**.

**Rite of Damnation.** You can make a 10-minute ritual to absorb the soul of a worthy creature that has died in the past hour.

**Whispers of the Dead.** The souls contained within the pages of this tome whisper constantly in your ear, making it difficult to sleep. While the tome contains 5 or more souls, roll a d12 at the end of each long rest. On a roll of 1, you gain one Grievous Wound at the end of a Long Rest. This changes to a roll of 1-3 at 40 souls, and 1-6 at 75 souls.

**Reliquary.** The tome can hold up to 101 souls. The time grants benefits according to the number of souls held within it, as follows:
- **1 Soul:** When you deal the killing blow to a worthy creature, you gain *Temporary Hit Points* equal to 2x your *Spellcasting Modifier*.
- **11 Souls:** You regain **2d8 hit points** every hour, provided you have at least 1 hit point.
- **22 Souls:** You gain an additional **+1 bonus** to *Spell Attack* rolls and to the ***Saving Throw DCs*** of your spells. 
	Your **Vulnerability: Radiant & Necrotic** increases by 5.
- **55 Souls:** You gain a **+3 bonus** to *Willpower Saving Throws*.
- **77 Souls:** You gain an additional **+2 bonus** to *Spell Attack* rolls and to the *Saving Throw DCs* of your spells. 
	Your **Vulnerability: Radiant & Necrotic** increases by 5.
- **100 Souls:** Your *Spellcasting Ability* score increases by 2. 
	The book becomes immune to any spells and transforms into a phylactery. You can use this item to undergo the ritual to become a Lich.

==Curse==
Each time you reach 0 hit points the tome will try to absorb your soul. 
You must make a DC 10 + the number of times this effect triggered *Willpower Saving Throw* to resist the effect. 

**Destroying the Item.**
*Grimoire Noir* can only be destroyed when it contains 100 souls. Once it contains 100 souls, the attuned creature may sacrifice their own life to destroy the tome, releasing the souls within. Each creature within 6 meters must succeed a DC 20 *Fortitude Saving Throw* or take **20d8 necrotic damage**. Creatures that succeed on the save take half damage.
Their maximum hit points are permanently reduced regardless if they saved or not.
