#### Description
A brutish looking handaxe with a wooden handle wrapped in leather.
If it is on the same plane of existence, you can summon the weapon for one Action Point on your turn, causing it to fly back into your hand.

*{Your heart aches with sorrow for lost kin when wielding the ax}*
