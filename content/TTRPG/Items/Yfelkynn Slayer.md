#### Description
One of the weapons developed in the skirmishes against Yfelkynn giants and their mounts.

**Killer of Giants.** This weapon deals an additional **1d10 damage** for each size class larger than the wielder of this crossbow the hit creature is. 
(e.g. a medium size creature gains +4d10 versus a colossal creature)
