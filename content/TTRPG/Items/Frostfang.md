*(1d8,1d10 slashing)*
*+2d6 cold damage*

#### Description
This magic sword has the power to freeze anything it touches, creating a layer of ice on its surface and dealing extra damage. 

**Icy Touch:** For one Action Point you can stab the ground and hit the pommel, creating a 3 meters radius frozen zone. It counts as *Difficult Terrain* and it lasts for 10 min. 
A creature moving through the area must make a DC 14 *Reflex Saving Throw* or fall *[[Conditions#Prone|Prone]]*. Objects within the area become brittle upon using the ability. 

This ability can be used once per day.
