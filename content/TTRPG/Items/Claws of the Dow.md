Uncommon
*(leather gloves) (unique)*

#### Description
A pair of leather gloves with each finger ending in a small metal claw.
When you do an *Unarmed Strike*, you deal **1d6 + *Agility & Strength Modifier* slashing damage.** 

You gain climbing speed equal to your *Movement*, and you can remain attached to a surface for 1 min.

*{Act before you think. - Blackdow, the Barbarian}*
