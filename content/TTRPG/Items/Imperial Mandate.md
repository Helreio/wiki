Legendary
*\[attune] (staff) (arcane focus)*
*(1d6,1d8 bludgeoning)*
*+12 max Lytel or Faith* 
*+3 to damage per Lytel or Faith spent casting the spell*

#### Description
This staff is adorned at the top with the standard of the Azak Empire showing dominance and might. 

**Surge.** Once per turn, when you cast a damaging spell through the wand, you can reroll 1 & 2's, taking the second result even if it's a 1 or 2.

**Imperial Mark.** When the user of this staff applies a [[Conditions|condition]] to a hostile creature with a spell, that creature is dealt and extra **2d4 bludgeoning damage** and becomes marked by this staff until the end of the user's next turn. When another friendly creature damages a creature marked this way, the marked is consumed and the creature takes **2d8 bludgeoning damage**.

**Cimmerian Conduit III.** When taking a *Short Rest*, the wearer of this item may expend *[[Rest Mechanic#Recovery Dice|Recovery Dice]]* to restore *1 + 3d4 lytel* per recovery dice spent.

**Components:** [[Blasting Wand]], [[Min’ra’s Tear]], [[Imperial Signet]].
