Legendary
*\[attune] (ring)* 

#### Description
While wearing this ring, you regain **1d4 hit points** every hour, provided that you have at least 1 hit point. 
If you lose a body part, the ring causes the missing part to regrow and return to full functionality after *1d12 + 1 days* if you have at least 1 hit point the whole time.
