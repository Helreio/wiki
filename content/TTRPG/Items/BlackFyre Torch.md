Epic
*\[attune] (scepter) (arcane focus) (unique)*
*(1d4 fire, 1d4 necrotic)*
*+3 damage to fire & necrotic spells*

#### Description
The shaft is made from netharum and silver and the small brazier at the top burns with a flame black as pitch, yet sheds a pale silvery dim light for 3 meters. No mortal has ever managed to extinguish it.

**Black Flame.** Whenever you reduce a creature to 0 HP with a spell the corpse erupts with blackfyre. All adjacent creatures take **1d6 fire** and **1d6 necrotic damage.**
