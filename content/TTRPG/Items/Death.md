Legendary
*\[attune] (dagger) (finesse, serrated, light, thrown (3/12m)) (unique)*
*(1d6 slashing, 1d6 bleeding)*
*+3 to damage*

#### Description
A cursed jagged [[Wyrm|wyrm]] fang that has felled countless creatures. The fang was retrieved from the remains of the ancient wyrm [[Va’reth]] after his death in the Second Age. 

A successful hit with this weapon consumes all the *Mercy* stacks dealing additional **1d6 bleeding damage** per Mercy stack consumed.

**Death’s Caress.** When a creature marked by [[Mercy]] attempts to move away from you more than 3 meters, as a *Reaction* you may throw Death at it. Make a ranged weapon attack with *Advantage* against the target.

*{The unavoidable End}*
