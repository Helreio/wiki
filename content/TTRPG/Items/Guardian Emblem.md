Legendary
*\[attune] (Sigil) (component)*

#### Description
This emblem is a symbol of your faith. You can attach the emblem (or remove it) to a suit of armor or a shield during a *Short Rest*.

The emblem has 3 charges. When you or a creature you can see within 6 meters of you is hit while you're wearing the armor or wielding the shield (that bears the emblem), you can use your *Reaction* to expend 1 charge to give them **Resistance: Physical equal to their maximum Armor (Ar)** until the start of your turn.

The emblem regains all expended charges daily at dawn.
