*\[attune] (bracelet) (unique)*

#### Description
This silver bracelet has a small blue sapphire containing surges of wild energy. 
You can cast the *[[Shocking Grasp]]* spell, and once per turn you can choose to cast it through the bracelet as AoE that affects all creatures within 1 a meter around you. 
The creatures need to make a *Fortitude Saving Throw* against your Spell Save DC. On a fail they only take **1d10 lightning damage** and gain the *[[Conditions#Shocked|Shocked]]* condition until the start of its next turn. 
By casting it in this manner the spell can be *Empowered* only to increase the damage. 
One additional 1d10 per lyrel spent up to your *Intelligence Modifier.*

*{You can shock your way through any problem. - Aldroksell}*
