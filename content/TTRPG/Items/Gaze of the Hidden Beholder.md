*(ring) (unique)*

#### Description
When the ring is placed on a solid surface made of stone (no thicker than 1 meter), wood (10 cm), metal (2 cm), it will create a temporary illusion of a 2-centimeter hole granting sight through the surface. 

*{“Refrain from using the ring for lewd activities”. - Koga}*
