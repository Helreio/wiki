Legendary
*\[attune] (censer) (arcane focus) (unique)*
*+2 to Spell Attack & Spell Save DC's*

#### Description
A gilded censer suspended by three silver chains. Its incense never burns away, and its fragrant smoke carries the warmth of unwavering faith. A holy relic created in the beginning of the Third Age.

**Litany of Faith.** Whenever you cast a beneficial spell that uses resources on a creature, you can choose one of the following:
- **Zeal.** The next spell or weapon attack they make before the end of their next turn deals **+1d6 radiant damage**.
- **Conviction.** The creature gains *Advantage* on its next *Attack* roll or *Skill Check* before the end of its next turn.
- **Burning Faith.** The creature gains *Temporary Hit Points* equal to *7 + Spellcasting Modifier*.

*{"Even the faintest ember, tended with care, may yet become holy flame." - Minerva’s dictum}*
