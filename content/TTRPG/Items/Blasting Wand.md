*+1 to damage per Lytel or Faith spent casting the spell*

#### Description
A simple yet potent arcane focus, the Blasting Wand is a conduit of raw magical force. While lacking refinement, it amplifies destructive spells with unrelenting power, favored by battle-hardened magi of old who seek overwhelming offense over finesse.

**Surge.** Once per turn, when you cast a damaging spell through the wand, you can reroll 1 & 2's, taking the second result even if it's a 1 or 2.

==Unstable Magic.== When casting a spell through the wand roll a flat d20. If you roll a natural 1 or 2 the wand flares with unstable energy, forcing you to make a DC 15 *Fortitude Saving Throw*. On a failure, you also take the spell's damage. On a success you only take half the damage (rounded up).
