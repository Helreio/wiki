For those who qualify, once a hireling has been found and agrees to join, a character sheet can be quickly rolled up for the hireling, if desired. A player may take on a hireling as their main character at any time. This is especially useful if their PC dies, or if the PC wants to take a break or retire from adventuring altogether.

The maximum number of hirelings cannot exceed your _Charisma Modifier._

The employer pays advance fees in case of sudden death for the family of the hire.

##### **Stat Block Example:**
**Hireling** (John)
*(Humanoid)*
**Hp:** 25 **Move:** 5m
**EC (Ar):** 11 (6)
**Fort +2, Ref +2, Will +1**
**Racial Passive:** Human, Leshy, Yfelkynn

**Longsword.** +4, 1m, 1d8+2s.
**LCrossbow.** +4, 16/64m, 1d8+1p.

**Warrior.** Action Surge 1/SR + One Fighting Style
**Acolyte.** Lay on Hands + One Blessing
