## Strength (Str) 
Strength measures bodily power, athletic training, and the extent to which you can exert raw physical force.
- ***Athletics** (Jump, Climb, Swim, Grapple, Push, Lift)*
- **Hold Breath:** Equals your _Strength Modifier_ * 1 minute
- **Carry Capacity:** Strength Score x 7kg

## Agility (Agi) 
Agility measures dexterity, reflexes and balance.
- **_Acrobatics_**
- ***Sleight of hand** (Disarm Traps, Pick Pockets)*
- ***Stealth/Hide** (Sneaking & Hiding)*
- _Finesse Weapons_ can use Agility instead of Strength on To Hit and damage.
- **_Movement:_** 3 + _Agility Modifier_ (min. of 1) meters.

## Charisma (Cha) 
Charisma measures a character’s persuasiveness, performance, ability to lead, force of personality and influence exerted to gain favors.
- **_Deception_**
- **_Intimidation_**
- **_Insight_**
- **_Performance_**
- **_Persuasion_**
- ***Attunement Slots** (min. of 1)*
- ***Henchmen** (to hire and command)*

## Intelligence (Int) 
Intelligence measures mental acuity, accuracy of recall, the ability to reason and process information.
- ***Arcana** (Detect, Read & Identify Magic)*
- **_Investigation_**
- **_Lore:_**
	- Religion
	- Occultism
    - Society
- **_Medicine_**
- ***Intuition** (Passive Intuition is 10 + Intuition)*
- ***Survival** (Forage, Animal Handling, Tracking & Nature)*

## Aetheric Erosion (AE) 
Aetheric Erosion is gained from touching [[Cimmerian Conduits]] or being near a [[Cruciable]].
Also when you cast a spell that requires more Lytel (spell points) than your current capacity allows, you risk eroding your very soul. Immediately after casting the spell, you take **1d6 *Aetheric Erosion*.**
[[Aetheric Erosion Table & Mental Effects]]

### *Proficiency* 
- ***Armor,*** if you are proficient you can use the armor without penalty (D.Adv on attacks, saves and skill checks).
* ***Kits and Tools,*** you are able to make use of it and add your appropriate skill modifier to the check. If you gain Mastery with an item you are also proficient with that item.

### *Mastery* 
**Skills, Tools, Spells** and **Weapons** can be improved as part of a ***Full Rest*** or ***Downtime*** by training and spending Exp.

**_Mastery I_**_._ You gain a +2 for a skill, tool or weapon attack modifier. The bonus increases by 2 for every rank further. Eg. **Mastery II (+4), Mastery III (+6)**
