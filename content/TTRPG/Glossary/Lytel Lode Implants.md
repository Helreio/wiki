Implants sanctioned by the empire are crafted by Master Makers of both _Aludry_ and _Seidhr._ One such name is ***Sasha Kuznetsov of House Lysnov.*** His work bears the simple mark **SK**, and commissions from him allow three spells of your choosing to be inscribed upon the lode.

By contrast, cheap knock offs that circulate through the black market carry only one or two fixed spells. _Summoning_ and _Necromancy_ are frowned upon within the empire. If you are caught with lodes made of _netharum_ and _thanium,_ after a thorough investigation by an inquisitor, you are sentenced to death.

The operation takes around one hour, and you gain **2d6 Aetheric Erosion (AE)** and **4 + 2d4 Lytel**. Any additional lodes after the first one will increase your **Aetheric Erosion (AE)** by **2d6** and **Lytel** by **2d4.**

**Spell Attack:** *Intelligence Modifier + Mastery I (+2)*
**Spellcasting Mastery:** Spell Attack and Spell Save DC can be increased from *[[General Talents#^5d9698|General Talents]]*.
