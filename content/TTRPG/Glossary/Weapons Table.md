## Properties

Many weapons have special properties related to their use, as shown in the Weapons Table.

### Heavy

A heavy weapon’s size and bulk makes it cumbersome for creatures to use effectively in combat if they don't have a Strength score of minimum 13. If they have a Strength Score of 17 or higher the weapon does **1.5 times (instead of x1) your _Strength Modifier_ (rounded up) additional weapon damage.**

### Light

A light weapon is small and easy to conceal and handle, making it ideal for use when fighting with two Weapons. When you take the attack action **you attack twice (1/Turn),** once with your main hand and once with your offhand, they both need to have the _Light_ property. If you have an *Agility Score* of 20 or higher you can attack with your offhand **twice per turn (2/Turn)**.

### Finesse

When making an Attack with a finesse weapon, you use your choice of your Strength or Agility modifier for the Attack and Damage Rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.

### Range

A weapon that can be used to make a ranged Attack has a range in parentheses after the Ammunition or thrown property. The range lists two numbers. The first is the weapon’s normal range in meters, and the second indicates the weapon’s Long Range. When Attacking a target beyond normal range, you have disadvantage on the Attack roll. You can’t Attack a target beyond the weapon’s Long Range.

### Reach

This weapon adds 1 meter to your reach when you Attack with it, as well as when determining your reach for _Opportunity Attacks_ with it.

### Thrown

If a weapon has the thrown property, you can throw the weapon to make a ranged Attack. If the weapon is a melee weapon, you use the same ability modifier for that Attack roll that you would use for a melee Attack with the weapon.

### Two-Handed

This weapon requires two hands when you Attack with it.

### Versatile

This weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property—the damage when the weapon is used with two hands to make a melee Attack.

### Siege

This weapon deals double damage against objects, fortifications, vehicles, etc. When it hits an armored creature it ablates Armor by 2 instead of 1.

### Multiattack

This weapon property allows for one _Attack Action_ to fire or swing your weapon in a flurry multiple times, the damage dice is reduced by two tiers for _Multiattack (2)_ and by three tiers for _Multiattack (3)._

Eg. weapon die is a 1d12, _Multiattack (2)_ becomes 1d8 and for one _Action Point_ you attack twice. _Multiattack (3)_ is 1d6 and for one _Action Point_ you attack three times.

### Serrated

This weapon deals bleeding damage against organic, unarmored, or monstrous targets. This damage bypasses natural armor and _Physical_ damage _Resistance_. Deals 0 damage to armored targets.

In case of multiple damage types, if the _physical_ damage part of the attack (b, p, s) does not reduce hit points, the _bleeding_ damage done is 0.

A bladed weapon can be modified to have this property, gaining additional bleeding damage (1d6) but the weapon’s normal damage die is reduced by one tier (eg. 1d10 becomes 1d8).

_Bleeding damage type_ is not calculated for *[[Actions#Parry (Reaction)|Parry]]* reaction.

### Exotic

Weapons with this property cannot be used if you did not have special training required by the weapon. They otherwise count as improvised weapons.
Exotic weapons can only be trained with a substantial time investment and Exp before being used properly in battle.

### Special

Weapons with special rules are described here.

**Whip:** Instead of doing damage on a hit, you can choose to grapple the target. The creature needs to do a _Reflex Saving Throw_ vs the _Attack roll,_ on a failed save the creature gains the *[[Conditions#Grappled|Grappled]]* condition. While maintaining the grapple you cannot attack with the whip.

**Lance:** If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can force the creature to make a _Fortitude Saving Throw_ against your _Attack_ roll. On a failed save, the creature has the *[[Conditions#Prone|Prone]]* condition.
