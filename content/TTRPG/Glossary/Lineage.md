# Humans

**Appearance:** Humans are one of the most diverse races in terms of appearance. Their physical traits can vary significantly depending on their ancestry and environment.
	**Age:** They reach adulthood in their late teens and usually live less than a century.
	**Size:** They vary widely in height and build, from 150 - 210 centimeters tall. Your size is **Medium**.

- ### Pessek (+2,+1 to any stat)
They originate from the red sands beyond the mountains, west of the Azak Empire. They live a nomad lifestyle.
**Desert Strider.** Your movement is **not reduced by difficult terrain** caused by sand, loose gravel, or dry terrain. When you take the **Run/Charge** action, you can **move across vertical surfaces and difficult terrain without expending extra movement** until the end of your turn.

- ### **Azak** (+2,+1 to any stat)
Pureblood citizens of the Azak Empire.
**Imperial Discipline.** You gain a **Mastery I (+2)** in one of the following skills: Athletics, Intuition or Survival, if you already have mastery you increase the _Mastery Rank_ by 1. 
When rolling _Initiative_, you add **1d4** to your roll.

# Leshy (Half-breeds)

They are born from a communion of Humans and Leshen (tribes sired by Leshari in the Second Age). They have special characteristics based on their Leshen descent.

- ### **Cernos, the White Stag** (+1 to any stat)
They are born to wander, their bodies built for relentless movement across endless terrain. Whether traversing dense forests or open plains, they move with an unnatural stamina, rarely tiring and always pressing forward.
**Appearance:** Slender, small ornate antlers, some have hoofed feet, glossy eyes, fair skin.  
**Age:** They reach adulthood in their early thirties and live around 150 years.  
**Size:** They have a very slender build, from 180 - 240 centimeters tall. Your size is **Medium.**
**Stag’s Reflexes.** When an enemy you can see targets you with an attack, you can use your _Reaction_ to move **3 meters** without provoking opportunity attacks.
**Wanderer’s Instinct.** You always know **which direction is north**, and you can recall terrain and settlements you have seen within the last week with accuracy.

- ### **Perrun, the Storm Eagle** (+1 to any stat)
Their bodies seem charged with the sky’s fury, and the surrounding air often carries the faint scent of rain. Perrun are known to move with the unpredictability of a storm, striking as swiftly as a lightning bolt.
**Appearance:** Beaked nose, rounded ears, clawed feet, a small hunch.
**Age:** They reach adulthood in their early teens and live around 60 years.
**Size:** They have a slender build, from 140 - 180 centimeters tall. Your size is **Medium.**
**Sky Touched.** When you fall, you can slow your descent, taking no damage from a fall of 6 meters or fewer and treating any greater fall as half the normal distance for damage calculation.
**Wind’s Swiftness.** Your _movement_ is increased by 2 meters.

- ### **Azhdaya the Iron Serpent** (+1 to any stat)
Azhdaya are unyielding and imposing, their presence like that of a coiled serpent, ever-watchful. Their unyielding spirit makes them natural leaders, warriors, and survivors, thriving in the face of adversity.
**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered, scaled patches on skin, slit eyes, forked tongue, ridged brow, thick limbs.  
**Age:** They reach adulthood in their early teens and live up to 200 years.  
**Size:** They have a sturdy and powerful build, standing between 170 - 210 centimeters tall. Your size is **Medium.**
**Auburn Scales.** Your EC is increased by +1.
**Venomous Fangs(1/turn).** Your Bite attack deals 1d6 Piercing damage + 1d6 Poison damage + Strength or Agility Modifier. The attack is done with the highest Mastery your character has with weapons.

- ### **Sirin the Enchantress** (+1 to any stat)
A mystical being of song and enchantment, Sirin’s voice is said to bring both bliss and sorrow, weaving magic into melody. Her presence inspires those around her, uplifting spirits or lulling foes into dreamlike trances.
**Appearance:** Elegant with soft feathers covering parts of their arms, neck, and shoulders. Sky blue, pupil-less eyes with an otherworldly beauty.
**Age:** They reach adulthood in their late teens and live up to 90 years, their voices only growing more potent with age.
**Size:** They are gracefully built, standing between 120 - 190 cm tall. Your size is **Small** or **Medium**.
**Silver-Tongued.** You can perfectly mimic voices and sounds you have heard for at least one minute. Creatures must make an *Insight Check* (DC = 10 + your Charisma Modifier) to determine if the voice/sounds are fake.
**Lullaby(1/SR):**
- **Of Woe.** 
	Your haunting song weaves sorrow into the minds of those who hear it, enthralling them. For two _Action Points_, you sing a mournful lullaby. Creatures of your choice within 6 meters that can hear you must make a _Willpower Saving Throw_ DC 10 + your _Charisma Modifier_ or be _Enthralled_ for 1 minute. A creature that **succeeds** on the saving throw is **immune** to this trait for 24 hours.
	**_Enthralled_** creatures are *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]* (See Condition), their movement speed is 0 and cannot take the _Attack_ action. The effect ends if the creature takes any damage or if someone else shakes the creature out of its stupor.
- **Of Solace.** 
	Your gentle melody steadies the spirit and rekindles resolve. For two _Action Points_, you raise your voice in a soft, comforting hymn. Creatures of your choice within 6 meters that can hear you are healed for 1d6 + _Charisma Modifier (min of 1)._ Any charm effect currently active on the chosen characters is rerolled with *Advantage*.

# Yfelkynn

The Yfelkynn, or Evil Kin within the Azak Empire, are an ancient race of giants, their massive forms shaped by the unforgiving wilderness they call home. Though feared by many, they are survivors, bound by oaths and traditions. The most commonly seen are slaves serving the Azak nobility within the mines. But some have gained their freedom after the Leshy Revolution.

- ### Duskwalker (+1 Agi, +1 to any stat)
**Appearance:** Lanky but powerful, their ashen-gray skin blends with the dusk. Their piercing, luminous eyes shift between hues of violet and green, and their long, matted hair carries the scent of rain.
**Age:** They mature by 30 years and can live up to 500 years, their wisdom deepening with the centuries.
**Size:** Towering at 180 - 280 cm tall, their lean forms are deceptively swift. Your size is **Medium** or **Large**.
**Shadowmeld.** If you stand perfectly still in dim light or darkness, you become nearly indistinguishable from your surroundings, you gain the *[[Conditions#Invisible|Invisible]]* condition while standing still.
**Gloom Stalker.** When attacking a creature that hasn’t detected you, you can roll an extra weapon damage die on your first hit.

- ### Grimstone (+1 Str, +1 to any stat)
**Appearance:** Heavyset and broad, their dark, rock-like skin is rough, resembling the stones of the earth itself. Their glowing ember-like eyes smolder with a quiet intensity.
**Age:** They reach maturity by 40 years and live up to 600 years, their strength growing with age.
**Size:** Massive and imposing, they stand at 240 - 320 cm tall with their sheer bulk. Your size is **Large.**
**Stoneborn.** You gain Advantage on _Fortitude Saving Throws_ against being *[[Conditions#Stunned|Stunned]]* or *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]*.
**Mountain’s Grip.** Your hands can crush rock, and no weapon is too heavy for you. You can wield **two-handed heavy weapons** with one hand.
