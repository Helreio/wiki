# Tier 0

- **Stats**
Roll 3d6’s for each Stat (A total minimum of 38 or reroll a new set). Standard array: 14, 11, 9, and 8.

- **Lineage** 
[[Lineage#Humans|Humans]], [[Lineage#Leshy (Half-breeds)|Leshy]], [[Lineage#Yfelkynn|Yfelkynn]]

- **Classes** 
Warrior, Thaumaturge, Warden, Strix, Shaman

- **Backgrounds**
*Custom:* Any two skills (+ 1 Mastery Rank), and any one tool , kit or Instrument.

- **Starting Equipment**
*Survival Pack (10 gp):* backpack, mess kit, tinderbox, lantern, oil (5), rations (10), one waterskin (3 liters), hempen rope (15m), small knife.

Any additional equipment is bought with your class starting gold from the lists: [[Weapons Table]], [[Armor Table]], [[Adventuring Gear]], [[Tools & Instruments]] and [[Consumables]].

# Tier I

- **Specialization** (Subclass)
Choose one Specialization from your chosen class.

- **Talents**
You have 60 Exp to spend. [[General Talents]] or Class specific Talents.
