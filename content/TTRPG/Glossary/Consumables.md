**Antidote.** 
	This white tablet, when consumed, removes the *[[Conditions#Poisoned|Poisoned]]* condition. ^e019ce

---
**Armor Repair Kit (1 use).** 
	This kit contains depending on which armor is intended to fix; heavy needles, sinew, leather patches, spare metal rings, rivets, and a portable hammer designed to restore damaged equipment. This activity can be performed as part of a *Short Rest*. ^e7fc4d
- **Light Armor Kit:** Light armor, Wooden Shield & Buckler takes 10–15 minutes.
- **Medium Armor Kit:** Medium Armor & Steel Shield takes 30 minutes.
- **Heavy Armor Kit:** Heavy Armor & Tower Shield takes 1 hour. 

---
**Ball bearings** (bag of 1,000). 
	For one Action Point, you can spill the bearings from their pouch to cover a level area that is 2x2 meters.
	Any creature moving across the covered area must succeed on a DC 10 *Reflex Saving Throw* or fall *[[Conditions#Prone|Prone]]*. However, if a creature moves through the area at half speed, they do not need to make the save. ^22082f

---
**Black Firebomb.**  
	*Finesse, Thrown (range 6/12)*, **2d6 fire damage** to target hit and **2d6 fire damage** to all creatures within 1 meter of the initial target if they fail a *Reflex Saving Throw* DC 12, half for a success. 
	Applies *[[Conditions#On Fire!|On Fire!]]* Condition. Smells of sulfur. ^b1f027

---
**Bolt Paper.** 
	An alchemical creation that wreaths weapons in lightning when rubbed along their surface. Rubbing this coarse paper along your weapon requires one Action Point, adding **1d8 Lightning damage** to your attacks with that weapon for one minute.
	This item can't be used with weapons with pre-existing elemental effects. ^0a4ac4

---
**Caltrops (bag of 20).** 
	For one Action Point, you can spread a single bag of caltrops to cover a 1-meter area. Any creature that enters the area must succeed on a *Reflex Saving Throw* DC 15 or stop moving until the start of their next turn and take **1 piercing damage**. 
	A creature moving through the area at half speed doesn't need to make the saving throw. ^0eeb33

---
**Counterweight.** 
	You spend 10 minutes clamping a heavy metal ring to the pommel or shaft of a bludgeoning weapon. For the next 1 hour, the weapon deals **+1 bludgeoning damage** on a hit due to increased impact force. ^78bc7b

---
**Dream Stick (3 use).** 
	This pipe is filled with a different assortment of plants boosting your natural healing during a rest period. During a *Short Rest* you regain one additional hit point. ^215115

---
**Fire Paper.** 
	Another alchemical creation that is also applied to weapons, sparking flames to their surface upon usage. Rubbing this coarse paper along your weapon requires one
	Action Point, adding **1d6 Fire damage** to your attacks with that weapon for one minute.
	This item can't be used on weapons with pre-existing elemental properties. ^6498f7

**Potion of Healing.** 
	Upon drinking the potion you are healed for: ^766f62
- **Basic:** 2d4+2
- **Greater:** 4d4+4
- **Superior:** 6d4+6

**Holy Water.** 
	For one Action Point, you can douse the contents of this vial onto a creature within 1 meter of you or throw it up to 4 meters, shattering it on impact. In either case, make a ranged Attack against a target creature, treating the holy water as an improvised weapon. If the target is a fiend or undead, it takes **2d6 radiant damage**. ^ccc9eb

---
**Holy Oil.** 
	Used for anointing during sermons and cleansing the burden of sin. For one Action Point, you can douse the contents of this vial onto a creature within 1 meter of you or throw it up to 4 meters, shattering it on impact. In either case, make a ranged Attack against a target creature, treating the holy oil as an improvised weapon. If the target is a fiend or undead, it takes **1d6 radiant damage**. 
	Applied to a weapon (or one piece of ammunition) it deals **1d6 radiant damage** for the next two attack. ^3469f3

**Healer's Kit (5 uses).** 
	This kit is a leather pouch containing bandages, salves, and splints. The kit has five uses. Using one Action Point, you can expend one use of the kit and stabilize them from *[[Conditions#On Death’s Door|On Death’s Door]]* condition. If you have *Mastery Rank I* or higher with the kit you can heal a creature for **1 + Medicine Skill Modifier** as you stabilize them. If a creature regains hit points from this kit it cannot do so until it finishes a *Short Rest*. ^d903c7

**Thieves' Tools (10 uses).** 
	A set of lock picks that can be used to open locks with a successful *Thieves' Tools Check* (1d20 + Agility Modifier + Thieves' Tools Mastery Rank). On a failed Check the lock pick breaks. ^1c41d7

**Twilight Soot (2 uses).**
	A mixture of powered krytth, produced by the Church and used to offer protection and concealment against the creatures of darkness. One use of this powder can be burned for 30 min in a thurible or a small bowl. ^ba3ee5

---
**Rabbit Flavor Supplements (5 uses).** 
	A pouch with five small black beads. When ingested you might have advantage on saves vs mind-altering effects for 10 min. It comes with a small note inside the bag.
	*{They put chemicals in the water. - signed Ghinn Blutaxt}* ^17045b

---
**Shield Barbs.** 
	A blacksmith can affix barbs to your shield, each time you Shield Block Reaction it deals piercing damage to the attacker. This modification is additional to a MasterCrafted or Enchanted effect.
	**Thorns:** *1d4, 1d8, 1d12* piercing damage. 
	This effect is intended to work with *[[Actions#Shove|Shove]]* action, *[[Fighting Style#Shield Master (R)|Shield Master]]* and *[[Warrior#Master at Arms|Master at Arms]]* Shield Bash Manuver. 
	When the shield is damaged by a *Critical Strike* the barbs are destroyed. ^8d2c80

---
**Whetstone (5 uses).** 
	It takes ten minutes to sharpen a bladed weapon. Sharpened weapons deal an additional **2d4 slashing damage** for the next two attacks that hit. The stone has five uses. ^4ad7f8
