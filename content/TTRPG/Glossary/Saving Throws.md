*You gain Mastery in Saving Throws from your class archetype.*

## Reflex

*d20 roll + Agility Modifier + other bonuses + penalties*
- Dodging out of the way of a trap
- Reducing fall damage
- Resisting spell effects like reducing damage from the [[Shatter]] spell

## Fortitude 

*d20 roll + Strength Modifier + other bonuses + penalties*
- Resist being pushed by a runaway cart
- Saving against a poison or disease
- Resisting environmental effects or elemental effects

## Willpower 

*d20 roll + Intelligence or Charisma Modifier + other bonuses + penalties*
- Resisting spell effects like the feeblemind, charm or banishment spell
- Resisting abilities like a ghost’s possession or mind altering effects
- Maintaining your composure after experiencing something alien

## Death Rolls 

*d20 flat roll*
- If you fall below 0 _Hit Points_ you gain *[[Conditions#On Death’s Door|On Death's Door]] condition.* You must make a _Death Roll_ on the start of your next turn with a DC of 10.
- A failed roll grants the character one *[[Hit Points#Grievous Wounds|Grievous Wound]]*. Any subsequent damage taken while *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]* grants an additional *[[Hit Points#Grievous Wounds|Grievous Wound]]* per damage instance.
- If you are healed by any amount, you are no longer *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]* (See Condition) but you retain all the *[[Hit Points#Grievous Wounds|Grievous Wounds]]* accumulated. An ally's _Medicine (Intelligence) Check_ will stabilize you and end *[[Conditions#On Death’s Door|On Death's Door]]* condition, but you will remain *[[Conditions#Incapacitated|Incapacitated]]* for 1d12 min.
- *[[Hit Points#Grievous Wounds|Grievous Wounds]]* are reduced at the rate of one per Long Rest and completely after a *Full Rest*, minimum days equal to half (rounded up) the number of Grievous Wounds.
