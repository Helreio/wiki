Inspiration is awarded at the end of the session by the players amongst themselfs. A character can have a maximum of three _Inspiration Points_.
- **Reroll a D20 Tests:** When you roll a d20 for a D20 Test (an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw), you can spend one _Inspiration Point_ to reroll the dice.
- **Disadvantage on Attacks:** When a creature rolls a d20 for an attack roll against you, you can spend one _Inspiration Point_ to impose _Disadvantage_ on that roll.
