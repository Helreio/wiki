### The Chart

For the renown system, this chart lists the different factions and the renown the player has with each one. Renown is tracked individually, although exceptions will be made in special cases.

Eg. Chart:

| **Faction**                 | **Renown** |
| --------------------------- | ---------- |
| Stormhold Keep              | 20         |
| Church of the Seventh Light | 15         |
| Deepstone Syndicate         | 0          |
| Azure Flame                 | 3          |
| Ravenhusk Guild             | 9          |

### The Scale

**0 - Mortal Enemies.** The faction will go out of its way to oppose or murder the party, depending on their alignment, motivations, and methods.

**5 - Enemies.** The faction views the Player Character as enemies that should be opposed when encountered. Faction members will oppose or murder the party (as their alignment, motivations, and methods allow) when convenient opportunities present themselves.

**10 - Neutral.** The faction doesn't care one way or another about the Player Character.

**15 - Friends.** Faction views the Player Character in a positive light. Faction members will aid when it isn't too much trouble or when properly recompensed or when it aligns with their motivations.

**20 - Trusted allies.** Faction considers the Player Character as trusted ally and confidant. Faction members will go out of their way to aid as much as possible. The faction may extend a membership offer if doing so fits with their motivations and methods.

### **Gaining and Losing Faction Renown**

As the player completes missions/contracts or otherwise does things in the world, those things either positively or negatively affect their favor with the different factions. When that happens, it will increase or decrease the player's favor with factions on the chart
