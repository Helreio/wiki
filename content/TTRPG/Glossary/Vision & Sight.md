**Normal Vision.** 
In *Bright Light*, creatures see normally. 
In *Dim Light*, creatures can see, but everything is lightly obscured to them. Imposes *Disadvantage* on sight-based *Intuition Checks* (-5 to passive *Intuition*).
In *Darkness*, a creature with normal vision is effectively *[[Conditions#Blinded|blinded]]*. 

---
**Darkvision.** 
A creature with darkvision can see in *Dim Light* as if it were *Bright Light*, and within *Darkness* as if it were *Dim Light*, though *Darkness* is usually perceived in black and white. ^31d756

---
**Blindsight.**
Allows a creature to perceive its surroundings without relying on sight within a specific radius, often used by creatures with echolocation or no eyes.  ^d714be

---
**Sense Life.** 
A creature can perceive the living and their general location even *Heavily Obscured* or hidden creatures out to a specific range. Usually three headed koshmeras have this sense. ^9e7fa9

---
**Eyes of the Third.** 
A creature partially sees into [[The Veiled Weft]]. The creatures and objects from the Weft appear ghostly and translucent.

---
**Arcane Eyes.** 
A gift from [[Making of the World; IO The Allfather|The Allfather]]. It allows the creature to perceive lytel within 12 meters, and even the school of magic within 3 meters.

---
**Abyssal Sight.** 
You can see normally in *Dim Light* and *Darkness*—both magical and nonmagical—within 12 meters of yourself. Over 12 meters you perceive *Darkness* as *Dim Light.* 

---
**Witchsight.** 
You can see the true form of any shapechanger or creature concealed by illusion or transmutation magic while the creature is within 6 meters of you and within line of sight. ^4d7111

---
**Truesight.** 
The most powerful sense; a creature can see in normal and magical *Darkness*, detect *[[Conditions#Invisible|invisible]]* creatures/objects, see through illusions, and perceive [[The Veiled Weft]] out to a specific range. ^190c8b
