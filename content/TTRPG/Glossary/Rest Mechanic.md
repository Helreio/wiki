## Short Rest 
A short rest is a period of downtime, at least 30 min long, during which characters can take a break, catch their breath, recover some of their resources and they can expend _Recovery Dice_ to regain hit points back.
	A _Short Rest_ can be taken **twice per day (2/24h)**.

## Long Rest 
A long rest lasts for at least 6 hours of downtime, during which characters typically sleep for 4 hours and engage in light activity for the remaining 2 hours, such as eating, reading, or keeping watch. 
	During the Long Rest they replenish all *[[Rest Mechanic#Recovery Dice|Recovery Dice]]* and recover all eligible features and class abilities.
	Long rests remove one *[[Conditions#Grievous Wounds|Grievous Wound]]* from characters.

After the first 3 hours they regain full health and all _Recovery Dice._ After the next 3 hours they regain their class features. The Long Rest cannot be interrupted more than 1 hour to receive the full effect.

## Full Rest
Takes a minimum of two full days, restores all features, Health and Recovery Dices. Can be combined with Downtime Activities. (Eg. Crafting an Item, Crime, Gambling, Pit Fighting, Relaxation, Religious Service, Research, Selling Items, Training, Work, etc.)
A Full Rest needs to be taken in a _sanctuary_, like a Town, Village or Home Base.

### Recovery Dice 
Are a pool of dice used during a _Short Rest_ to recover hit points. The pool is equal to *Strength Modifier (min of 1) + Agility Modifier (min of 1)* and refreshes after a _Long Rest_.
	**Math:** *1d6 + Strength Modifier (min of 1) + Agility Modifier (min of 1)* for each dice rolled to heal yourself.
