### Evasion Class (EC) 
Natural evasion against Attacks.
	**Math:** *EC = 10 + Agility Modifier + Other Bonuses (Eg. Armor, Shield, Spells, Class Features etc.)*

### Armor (Ar)
When you are hit by an Attack that exceeds your _Evasion Class (EC)_, you reduce the physical damage by an amount equal to your Armor value. This represents failing to evade the Attack completely, but mitigating some damage with your armor.

- **Armor (Ar) is ablated** (reduced) upon taking phisical damage **by 1.**
	At 0 Armor the items are damaged but it still grants their other bonuses. The armor can be repaired with a repair kit or at the appropriate craftsman (blacksmith, leatherworker, etc).
- Being hit by a ***Critical Strike*** bypasses Armor (Ar).
- Your Armor (Ar) is determined by the type of equipment you are wearing. Consult the [[Armor Table]].
- If you are not proficient with that type of armor, your attacks, saves, skill checks have _disadvantage_ and you cannot cast spells or incantations.

***Example Stats for MasterCrafted Armor***
MasterCrafted potency is based on the quality of the reagents and Skill Checks when crafting the armor.
- Evasion Class (+1, +2)
- Elemental Resistance (Res: Fire 5)
- Stat Bonus (+ Str, Agi)
- ½ weight
- Hit Points (+3, +7, + 14)

_Enchanted effect is guaranteed upon applying the enchant scroll._

- Skill Bonus (+ Stealth, Initiative, Arcana)
- Abilities (Cast Static Veil, 2 times per day)
- Elemental Resistance (Res: Acid 3)
- Advantage/Flat Bonus vs Spells or Effects

Common items can only have one MasterCrafted and one Enchanted bonus.

### Weapons
**Proficiency:** Proficiency with a weapon allows for the full extent of that weapon capabilities adding the relevant modifiers to the attack roll and damage roll.

- **To Hit:** *D20 + Ability Modifier + Mastery + Other Bonuses (Class Features, Abilities, Weapon Enchants/MasterCrafted)*
- **Critical Strike:** When you _Critical Strike_ you double the **damage dice** and ignore any **Armor** (even natural armor) the creature has. *Resistances still apply.*

**_Example Stats for MasterCrafted Weapons_**
_MasterCrafted potency is based on the quality of the reagents and a Skill Checks when making the weapon._

- +1,+2 To hit and Damage
- Elemental Damage (+5 Fire damage, +1d4 Cold damage)
- ¼ weight

_Enchanted effect is guaranteed upon applying the enchant._

- Abilities/Spells (Cast Burning Flames, 2 times per day)
- Extra Damage vs Creature Type (+5 weapon damage or +1d10 weapon damage) (Slayer)

Common items can only have one MasterCrafted and one Enchanted bonus.
