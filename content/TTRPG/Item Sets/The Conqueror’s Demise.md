**Set (2):** *Resistance: Physical 5*
**Set (3):** *+3 to Fortitude Saving Throws*
**Set (4):** 
- *+2 to Strength & Agility Scores*
- You change your creature type to *Undead* & *Fiend* and you do not age. 
- The *Hit Point* bonus from [[Girdle of Eternal Reign]] is tripled. 
- [[Hand of Oblivion]] can now be used once per Turn.
- When you use [[Nightfall#^fb277a|Shatter]] you are also healed by the necrotic damage done by the ability.

Upon completing the set, the curse takes effect in 7 days. 

==Curse of the Conqueror==
Your alignment changes to *Lawful Evil.*
You strive to carve an empire in your wake. Each creature you kill comes back as a whitier husk. Your sole purpose is the kill all the 7 Magi in the world, crowning yourself upon their bodies as the Eternal Ruler of Mareth'Sol as [[Kahn-Rokh the Conqueror]] intended. 

**Items:** [[Hollow Radiance]], [[Nightfall]], [[Girdle of Eternal Reign]], [[Hand of Oblivion]]
