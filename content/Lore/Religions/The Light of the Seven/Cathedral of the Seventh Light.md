The cathedral stands as a towering monument to unyielding, blinding faith. 
Built from monolithic blocks of pristine, reflective white marble that capture every glint of the sun, its sheer, windowless walls stretch upward like a fortress. The roofs of its towers are painted golden. 
High above the heavy, brass-reinforced doors hangs a Grand Suncatcher—a massive, multifaceted crystal that catches the daylight, splintering it into sharp, piercing rays that can be seen for kilometers from outside the city.

Inside, the air is cold and devoid of shadows. Every surface is polished to a mirror-like sheen, reflecting the glow of seven colossal, burning braziers that line the central nave. Echoes of chants reverberate through its halls as litanies and rituals never stop. 

The Archluminar resides on the top of the highest tower.
