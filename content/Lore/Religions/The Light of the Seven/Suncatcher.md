The **Suncatcher** is the most common religious symbol used by the masses within the [[Azak Empire]]. 
When left in sunlight it retains residual light. They usually take the form of a necklace in a triangle shape with a circle overlapped, and a small stone weaved in the middle. Most houses have them hung in windows or doorways for protection.
