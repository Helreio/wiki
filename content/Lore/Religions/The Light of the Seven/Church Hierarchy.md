**Acolyte:** Entry-level initiates and assistants within the church.

**Warden:** Ordinary clergy members responsible for administering to the spiritual needs of citizens.

**Wordbearer:** A clergy member who protects a church in an area eg. village, town.
**Lightkeeper:** Individuals who deliver sermons and spread faith among the populace.

**Seer:** Envoyat working for the church for information gathering and precognition. 

**Līhteran:** Members of the clergy specialized in navigating through [[The Veiled Weft]]. 
_Creed:_ “Līht þæt wæġ mid þínum fȳre.” - Light the way with your fire. ^4e190d

**Strixbane:** The one who deals with rogue thaumaturges or strix. 

**Daughter of Ash (Áscynn Dohtoras):** Around an Abyss rent, the sky bleeds red and ash falls. 
Whatever it touches decays, iron corrodes, timber rots, cloth frays like cobweb, and flesh grows old before its time. Strength leeches from bone; life thins to a brittle thread, and then snaps. Only a rare few, always women, endure the blight. Their hair pales to white upon first exposure, a mark of the burden they carry.

**Luminary Elect:** High-ranking officials who oversee large regions of the Empire and have significant influence within the Church.

**Lightbearer:** Exceptional individuals who are venerated for their extraordinary acts of faith and devotion. ^f4580f

**Confessor:** Ecclesiastical judges who enforce the religious laws of the church and often serve as advisors to higher-ranking members.

**Archluminar:** The highest-ranking official of the Church, serving as the spiritual leader of the entire Empire.
