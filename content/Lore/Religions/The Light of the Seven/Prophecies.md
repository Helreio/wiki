##### Transcript of Brother Oriandros, one of the First seers born in the Third Age
 
*“The sleeping sun weeps of sorrow,* 
*Though his children will not see the morrow.* 
*Crimson will be the lake,* 
*as the chain will break.*
*Death and decay in his wake,*
*A human form shall take.* 
*Faired skin boy will draw* 
*A scarlet blade from the maw.*
*The last of twelve kin,* 
*Vengeance within.”*
