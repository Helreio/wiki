**The Sovereign of Dunes** is venerated by the pessek people as the god hidden in [[The Great Dunes of Al-Kazim]]. 
Large statues made of sand animate to protect his loyal servants.
The Sovereign resides in **[[Armit-Han]], the First Hearth**.

Pouring precious, clean water over the feet of a sand statue is the highest form of devotion. The sand absorbs the water instantly, darkening the statue's base. The people believe this nourishes his connection to the settlement.

Culturally, children and travelers gather the finest, purest white sand from dangerous, deep desert dunes and lay it at the statue's plinth. 
During times of need, the statue is offered blood by the faithful. As part of the offering a small prayer in [[Pessekian|pessekian]] is spoken. 

*"From water He draws strength,
Praise be His Hearth. 
In sand He binds our names,
O Al-Kazim, 
May Your dunes ever shift."*
