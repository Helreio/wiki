### The First Sphere (Rulers)

The Heavenly Hosts are seven beings created from the breath of the Allfather within [[The High Heavens]]. 

- **Kalydra et’Leukos, The Firstborn.** Under the sign on the Sun he governs _The Heavenly Council_ from his Ruby Throne. _Principality of Light._ 
- **Savr'ya the Iron-bound, The First Daughter.** Born under the Celestial Bodies and their movement through the Endless Sea. _Principality of Chains._
- **Rak’ul,** stands in argent fire with eyes that suffer no falsehood. He is the scale, the unseen blade, and the final measure of mortal and divine alike. _Emanation of Judgment_ 
- **Het’nor,** _Emanation of Wisdom,_ speaks in quiet truths, seeing all paths yet choosing none, for wisdom knows the cost of every step.  
- **Halr’od.** Forged in the first sundering of stars, he bears the crown of ruin. He is the fire that cleanses, the silence that follows thunder, the unmaking that makes way for dawn. _Principality of Destruction._ 
- **Svar’bog,** turns the eternal hourglass; through him all things rise, fall, and are counted in their appointed hour. _Emanation of Reckoning (time)._
- **Min’ra, The Lastborn.** Born under the sign of the Moon, embodying the life and death of _The Golden Cycle_. The _Emanation of Perpetuity,_ comes in three different guises, a young virgin **Isil**, the mother **Rána** and **Effrie** the Old Crone.

### **The Second Sphere** (Soldiers)

- **Ethyr of Min’thär,** Wardthane of the Endless Heaven. 
- **[[Sunsworn of the Ruby Throne]]**, royal guards protecting [[Ealorwyrd|Ealorwyrd, The Ever-Fated Light]], where Kalydra resides. 
**[[The Lightbound Legions]]**, is the army fighting against the Hollowed Lords.

---
### **The Third Sphere** (Messengers)

- **Dawn Heralds** are radiant beings tasked to oversee [[Mareth'Sol; The Physical World]]. 
- **Minerva; Herald of Kalydra** guided the Archluminar (Head of the Church) in the First Founding, during the end of the Second Age. Her chosen champion **Hroth, The Duskbreaker** fought against [[The Abyss]].
