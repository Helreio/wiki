The Yfelkynn, or Evil Kin within the [[Azak Empire]], are an ancient race of giants, their massive forms shaped by the unforgiving wilderness they call home. 
Though feared by many, they are neither mindless nor cruel—they are survivors, bound by tradition and deep traditions. The most commonly seen are slaves serving the Azak nobility.
