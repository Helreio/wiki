Creatures sired by the [[Leshari; Wild Gods|Leshari]] Wild Gods ~4000 years ago.

Are a race of humanoids living in mostly nomad tribes throughout the world. Most of their religious practices revolve around using the land to the fullest and keeping the balance and the spirit of their Sire. 
They are recognized by features they inherit from their Sire.
