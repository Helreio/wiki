In the Third Age, the **Age of Twilight** (Mareth'Sol:/[[Old Azakian]]), Vel’myr, the magic of old, has grown scarce. Only a handful of children are born every 100 years with a true connection to the [[Crucible]]. 
Their blood is humming with ancient power, and they are called [[Kindling|Kindled or Kindling]]. Of these, the strongest are chosen to become apprentices of a **Magus**. They are taken from their families to spend their lives in service and study beside their master, learning the forgotten arts that the world itself seems to have almost forgotten.
