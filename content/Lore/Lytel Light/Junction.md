The area where two [[Cimmerian Conduits]] intersect is called a **Junction**. 
Sometimes the [[Lytel Light|lytel]] around the junction is altered; either the magic is stronger if cast or it has different effects based on the type of lytel school. 

Any Junction can be used by a [[Church Hierarchy#^4e190d|Līhteran]] to pass in or out of [[The Veiled Weft]].
