**Lytel** is the gift from the _Allfather_ as His essence created the universe. Most creatures have it in tiny amounts. Within the population only 10% have an affinity, granting them innate magic from birth, [[Cinders]]. Around 1-2% have a higher affinity, [[Kindling|Kindling or Kindled]] with the potential to become apprentices under a Magus and learn [[Vel'myr]], the old magic.
