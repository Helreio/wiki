**Channelers** are able to cast *[[Blessings Spell List|Blessings]]*, *[[Incantation Spell List|Incantations]]* and manage sacred rituals. 

[[Warden|Wardens]] are initiated with holy sigils, embedding them into their skin during the initiation ritual. These practices are only performed within the [[Church of the Seventh Light]], granting them the ability to _channel_ the powers of *[[The Heavenly Hosts]].*

As protection against unholy creatures a thurible is filled with powered silver and [[Consumables#^ba3ee5|Twilight Soot]], and burned.

The [[Suncatcher]] is the holy symbols given form, most commonly used by the masses for prayer and holy service.
