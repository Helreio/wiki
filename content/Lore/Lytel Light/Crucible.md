The area where three or more [[Cimmerian Conduits]] intersect is called a **Crucible**.  

The remaining crucibles from the [[Second Age]] are atop of mountains guarded by a Magus. 
They are [[The Seven Pale Stars]] which guide the [[Church Hierarchy#^4e190d|Līhterans]] through [[The Veiled Weft]].
