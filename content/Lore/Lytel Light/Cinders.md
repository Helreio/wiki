**Cinders** (Sinder:/[[Old Azakian]]) are humanoids who are magically inclined, a quart [[Kindling|kindled]]. They are used by the military as stock for the first generation of [[Thaumaturge|Thaumaturges]]. 

Dross filth is a popular term used derogatory towards anyone who is part of the military or has implants.
