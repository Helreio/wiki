**Lytel Lode** is a metal stud or rivet implanted in your body, usually in your forearm bones, that enhances the ability to draw upon the magic of the land. Different types of alloys grant access to different types of magic schools. As you use magic past your capacity you will burn yourself out, degrading your body and mind. This affliction is called by the masses **Scorched**, the nobles call it **Effulgence Ebb.**

Unsanctioned use of implants is illegal, their manufacturing is strictly controlled by the Azak noble houses, each Master Maker only produces implants for the emperor's military. Although some black market knock offs are bound to appear from time to time. 

The users of implants are called **dross filth** as a derogatory term by the faithful of [[Church of the Seventh Light]], as it is seen as impure to desecrate the body’s Light.

**The Lodes** are alloys of *[[Krytth]]* \[Kr] with different metals like gold \[Au], copper \[Cu], Zinc \[Zn] etc. *[[Summoning Spell List|Summoning]]* and *[[Necromancy Spell List|Necromancy]]* are frowned upon within the empire and are punishable by death if you are caught with lodes of _netharum_ and _thanium_.  

[[Cinders]] with implants became the first generation of *[[Thaumaturge|Thaumaturges]]*.
