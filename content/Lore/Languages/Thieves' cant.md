It's a secret mix of dialect, jargon, and code that allows hidden messages in seemingly normal conversation. 

Secret signs and symbols are used to convey short, simple messages, such as whether an area is dangerous or the territory of a thieves' guild, whether the people in an area are easy marks or will provide a safe house for thieves on the run.
