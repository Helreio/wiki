The **Veiled Weft** is a transient dimension, in constant change, that connects the planes together. It has broken parts and pieces from all the planes melded together, ever shifting.

Traveling through the Weft will corrupt the soul and lead travelers astray, forever lost.  Only trained guides (Līhterans) from the church are able to navigate by following [[The Seven Pale Stars]], seven fires on the eternal sky.  

The journey through the Weft takes a seventh of the time it would take in the physical realm.
