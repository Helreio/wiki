#### Absolution (Ablusyn) 
“To be made white in fire.” A rite of cleansing where sin or taint is burnt away through divine flame or suffering. Commonly invoked before battle or burial. ^9e194e
#### Ashen (A’shenn) 

^553dd5

“Touched by ruin.” Refers to one who has survived the sacred flame or the rot of divinity; pale of skin, hollow of gaze.
#### Anointed (Anoitair)
“Marked by purpose.” Said of those chosen for divine trial. The mark may burn, bleed, or whisper under moonlight.
#### Atrament (At’rem)  
“Ink of memory.” A sacred fluid used to write pacts or record sin; it cannot be washed away by mortal means.
#### Benediction (Benedykt)
“A word given as light.” A holy utterance believed to carry the will of gods, used to sanctify, bind, or break curses.
#### Blightborne (Blytharn)
“Flesh that remembers decay.” Name for the diseased pilgrims who wander wastelands, their faith measured in endurance.
#### Covenant (Kovennat)
“The binding made unseen.” An oath between mortal and divine; the sigil or relic that seals it is
often hidden under flesh or bone.
#### Crimsoned (Crimsair)
“Baptized in blood.” Used of knights and penitents who have shed sacred blood in sacrifice or
cleansing.
#### Caelmir
“Sky hollow.” An ancient word for divine absence; the space where gods once stood and watched.
#### Divine Quiet (Quies Divir)
“When the gods turn away.” The absence of guidance; a silence heavy enough to drive men to faith or madness.
#### Dross (Drosh)
“The unrefined.” Those deemed impure or half-made — wielders of imperfect magic, or those fused with metal and wire.
#### Eidolon (Eyd’lon)
“Echo made flesh.” A spirit shaped by memory or worship, not born but remembered into form.
#### Exalt (Exaltir)
“To lift through pain.” A state achieved when mortal suffering mirrors divine agony; priests claim such torment opens the soul’s gate.
#### Fellmark (Fel’marh)
“The stain that does not fade.” A wound, curse, or oath that clings beyond death; visible on the
spirit’s skin.
#### Feyward (Feiwair)
“Blessed by unseen hands.” Said of travelers who cross wild places and return unscathed; their luck is often distrusted.
#### Grace (Graisen)
“The unseen tether.” Believed to bind souls to their fate, like a candle’s flame to its wick. When
broken, the body withers, but the light wanders on.
#### Grievant (Grevyn)
“Bearer of divine sorrow.” A title for those who carry holy burden or guilt; marked by long silences and calloused palms.
#### Lightbearer (Lyght’barr)  
“One who carries flame unburned.” Anointed paladin or saint; their burden shines brightest before their fall.
