An imposing shadow looms over the village square. The Laughing Manticore inn is almost empty on stormy nights. Only Bertram the Innkeeper, John the Liveryman and his new acquaintance can be heard chatting. 

"Another round Bertram! Dark ale this time for my new acquaintance here." John proclaimed with a smile flashing his bucked teeth. “What brings you this far in the countryside? Any interest in Odenwill or just passing through? " curiosity packing every word.  

"Just wondered upon [[Odenwill]]. I am traveling North, towards the capital." the old man replied, in a mellow tone locking eyes with the youth. A few seconds pass and John continues.

"Oh, it must be hard traveling.. alone” The word failed to leave his lips as his eyes fell upon the cold iron inlay of the sword. There a jagged pattern of winds and twists split the steel in half with symbols known only by the Master Maker.  

With a gulp he continues. “Sometimes I forget there are villages other than this one. I wish I could travel, but my mother has fallen ill as of late…” Another gulp. “Aren't you afraid of being mugged? That sword looks like it costs a fortune". He shifts his weight on the chair in an awkward manner. Bertam remains composed and raises an eyebrow at the blurted words, also curious to hear the answer.

The elderly man grumbles with a dead tired voice. "This sword.. was a gift from someone dear to me… If someone may try to take it, then it shall be from my own dead hands…" 

Voice trembling. “Ah.. a wonderful gift… I see?” Exclaimed John with a fearful pale face. ”Lets drink to it!” Grabs the mug, shaking, part of it spills on the counter as he lifts it up.  

Seeing this the man continues with a grave tone. "In the world there are things more dangerous than highwaymen. This weapon is made to be used against creatures that are not… human.” As he speaks a locket can be seen hanging around his neck, marked with a distinct symbol. The engraving depicts a gold hand on a white marble encompassed by a sun. Looks similar to The Morning Lord’s motif, Bertram thought as he quickly glanced at the weary traveler.

With a heartily demeanor and a booming voice “Fear not, son! My mere presence keeps them at bay.” A mischievous grin followed by thunder clears the heavy air. 

Relieved, John forces a smile hoping for a more pleasant subject than old fairy tales read to him in church. 

"Have you heard or seen anything unusual lately?" The burly man inquiries looking at the innkeeper. 

John sighs and scratches his beard thinking. 

"Some of the graves have been dug up, the remains have gone missing." Bertram spoke while pulling up a chair to join them. "That old woman, Wilma, saw a few nights ago somebody lurking, watching her through the bedroom window. When she yelled, it dropped on all fours and ran away." 

John chimes in. "I heard Alan The Huntsman went for some game on his usual trail. He found a dead stag with weird claw marks on the body and only the heart was missing. No wild animal would just leave the prey there, I reckon.`
