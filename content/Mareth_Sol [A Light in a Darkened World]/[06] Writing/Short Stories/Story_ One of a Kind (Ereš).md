I remember the muffled sounds of gravel and earth being dug woke me from my eternal slumber. My chest felt lighter with each passing moment. A dark emptiness kept me company but now a small light protruded from above. The smell of unearthed soil flooded my nose. A hooded figure stood, a lantern at the edge of the grave, mumbling words into my mind, beckoning me. 

Another came into view, holding a shovel. His lifeless eyes with no will of their own, moves as he was given a command. I recognised his emancipated form, the one who always searched for meaning in liquor bottles in between beatings of his wife. Resentment and agony writhed inside my chest like a battle between the elemental lords Artan and Nen. I screamed at my body to move, no words came out. I wanted to entangle my bony hands around his neck and end him a second time but alas only my fingers twitched with the pain of snapping branches.

“Ahh.. a fine specimen, I shall make good use of you” said the sorcerer barely moving his cracked lips into a smile. With a flick of the wrist I was engulfed by darkness.

Later I found myself in a house, my body moving on its own obeying the one who had awoken me. In my right hand I was holding a staff, the wood twisted at the end into a scrawny hand. There, a small red bead stood, nestled between the twisted fingers. A few bodies lay sprawled at my feet, simple peasants, a family undeserving of this sudden and gruesome end. 

Their blood was flowing crimson, the stone pulsating like a heartbeat, feeding on their lytel. My master tediously tore their bodies apart searching for something. He was not pleased, wincing in frustration when he heard commotion outside. A mob, roaming through the village, no doubt of their intentions. 

Did he think they wouldn't notice the peril in their village?

The sorcerer's voice overpowered my thoughts, It moved me in front of the door as it was flung open. The first in was a fairly young farmer holding a pitchfork. As he quickly pierced my chest, the staff touched his shoulder, his form dissected and withered fell to the ground in a sickening crunch. The others quickly dispersed at the grizzly sight before them. Only one stood firm in his conviction. A warden of Kalydra. Upon uttering holy wards I felt the radiant fire embracing my form, soon turning me to bones and dust. 

Before finishing the incantation the other thrall jumped through the window, rolling fast to the side snatching the crosier while putting a dagger in the warden’s neck. My body, extinguished, lay burnt on the floor. My mind cleared, my own again. 

The sorcerer stood above me with stern eyes. Contemplating. Motioned to the thrall to come back. He kneeled down, removed the pitchfork and was about to rip open my chest. 

“You shouldn't do that” a whisper of a voice came through sobs. A child with red cheeks came out of the cabinet. The sorcerer stopped for a second, then the thrall darted towards the boy. I struggled commanding my body into submission and it finally moved according to my will. I grabbed the staff and swung with all my force, breaking it against the body of the sorcerer, destroying its torso. The stone found its new meal, draining his essence. The sorcery controlling the thrall broke leaving it crumpled on the ground. I weakly stood and hobbled towards the scared child. On the way I grabbed a knife and pierced through the thrall’s spine.

Goodbye Father.

“You are safe now” I said. My unused raspy voice did not scare him. The boy watched my every move. “What is your name?” i continued. 

“Eres…”

“Now Eres, go to the chapel and ..“ the words failed in my throat, I felt my body decaying rapidly as the magic was dissipating. I was slipping back to the empty void where I slumbered. The child touched my burned face, a deep golden warmth poured into me. 

This is as far as i go, Min’ra guide me to the Endless Heavens. 

“No..” whispered the boy. As I opened my eyes, he was cradling the stone in his grimy palm, a single red tear flowing down his cheek.

“Thank you for reminding me of my purpose.” where the last words I heard as tremendous pain surged through my soul, dragging me back from the Crone’s clutches.  

A soft rustling of leaves woke me. As I came to my senses I realized I was standing in the shadow of a great oak. My body felt warm, alive again. The dull pain inside my chest, a stark reminder that it was not a dream. 

In my new life as a deathless, I find myself Commander of the Deathguard, serving my new Master in his crusade.
