
### **White’s Operation** 

‘No… Stay away!’ growled [[Ghinn Blutaxt|White]] through clenched teeth, as vicious eyes hover above him. The tall lanky man with an arrogant smile takes a seat close to him…again. 

‘Ah, welcome back, 795. Rejoice, soon our paths will part, as the ritual will be concluded.” [[Emmeret]] licks his lips in anticipation and chooses with glee a fresh Netharum lode and an emerald quill from his tool collection.

‘Don't worry, the others have their role to play.’ A joyful thought twists his face in an unnatural manner.

‘No…’ croaks White as he pulls against the restraints. The only thing he sees next is the blue hue around the doctor's instrument, cutting into his flesh. Pain is surging through his body, bones feeling like they are about to break from the convulsions. Between each cut he gets a little respite, like breaths of fresh air of a drowning man. He closes his eyes tightly for a moment, bracing himself for the next wave of pain. The skin shrivels near the glowing hot tip, which hangs with a faint buzz in the air, hungrily waiting to engrave the next mark. As it tears into the flesh, a myriad of colours flash in White's mind, swirling and contorting in impossible shapes. A wheeze escapes through cracked lips as the pain overtakes him.

For a moment, everything becomes lightless and still. Again, he finds himself on a body of murky water, stretching towards the horizon under a starless sky. All the earthly feelings are left behind. Looking down at his feet, he takes notice. Only his reflection in the shallow water betrays the space around him. An imposing black mass, made of coiled tendrils, swirls forming a sphere which emanates a pulsating dull light. 

 A sharp pain tugs at his side, pulling him back to reality. The grimy stone is caked with brackish blood. The disturbing smile of the doctor looms over him. 

‘Now, now, I need you to be conscious…it would ruin our fun if you were to faint again.’ The uttered words echo within the cold room while his bony hands work fast and precise, carving magical symbols deep within the canvas of alabaster flesh. Writhing with agony, White’s eyes roll back plunging him into darkness. Within the bleakness, his heart surges with the rhythm of anger and wrath, unquenching, unrelenting, overflowing, as it starts coursing through his veins. 

He forces himself to open his eyes although the pain is unbearable. He stares intently at the man through ragged breaths. Dislocating a finger on his left hand, the restraints slip away. With a guttural howl he snatches a surgical knife attempting to implant it into the neck of his tormentor. Alas, it only grazes the doctor’s jaw, as Emmeret is taken aback, quickly disengaging to a safe distance. ‘You worm!!..’ 

"Things like you will not stand in the way of greatness!”. He pauses for a moment, lightly touching the cut. ‘No matter, I am done with you.’ 

A sardonic smile creeps on the boy's face, as his eyes open wide, burning with spite. Suddenly, he jabs his throat, dragging the instrument to the side, slicing it open. Blood is oozing from the wound while gurgled laughs escape the White’s crooked mouth. 

Emmeret freezes for a moment, then quickly regains his composure and turns towards the door yelling at the henchman flanking the entryway. ‘Bring specimen 954. Quickly!’ Turning to the boy he snorts “Having fun, are we?” Coughing blood, his grin gets wider. 

In a short time the henchman rushes back with [[Scarlett]] over his broad shoulder. A mop of crimson hair swaying in a frenzy in her futile attempts of resistance. She is thrown on the cold stone floor in front of the operation table. Pulling herself up, soft gurgles reach her. With wide eyes she notices White’s cut throat, he's bleeding to death with a satisfied smile plastered on. 

Scarlett quickly jumps into action, with both hands over the gashing wound. “He finally pushed you too far… Hang in there.” One of her hands glided over the lodestones while the other rested over the wound. His smile faded as he saw her worried face. A red hue engulfed her fingers, small drops of blood congealed onto strings. Slowly, threading the gash close, pulling the skin together. When the skin connected back, runes started to glow from White's body steam coming off, burning his lytel forcefully to accelerate the healing. In a few moments the stones were dull with light. 

The burley disfigured man approached Scarlett at the signal from Emmeret. His hands started to shake and muscles tensed as he was getting within 4 feet of Scarlett. she cocked her head to the side with a sardonic smile still clutching the stone. 

“What is it? Difficulty walking? You should eat more vegetables. “ As her stare brought a wave of magic on the man. Dropping to one knee. Trickles of blood pouring from his eyes.

A small shadow merged from a crack in the floor, it crawled on Scarlett's foot, breaking her focus. 

“No, not again... Fucking rats!! Why aren't you obeying?!!” She shrieked in distress while stomping the cold and damp floor. In her confusion a meaty hand grabbed her neck, slowly squeezing the life out of her. In a calm demeanour Emmeret grabbed a syringe from his front pocket. The needle was quickly plunged into her arm, after a few moments she became limp. 

A surge of fury, again his dull orange eyes radiate with intensity. Another syringe was thrusted in his leg. Light was fading fast, he only managed to catch some words “[send word to] prepare the ritual. We [go] forward [with the plan].” before the warm embrace of the lake.

### **White’s Rescue**

Thunk… the sound of something slamming against the heavy wooden door while a faint smell of burning oil permeated the damp and musty room. Through straining eyes, White saw a flickering light dispelling the darkness. Shadows danced on the grey bricks projected from outside through rusted iron bars.

“How dare you! Aghh… “ A wet squelch, cracking bones accompany the scuffle outside. Dark green, familiar eyes peered inside through the hatch. “Sable?..” White spat the word through parched lips. How is he here? 

“Here.” grumbled [[Sable]], muffled by the door. 

“Open the lock or would you like to have a happy little accident like him ?” conveyed Scarlett in a sarcastic voice. A soft jingle followed by a click, the door creaked open flooding the room with yellow light. Three figures entered, first was Scarlett with her wild red hair now wet and plastered to her head, her weary eyes fell on the metal bed restraining the albino. Second was Sable, his dark complexion making him hard to spot if not for him caring the lantern, only his emerald green eyes reflection the light   

“Watch her, if she dares to try anything, make her suffer.” The words sent chills in the devotee's spine. Sable nodded in agreement, keeping his eyes on her. 

Scarlett made quick work of the leather restraints, cutting them carefully to not harm White. His body still moves with a certain sluggishness, the drugs given to him made sure he remained subdued. 

“Are you ok?” White spoke with a weak voice, glancing over the bruises on her neck. “Where are the others?” he inquired, remembering the faces of the other nine children in this god forsaken orphanage. 

“They were taken before I could escape. It took me a bit to cleanse my blood from the drugs and draw enough lytel to use on the guards. We need to get out of here, now.” 

A shadow moved in White’s peripheral vision followed by a gasp of air, a metallic odour pricked at White’s nose. Scarlett quickly turned around to see Sable with an unhinged grin as he pulled out a dagger from the woman's chest. Her body slumped in a heap on the floor. “She moved. I couldn't risk it.” Sable spoke while cocking his head to one side with a curious frown, looking at the mess he made. The pooling blood seeped the fabric of her yellow robe. White's eyes caught the symbol on her sleeve, a sun dripping. The symbol had been forever burned into his mind, reminding him of the Doctor. 

“There's nothing to worry about now.” Sable broke the silence snapping them both out of their stupor. “Yes, you take White and I'll take the lantern.” replied Scarlett while shifting her gaze to Sable then White. White was 10 and Sable 12 meanwhile she was 13 yr old, the eldest. But in this moment at the dim light of the lantern she felt younger, less broken than them. 

They travelled at a brisk pace through the winding narrow corridors, Scarlett in the front while Sable was carrying White on his back. 

On reaching the doorstep leading above ground they stopped confused at the scene before them. A thick milky mist crept through the streets, clinging to the ground like a ghostly shroud. “There is no sunlight.” Scarlett remarked in a low whisper. “It should be around noon.” an inquisitive frown deepened her expression. 

“Damn.. They're onto us. It's too late to escape. ” babbled Sable frantically. 

“Shush, stupid!” snapped Scarlett, straining her eyes and ears to scan the area. She noticed a faint lantern light piercing the haze, unwinding each coil in its path. “What are you doing here?”  A ragged voice had broken the silence. 

A knock reverberated through the massive oaken doors. “Father John, It’s Robert, open up.” After a deep inhale he continued in a stern voice. “First he created Light from His Radiant eye to balance the Darkness in His other. From His blood, The Astral Sea took form as a binder to hold everything together.”

### **Escape from the Church**

After everything was set in place the old [[Ray Willcok|Inquisitor]] signalled to Pastor John to head up the stairs. Ringing the bell was paramount for their escape. Scarlett had been given a few holy water vials and a table leg. Sable was wielding the dagger snatched from the orphanage, while White carried a brass candle holder. That only left Robert to guard the rear with three bottles of lamp oil corked with rags. 

The sturdy oak doors shifted with a mournful whine under the effort to pry them open. Wisps of mist coiled at the threshold, not daring to intrude inside while the torchlight illuminated no more than a few metres. The deafening silence was unsettling. 

“Stick to the plan as I told you. Stay close and follow me.” The Inquisitor reminded them in a miffed tone. With one hand on his staff, he mumbled a few sacred words into the milky white sphere. After a brief second the sphere started glowing with a golden hue, penetrating the mist further than any torch. As they moved silently forward, a cold chill came upon them. 

Pulling on Scarlett’s gown, White whispered towards her. “I think we are being followed. Listen to the footsteps. There are more than there should be.” White was straining his ears, finding a peculiar shuffle of dragging feet coming from the right near the abandoned cart; he counted around four sets of footsteps. Scarlett’s crimson eyes locked forward, on the Inquisitor's light, then glanced briefly to White. With a hushed voice she replied “Stand behind me and run when I tell you.” 

Sable’s face dropped upon hearing this, “They're here… It’s a trap. They're here!!” 

“Dammit! Slave boy! Keep your mouth shut!” The Inquisitor hissed. “ You will get us all killed if you ….”Something flew through the air, a man-shaped shadow, crashing into the Inquisitor. The sphere rolled forward, now illuminating the grotesque figure of a man with the head of a pig, skin stretched awkwardly to one side on his skull, struggling to rip the old fool's neck with its teeth, keeping him pinned on the ground. As his lanky arms were pulling on the staff, White spurred into action, adrenaline filling his system, he dashed towards the creature raising the brass candle holder. First hit connected with a thunk, a few droplets of brackish blood spurted from the wound onto his face. A scornful grin creeped on White’s thin lips as he struck again, delivering a wrathful clobbering. Finally releasing all the hatred bottled up. The creature, dazed by the relentless attacks, wavered backwards. The distraction created was enough, with a quick motion the inquisitor managed to point two fingers towards the creature while uttering holy words. A faint light gathered in his hand and a small bead of white light shot forth, hitting the chest, destroying a fist size chunk from the creature's torso. Stumbling backwards it let out an ear-splitting squeal, White dropped his now bent makeshift weapon, covering his ears he dropped to the ground.

With dagger in hand Sable sprang forth and jumped on the man-pig’s back, stabbing furiously to finish it off, managing to pierce one of the eyes before being flung several meters through the air landing with a thud and a yelp near the courtyard’s well. 

Seeing this Scarlett threw one vial towards the creature bathing it in holy water. A hideous gurgled laughter was produced within a thick throat, half human half animal. With a weary hand on his staff, the old man heaved himself up, with two fingers up, he gazed at the creature, words escaping his lips. 

“I call upon Rak’ul, from the pure to the divine. Judge this creature before thy, send it to its rightful place.”  White flames sparked at the creatures feet, amplified by the holy water, it was instantly consumed, only the charred body remained crumpled on the cobblestone. 

“Stand back foul demons!!” yelled Robert as he threw one bottle into the dense mist. Upon contact, a wreath of flames engulfed the area to the right with a fuzzy glow, with it two creatures had their outlines shown by the light. 

“Where are you Sable?!” yelled Robert concerned, trying to keep his eyes focused where the creatures watched the group. A painful moan up ahead, past the light into the dark mist. “Sable?” spoke White moving towards the sound. A hand attempted to grab him by the collar, as he passed the old inquisitor. With a move almost like water he avoided his grasp. “Spoiled brats, they never obey their elders.” mumbled the old man. White ignored the disgruntled words listening for his friend up ahead. Crunch, the sounds of bone cracking followed by a thump, silenced the moans. He moved closer and saw the outline of a broken heap on the ground, twitching. “Damm mimicry, it copied my voice.” said Sable with disdain, as he came into the light of the sphere. A viscous dark liquid soaked his torn clothes. White took notice of his hardened skin, of the scrapes and cuts on his face, hands and forearms.  

“Aghh..” a croak from the back. White’s eyes darted backwards to see a claw piercing Robert’s chest through. He was still grasping two more bottles. With a weary smile, a thin line of blood at the corner of his mouth he grunted, “Run.” as he dropped the remaining bottles at his feet, lighting himself on fire alongside the creature. Shrills filled the misty air, a whirling bead of light flew to end the burning abomination, blowing the creatures head to chunks. White felt an overwhelming sorrow for the young man as his last defiant smile against death remained imprinted in his mind, everything felt lost, one of the few who showed him kindness was taken like the others..  His stupor was interrupted by two finger length rods, which fell on the ground from the creature, a mix of two immiscible metals, one shown with a golden colour while the other absorbed the light. White took notice of a faint blue aura dissipating into the surrounding mist. The others fled, no creatures left in sight. 

The group, now together, was slowly making their way across the square towards the northern street, towards the gate and with luck the inquisitors' carriage. The fires were extinguished quickly by the thick damp mist, the only light remaining was the sphere held by the old man.

A whisper came from all around, “Give me the children … Priest. My master demands it.” A pitter patter sound of multiple long legs across the stone punctuates every word.

“You wretched creature. You are an abomination! Scaring the earth you walk upon. These children belong to the church!“ cried out the old man with religious zeal while searching the area for the position of the enemy.  White’s nose was flaring, picking up a subtle scent of a filth combined with a wet dog as they got closer to the centre of the square. 

A cackle inverwinded by clicking sounds sent chills down their spines. Everybody’s attention was drawn towards the church’s direction. Six black eyes glinted at the edge of the light, embedded into a woman’s head on top of a spider-like body the size of a horse. Six rods protruded from her head, three on each temple, made from the same weird metal.

Sable’s body trembled at the grotesque sight slowly backing away, his eyes seeking Scarlett. Her hand caught his while speaking above a whisper “Don't be a fool we cannot outrun this thing, we better fight.” her nails digging into Sable with a strained expression. 

“Come children, your presence is expected.” said the woman while winding a sticky silk into a ball with two of her eight legs.  

With no time to waste the Inquisitor yelled out with a booming voice “Now John!!”. A deep rumbling sound vibrates the air, startling the creature. Raising her body and with a twisting motion, her head was at an impossible angle towards the church’s tower hissing. 

Sable darted to the left slinking through the mist beyond the light, attempting to flank the exposed belly of the spider. Breaking through the mist he realised the creature was prepared, throwing the ball of silk at his feet, he stumbled plunging head first on the grimy damp stones, the dagger slipping from his hand, while the sticky substance was restraining his movement. One of her raised legs, in a blur of motion dislodging the mist, found his right shoulder, impaling him to the ground. Not even hardening his skin was enough to deflect the blow. Meanwhile White leaped for the dagger, grabbing it with one hand. The next second he plunged into a slide and managed to dodge one of the spider legs coming towards him,arching his back he glided on the slippery surface under the creature's hairy abdomen at the same time tearing a huge gash into it. Scarlett threw her last vial when a man’s scream startled her, missing the creature. “Father John” she murmured. A ghastly howl replaces the waning bell tools.   

The Inquisitor finished reciting the holy words, a sphere of light grew the size of a human head at the end of its coiled staff. Thrusting towards the enemy it shoots out tearing the mist apart with a whirring sound, blasting half of the spider like creature to dust. “Move! Now!!”.

White got quickly on his feet running towards Sable, “Are you ok?” spoke in a weary voice. “Stay still.” With a quick jerking motion White pulled out the leg. Sable grunted in pain, with the other hand clutching his wound. “Thank you.” he replied. White smiled meekly pulling his friend onto his feet still at the edge of the light.

A wheeze of foul air passed them, almost knocking them back to the ground. In an instant the light was extinguished, the old Inquisitor grunted in pain as the hand holding the sphere was crushed. “Accursed moonborn.” said the old man through gritted teeth. “You shall never extinguish the light!” yelled while the jagged claws tore into him from the hip all the way to the collar bone spilling his guts. The moonborn released his grip tossing backwards the body over the edge of the well. 

Through the thick darkness and the heavy mist White couldn't see anything, only hear the raspy breath of Sable clinging to him. A waft of putrid rotting meat and wet fur instantly hit him, a second before an object forced its way into his guts flinging him a few metres back and rendering him unconscious…
