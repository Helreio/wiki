Rapport 19-307: Red Moon Incident

Inquisitor [[Ray Willcok]],
67 yr old 

These are excerpts found inside his journal, documenting Inquisitor Ray Willcok’s accounts. 

“After it had been brought to my attention the [REDACTED] village had been missing their tithes the past month. The concerns of Lord [REDACTED] about a cult spreading influence in the area had me on the road to investigate further. During travel I attempted contacting the High Inquisitor via epistle. The quill had an abnormal reaction, instead of writing a response, it pressed itself with a harsh motion on the parchment and broke in half. I have never encountered this peculiar reaction in all my years of service. 
Closing in on my destination a thick fog started to roll in on the road, nothing unusual, as the village is near a lake within a small valley flanked by mountains on three sides. Upon my arrival at the gate everything was still. Rancor permeated the air. It felt thick, almost suffocating. I had to use one of the Seals of Purity to dispel the oppressive energy around me. After telling the coachman to wait there until I returned, I made my way into the dreary street ahead towards the central square. Most of the houses appeared untouched from the outside, no trace of struggles or skirmishes. Something moved, I'm certain I saw something with the corner of my eye cresting the edge of the roof on my left, it looked like it slithered from one house to another. A foul smell of blood and viscera surged from the square. Confronted with this undead presence a shiver went down my spine and I hurried my pace. First I need to get to the temple to garner insight into this predicament and support. May Kalydra's light protect me.”

---

"I am safe for now within the temple. The warden, Joseph, keeps the people here safe burning the last reserves of twilight soot mixed with silver dust. For now it keeps the wicked creatures at bay and the temple sanctified. I fear this temporary solution will just prolong the agony and fear.
After his reluctance of opening the doors for me, he disclosed a few strange occurrences about what happened. A few days before the mist some of the villagers went missing in the night after hearing knocking at their door and voices from their loved ones asking for aid. Bob, a farmer, heard his boy yelling for help. The Light guided him while he grabbed the door handle as his own son whispered from behind with a troubled voice. *“Dad? Why are you at the window? Why should I come out?" Upon hearing that, Bob started to light the thurible with silver, grabbed the child and sought shelter in the attic. 
I understand now the hesitation to trust my credentials while I was seeking entrance. He  insisted that I should recite the First Order of Creation as proof. 
([[Making of the World (IO The Allfather)|“First he created Light from His Radiant eye to balance the Darkness in His other. From His blood, The Endless Sea took form as a binder to hold everything together.”]])
After a quick inspection, only one acolyte, one woman and three children from the orphanage are here besides warden Joseph. A few guardsmen, three villagers and Warden Oliver attempted to cross the mist taking one censer with them for protection. They never stood a chance. As they reached the end of the square, screams and death could be heard. Their light was extinguished by the evil forces at play. Perhaps I should have listened to Lightkeeper Almo and brought some of my personal guards.

---

Laura, one the assistants of the orphanage, took three children with her as the mist rolled in. She claims there wasn't enough time to save more. I have yet to find anything about the rest of the young ones . What intrigues me are the oddities of these children. The older boy appears to be twelve with a dark complexion, black hair, green eyes and a slave brand, which is, curiously, located behind the ear, possibly a pessekian stowaway abandoned here. This one shows in particular a lack of emotional control. His outbursts of fear and manic laughter are a sight to see. I heard the girl calling him Sable. She seems more like around 13 yr old, older than the other two. Her devilish red eyes and hair betray her nature. They call her Scarlet, a name fit for her dissident kind. The daggers in her eyes towards the caretaker overflow with hatred, I can only imagine the lack of discipline and manners she acquired in that orphanage. 
The last one makes my skin crawl, the little boy with a gnarly scar on his neck, called White. The gods and the parents have forsaken him since birth, cursing him with unholy alabaster skin and hair. Around ten years old, his eyes have an unusual color, a dull orange, they burn with fervor. He moves slowly like a beaten dog, sluggish, trying to seem inoffensive, but I won't be fooled. I will need to investigate further, but if it's what I presume, the Lord Inquisitor [REDACTED] might find him a purpose within the church.
I must end my thoughts here, I am being called by the warden, we need to formulate a plan of escape. The acolyte, Robert, found a luminous globe under the altar. We might use a blessing of light to secure safe passage. “

---

The last entry was written on a torn page soiled with blood and grime.

“I have failed in my duty. I lay broken at the bottom of this well. I fear the fight up above is still going on. The Lord Inquisitor needs to know. The children are the key. They must die so we can live. Our survival is at stake. May the Light save us.”
