**Interrogator:** Acolytes who have proven themselves and have been granted more authority and responsibility. They often lead missions and investigations under their supervision.

**Inquisitorial Hand:** Experienced operatives who work directly under the guidance of an Inquisitor. They have significant authority and autonomy in carrying out the will of their Inquisitor.

**Inquisitor:** The highest rank within the Inquisition. Inquisitors have nearly unlimited authority to investigate and eradicate threats to the Empire. They answer only to the Arch-Lector and operate with near-complete autonomy.

**Arch-Lector:** The leader of a major faction or organization within the Inquisition. They have immense authority and are among the most powerful individuals in the Empire.
