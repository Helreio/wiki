##### Transcript of Brother Benjamin, one of the First seers of the Third Age within the [[Church of the Seventh Light]]
 
“The sleeping sun weeps of sorrow, 
Though his children will not see the morrow. 
Crimson will be the lake, 
as the chain will break.
Death and decay in his wake,
A human form shall take. 
Faired skin boy will draw 
A scarlet blade from the maw.
The last of twelve kin, 
Vengeance within.”

##### Text found inside the *[[Lost Scrolls of Ta'nur]]*

“The scarlet Sun shall weep
For one of the twelve kin
While the pillar of thews will fetter
The last remaining Sin”

##### Envoyant's dream about [[Darrion Goddard]]'s journey from [[Azak Empire]] into [[The Veiled Weft]]

"The veiled gloom smothering the light, 
Heralds the fall of the Lightbearer knight. 
When light wanes and shadows slant still,
The ash shall rise to thwart thy will.
It shall rot thy hope and blind thy sight,
The veiled gloom smothering thy light.”
