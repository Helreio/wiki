**Channelers** are able to cast _Blessings_, _Incantations_ and manage sacred rituals. 

Wardens are initiated with holy sigils, embedding them into their skin during the initiation ritual. These practices are only performed within the **[[Church of the Seventh Light]]**, granting them the ability to _channel_ the powers of  *[[The High Heavens]] (The Divine Order).*

As protection against unholy creatures a thurible is filled with powered silver or [[Twilight Soot]], and burned. Silver offers concealment and Twilight Sooth repels.

The [[Suncatcher]] is the holy symbols given form, most commonly used by the masses for prayer and holy service.
