
Within the capital of the Azak Empire, **[[Arquen]]**, are academies where the lost arts of **[[Vel'myr]] (ancient magic)** is studied and refined for use by non **Kindled**. Each bears its own specialty. _Morezov, Rostevar_ and _Karven Houses_ are the main investors in the advancement of _lodes_. The most prestigious are the **[[Ta’vriek Arcanum]]**, focused on destructive power on the front lines, and the **[[Vak’t Halls]]**, specializing in control and obfuscation.
