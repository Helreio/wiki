**Lytel** is the gift from the _All Father_ as His essence created the universe. Most creatures have it in extremely small amounts. Within the humanoid population only 10% have an affinity, granting them innate magic from birth ([[Cinders]]). Around 1-2% have a higher affinity ([[Kindling (Kindled)|Kindling or Kindled]]) with potential to become apprentices and learn the old magic, [[Vel'myr]].
