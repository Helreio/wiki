**Appearance**: Humans are one of the most diverse races in terms of appearance. Their physical traits can vary significantly depending on their ancestry and environment.
**Age**: They reach adulthood in their late teens and live less than a century.
**Size**: They vary widely in height and build, from 150 - 210 centimetres tall. Your size is Medium.

- **[[Pessek Kalifate]]**:    
	**Desert Strider.** Your movement is not reduced by difficult terrain caused by sand, loose gravel, or dry terrain. When you take the Run action, you can move across vertical surfaces and difficult terrain without expending extra movement until the end of your turn. 
  
- **[[Azak Empire]]**:  
	**Imperial Discipline.** You gain a Mastery I (+2) in one of the following skills: Athletics, Intuition or Survival, if you already have mastery you increase the level. When rolling Initiative, you can add 1d4 to your roll.

[[Church of the Seventh Light]] is the main religion within the Azak Empire.
