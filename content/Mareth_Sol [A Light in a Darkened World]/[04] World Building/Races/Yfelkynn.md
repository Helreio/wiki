The Yfelkynn, or Evil Kin within the Azak Empire, are an ancient race of giants, their massive forms shaped by the unforgiving wilderness they call home. Though feared by many, they are neither mindless nor cruel—they are survivors, bound by tradition and deep traditions. The most commonly seen are slaves serving the Azak nobility.
  
==**Duskwalker**== (+1 Agi, +1 to any stat)
	**Appearance:** Lanky but powerful, their ashen-gray skin blends with the dusk. Their piercing, luminous eyes shift between hues of violet and green, and their long, matted hair often carries the scent of earth and rain.
	**Age:** They mature by 30 years and can live up to 500 years, their wisdom deepening with the centuries.
	**Size:** Towering at 250 - 390 cm tall, their lean forms are deceptively swift. Your size is Large.
	**Shadowmeld.** If you stand perfectly still for 1 round in dim light or darkness, you become nearly indistinguishable from your surroundings, giving creatures Disadvantage on Intuition checks to notice you.
	**Gloom Stalker.** When attacking a creature that hasn’t detected you, you can roll an extra weapon damage die on your first hit.


==**Grimstone**== (+1 Str, +1 to any stat)
	**Appearance:** Heavyset and broad, their dark, rock-like skin is cracked and rough, resembling the stones of the earth itself. Their glowing ember-like eyes smolder with a quiet intensity.
	**Age:** They reach maturity by 40 years and live up to 600 years, their strength growing with age.
	**Size:** Massive and imposing, they stand at 240 - 360 cm tall with their sheer bulk. Your size is Large.
	**Stoneborn.** You gain Advantage on Fortitude Saving Throws against being Stunned or Incapacitated.
	**Mountain’s Grip.** Your hands can crush steel, and no weapon is too heavy for you. You can wield two-handed weapons in one hand, but you cannot dual wield.

Slaves for the [[Azak Empire]]'s nobility.
