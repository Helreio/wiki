Creatures sired by wild gods ~4000 years ago.

Are a race of humanoids living in mostly nomad tribes throughout the world. Most of their religious practices revolve around using the land to the fullest and keeping the balance and the spirit of their Sire. They are recognised by features they inherit from their Sire.
 

==**Cernos, the White Stag**== (+1 to any stat) 

They are born to wander, their bodies built for relentless movement across endless terrain. Whether traversing dense forests or open plains, they move with an unnatural stamina, rarely tiring and always pressing forward.
	**Appearance:** Slender, small ornate antlers, some have hoofed feet, glossy eyes, fair skin.  
	**Age:** They reach adulthood in their early thirties and live around 150 years.  
	**Size:** They have a very slender build, from 180 - 240 centimeters tall. Your size is Medium.
	**Stag’s Reflexes.** When an enemy you can see targets you with an attack, you can use your Reaction to move 2 meters without provoking opportunity attacks.
	**Wanderer’s Instinct.** You always know which direction is north, and you can recall terrain and settlements you have seen within the last week with accuracy.
  

==**Perrun, the Storm Eagle**== (+1 to any stat) 

Their bodies seem charged with the sky’s fury, and the air around them often carries the scent of rain. Perrun are known to move with the unpredictability of a storm, striking as swiftly as a lightning bolt.
	**Appearance:** Beaked nose, rounded ears, clawed feet, a small hunch.
	**Age:** They reach adulthood in their early teens and live around 60 years.
	**Size:** They have a slender build, from 140 - 180 centimeters tall. Your size is Medium.
	**Sky Touched.** When you fall, you can slow your descent, taking no damage from a fall of 6 meters or less and treating any greater fall as half the normal distance for damage calculation.
	**Wind’s Swiftness.** Your movement is increased by 2 meters.

 
**==Azhdaya the Iron Serpent==** (+1 to any stat)  

Azhdaya are unyielding and imposing, their presence like that of a coiled serpent, ever-watchful. Their unyielding spirit makes them natural leaders, warriors, and survivors, thriving in the face of adversity.
	**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered, scaled patches on skin, slit eyes, forked tongue, ridged brow, thick limbs.  
	**Age:** They reach adulthood in their early teens and live up to 200 years.  
	**Size:** They have a sturdy and powerful build, standing between 170 - 210 centimeters tall. Your size is Medium.
	**Auburn Scales.** Your EC is increased by +1.
	**Venomous Fangs**(1/turn). Your Bite attack deals 1d6 Piercing damage + 1d4 Poison damage + Strength or Agility Modifier. The attack is done with the highest Mastery your character has with weapons.  

  
==**Sirin the Enchantress**== (+1 to any stat) 

A mystical being of song and enchantment, Sirin’s voice is said to bring both bliss and sorrow, weaving magic into melody. Her presence inspires those around her, uplifting spirits or lulling foes into dreamlike trances.
	**Appearance:** Elegant with soft feathers covering parts of their arms, neck, and shoulders. Golden, pupil-less eyes with an otherworldly beauty.
	**Age:** They reach adulthood in their late teens and live up to 90 years, their voices only growing more potent with age.
	**Size:** They are gracefully built, standing between 160 - 190 cm tall. Your size is Medium.
	**Silver-Tongued.** You can perfectly mimic voices and sounds you have heard for at least one minute. Creatures must make an Insight check (DC = 10 + your Charisma Modifier) to determine if the voice/sounds are fake.
	**Lullaby of Woe**(1/SR). Your haunting song weaves sorrow into the minds of those who hear it, enthralling them. For two Action Points, you sing a mournful lullaby. Creatures of your choice within 6 meters that can hear you must make a Willpower Saving Throw DC 10 + your Charisma Modifier or be Enthralled for 1 minute. A creature that succeeds on the saving throw is immune to this trait for 24 hours.

Enthralled creatures are Incapacitated (See Condition) and their movement speed is 0. The effect ends if the creature takes any damage or if someone else shakes the creature out of its stupor.

Incapacitated. A incapacitated creature has only one Action Point per round, has no reactions, movement is halved (rounded up) and can speak only falteringly.

The creature has disadvantage on Reflex & Fortitude Saving Throws.
