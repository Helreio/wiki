Only a few are known, gleaned from the testimonies of seers and cultist confessions.

**Lug’krum, The Conqueror** \- An entity, as described by cultists of the Mourning Sun, with a humanoid body and black horse head adorned with a white star on its forehead. An elder koshmera towering over 50 meters tall.

**Ma’el, The Unborn** \- He is depicted in a mural as a fetus-like embryo within an eightfold crystal, whose influence spreads among the faithless corrupting their soul. 

**Hy’ril of Ráima, The Deceitful Queen** \- Expeditions through [[The Veiled Weft]] have encountered her prey, or better put her playthings. They had broken minds, the worst have been grafted together with oversized insect parts.

**Lá'vear, The Faceless** \- Only a mention of his name was found, etched into the narthex's door in an abandoned village.  

**Gatekeeper Hiron/Hiros -** _Guardian of the Obsidian Gate, he inhabits two malformed bodies sharing one soul, eternally guarding the upper layers of the abyss” -_ Testimony extracted during a confession under Inquisitor [[Sayre Spryt]].
