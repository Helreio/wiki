**In the Beginning**

There was an entity beyond time and motion, named **IO, the Allfather.**

Eternal and unchanging, he beheld the weight of his own existence and found it incomplete. For what is endless without purpose? What is eternal without perfection?

Thus, he cast himself into fragments, to create a universe in which he would witness all that is and shall be. Every star a thought, every life a heartbeat of the Allfather’s dream.



### The Order of Creation

**“First** he created _Light_ from His radiant golden eye to balance the _Darkness_ found in His other. 

From His blood _The Endless Sea_ took form as a binder to hold everything together.

**Second** was _The High Heavens_, where His mind will reside, bringing order. After He cast His heart at the bottom of the Sea, where The Abyss took form. Emotion and chaos dwelling within.

**Third,** came time for _The Golden Wheel_, at the centre point between _the Heavens_ and _the Abyss._ Around the wheel the Physical World coalesced.

**Fourth,** from parts of Himself, He moulded the _elemental forces_ within The Endless Sea. Giving domains to the Lords of Flame, Tide, Sky and Stone.

**Fifth** is when from His last remains, celestial bodies were born, then fell into place, around _the Golden Wheel_, governing the flow of time and magic within the universe. 

**Sixth** was time for the creatures to take shape by shattering Himself and spreading the fragments.

On the **Seventh** His last breath spun _The Golden Wheel,_ as the **Allfather** dreams within His creation.” 

*— passage from The Seventh Light holy book.*
