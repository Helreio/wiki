Yfelkynn traditions are passed down orally, these four represent the main deities.

**Flamelord Artan** \- Born from the first ember that set the heavens alight, Artan is the eternal flame that neither time nor tide can extinguish. His breath, a tempest of golden flame that forged the stars.

**Tidelord Nén** \- Serene yet unfathomable, Nén flows between tranquility and tempest. Her form is ever-changing in quenching the flames.

**Stonelord Már** \- A being of stone, roots, and unshakable will, Már is the foundation upon which all things rest. His presence is ancient and immovable, the slow but inevitable force of mountains rising and civilizations crumbling. 

**Skylord Hwé** - Swift and elusive, Hwé is the whispering gale within the raging storm, felt but unseen.
